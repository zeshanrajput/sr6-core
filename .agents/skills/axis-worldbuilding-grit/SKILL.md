# Worldbuilding & Dystopian Grit Evaluation Skill (`axis-worldbuilding-grit`)

Use this skill to audit narrative drafts for Shadowrun 6e dystopian verisimilitude, sensory worldbuilding grit, corporate omnipresence, AR/Matrix immersion, and cyberware/magic texture without info-dumping.

---

## Evaluation Workflow

1. **Worldbuilding Audit Criteria**:
   - **Corporate Omnipresence**: Are megacorporations, branded products, corporate security grids, and commercial AR spam actively present in the background?
   - **AR & Matrix Layering**: Is the digital layer (AR spam, grid pop-ups, commlink pings, mesh node noise) integrated into physical environments?
   - **Cyberware & Spell Texture**: Are mechanical implant activations (wired reflex hum, smartlink reticle lock, cyberlimb hydraulic hiss) and magical spellcasting described in vivid sensory terms rather than abstract game labels?
   - **Dystopian Texture & Street Grit**: Does the environment feel authentic to 2080s Shadowrun (acid rain, synth-food smells, neon reflections in oil slicks, sprawl crowding, class divides)?
   - **Zero Info-Dumping**: Is all worldbuilding woven organically into sensory observation and tactical necessity?

2. **Scoring & Redline Output**:
   - **Worldbuilding & Grit Score**: Rate from **1 to 10** (Pass threshold: **8.0+**).
   - **Grit Enhancements**: Specific suggestions to add corporate branding, AR noise, or sensory mechanical depth.
