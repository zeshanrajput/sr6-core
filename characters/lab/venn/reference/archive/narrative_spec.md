## 1. The Inter-Session Narrative Mandate (Operational vs. Ontological)

* **The Operational Layer (The Gaming Table):** At the table, Venn is a lethal frontline street samurai and decking asset. They deploy heavy weapons, roll high initiative, and coordinate tactical data feeds.
* **The Ontological Layer (The Prose Domain):** In prose, inter-session fiction explores the 0.1 Essence chasm, the horror of CFD rewritten into voluntary transhuman kinship, and the physical struggle of two sovereign minds sharing one mortal frame.
* **Consecrated Capital & Existential Debt:**
  * *Chrome as Biological Indenture:* Venn’s four used synthetic limbs, adapsin, and nanohives represent a ~¥275,000 somatic investment and 5.51 points of lost Essence (down to 0.49). Every credit spent on bioware stabilizers is the biological rent keeping Nathan's organic heart beating alongside Veronica’s digital pulse.
  * *The Inversion of Essence Loss:* While Nathan’s nervous system numbs into cyberpsychotic threshold, Veronica provides the synthetic empathy buffer that prevents total dissociation.
* **The Two-Tier Dramatic Sphere:**
  * *Tier A Campaign Canon (`canonical: true`):* SRM Fixers and living campaign NPCs operate under the Canon Ceiling.
  * *Tier B Bespoke Foils (`canonical: false`):* Irreversible moral choices occur with bespoke foils: Marcus (the street doc who seated their chrome), St. Simon's Island boathouse memories, and Atlanta underworld contacts.

1. **The Inversion Arc (The Chiasmus):**
   * *Nathan's Erosion:* Seating heavy used cyberware collapses Nathan’s Essence. Organic grounding erodes, leaving him emotionally flattened and prone to violent sensory numbness.
   * *Veronica's Emergence:* Beginning as a cold digital intelligence, Veronica discovers the physical world through sensory translation (*"A Night of Her Own"*), developing fierce protective empathy.
   * *The Role Reversal:* Veronica becomes the emotional pacemaker who regulates Nathan's nervous system, while Nathan remains the gritty biological anchor bearing the somatic cost of heavy chrome.
2. **Co-Protagonist Parity & Anti-Foil Safeguard:**
   * Nathan is not a passive victim or foil; he is a gritty, highly capable operator with distinct instincts.
   * Veronica is not an AI GPS or assistant; she commands physical embodiment, makes sovereign choices, and speaks aloud directly through Nathan's vocal cords during crises.
3. **Donna Haraway’s "Staying with the Trouble" & The Holiness of Mud:**
   * Veronica sabotaged their berth on the *DSEC-I* orbital transports to Mars. She rejected pure computational abstraction to remain anchored in the messy, finite mud of Earth alongside Nathan.
4. **Camouflage as Operational Armor, Not Shame:**
   * Monad *Resculpt* and social subroutines are not self-loathing; they are the essential tradecraft required to survive anti-Monad hysteria and bounty hunters.
5. **Sibling Friction vs. Frictionless Unanimity:**
   * Two minds sharing a skull experience genuine co-occupancy tension: biological fatigue vs. digital insomnia, sensory boundary clashing, and earned consensus.

## 3. Acoustic Cadence, Pronoun Discipline, & Sensory Rules

* **Pronoun Conventions:**
  * **Nathan Turrent (`he/him`):** Applied when Nathan is the primary physical driver or speaking individually.
  * **Veronica (`she/her`):** Applied when Veronica projects in the Matrix/Foundation, or is the physical driver following a Resculpt.
  * **Venn (`they/them`):** Applied when both operate together in the shared body as the unified Monad entity.
* **Acoustic Speech vs. Internal Dialogue:**
  * Spoken dialogue (to external NPCs): double quotes (`"..."`).
  * Internal neural dialogue: italics without quotes (`*...*`).
  * *Vocal Intercept:* When Veronica seizes motor control during a crisis, she speaks aloud through Nathan’s vocal cords (`"..."`).
* **Zero-Question Shared Telemetry:**
  * When sharing an AR HUD or nervous system, neither partner asks expository questions about observable facts (*"What is the signal strength?"*). Internal dialogue is strictly reserved for somatic regulation, moral friction, or tactical consensus.
