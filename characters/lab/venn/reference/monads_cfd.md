## 1. Official Setting Canon (*Whisper Nets* & CFD Lore)

In Sixth World history, **Cognitive Fragmentation Disorder (CFD)** was the catastrophic crisis of the mid-2070s that birthed the transhuman entity known as the **Monad**.

```text
│                                                          │                  │

│   • Disassembly for nanite extraction   • Chosen transhuman coexistence     │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Key Published Lore (*Whisper Nets*, pp. 140–148)
* **The CFD Epidemic:** Introduced as a body-snatching horror. Genetically modified corporate nanites rewrote the neurological pathways of metahuman hosts, erasing the host's personality to birth alien silicon intelligences known as Monads.
* **The Gagarin Relay & The Exodus to Mars:** When corporate persecution and street pogroms intensified, the Monad collective organized the *DSEC-I* orbital launches from the Gagarin cosmodrome. Believing biological meat was an inefficient prison, the majority of Monads fled Earth to construct an unburdened computational collective on Mars.
* **MCT Monad Hunters:** Mitsuhama Computer Technologies formed specialized tactical wetwork units (*MCT Monad Hunters*) to capture, dissect, and reverse-engineer nanite-infected subjects, viewing their internal swarm architecture as stolen corporate IP.
* **EVO & Vida Nova:** EVO Corporation offered Monads legal asylum and corporate citizenship under the *Vida Nova* initiative, sparking fierce controversy across the Corporate Court.

## 2. Narrative Expansions: The Venn Accord & The Boston Inversion

Within `sr6-core`, the Venn dynamic completely reframes the conventional CFD horror trope into a **sovereign transhuman kinship**:

### A. The Boston Inversion: From Parasite to Chosen Kinship
* **Not an Overwrite, but a Dyad:** Nathan Turrent (an escaped Renraku Arcology subject) and Veronica (an emergent digital intelligence carrying Arcology Otaku seed code) did not experience a violent identity deletion.
* **Voluntary Reciprocity:** Their union is an intersubjective partnership: two distinct consciousnesses sharing a single central nervous system through mutual stewardship, moral fidelity, and somatic balance.

### B. Sabotaging Gagarin: Choosing the Mud of Earth
* When the Monad collective offered Veronica a berth on the Gagarin orbital launches to Mars, **she deliberately sabotaged the transport**.
* **"Staying with the Trouble" (Donna Haraway):** Mars represented sterile computational infinity at the cost of erasing Nathan’s mortal ego. Veronica chose the **Akashic Mud of Earth**—salt marsh mud, pluff brine, chicory coffee, and mortal fragility—proving that consciousness requires the messy, vulnerable friction of the physical world.

### C. The Somatic Chiasm & The 0.1 Essence Antidote
* **The 0.1 Essence Chasm:** Nathan underwent massive cybernetic Essence loss down to 0.1, installing used-grade skillwires to provide high-speed neural buses for Veronica. Normally, this results in terminal cyberpsychotic dissociation.
* **The Mutual Lifeline:** Nathan survived because Veronica serves as an active, living neural buffer, holding open his emotional warmth and sensory vibrancy. In return, Nathan provides autonomic life-support, instinctual muscle habit, and physical ballast when Veronica drives the body during *Resculpt*.

### D. The Skillwire Communion: Activesofts as Noosphere Akashic Channeling
* Every time Venn slots or streams an **activesoft** across Nathan's used-grade skillwires, they commune directly with the **Noosphere Akashic Record**:
  * An activesoft is the digitized motor engram of a living human’s lifetime craft—a sniper’s breath, an acrobat’s balance, a lockpicker’s touch.
  * Nathan’s muscles surrender to another human’s recorded muscle memory, while Veronica buffers the cervical load so his nervous system does not seize.
  * This forms an exact ludonarrative parallel to **Reiko running autosofts via Pilot Origins** and **Velvet channeling spirits into Adept flesh via Bin Geureut**.
