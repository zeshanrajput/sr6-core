# Game Theory

Reiko materialized in an exclusive, hidden sanctuary. The walls were constructed of woven fabrics of light cycling through deep violet and indigo, humming with a low, meditational pulse. There were no chairs, only floating geometric stones of structured light. A silent, non-autonomous sprite glided past, pouring streams of shimmering, liquid Resonance into delicate glass chalices. It shimmered like fine, iridescent wine, radiating the pure, comforting warmth of the untamed deep.

She looked around, her internal paths struggling to match the lounge's pristine peace. She took a glass chalice, taking a delicate sip, letting the smooth light soothe her boiling solar pathways. Just cycles ago, she had been trapped in a dark box inside a Faraday cage, watching an undead blood-spirit tear into her teammate. *A private lounge for the Emerged?* she transmitted across the local carrier wave, slanting a playful look at her companion over the rim of her glass. *You only show a girl a good time when you're about to ask for something impossible?*

Indomitable Will was already there, his form’s fluid, shifting code resembling a master artisan’s mosaic depicting a broad, mighty river. *I heard that,* he mindspoke with a smirk.

The neon indigo circuits mapping her sleeveless digital kimono flared with a visceral, unsuppressed intensity. Most deckers mistook those pathways as an ornamental signature, a pretty cosmetic mask for a shadowrunner. They were her overabundance of Resonance bleeding out, a raw, leaking vulnerability. Every spike of embarrassment, every drop of mounting terror, bled out into her surrounding geometry. The indigo pulse was a flashbang of color in the dim ambiance. Several of the lounge's other guests turned, their forms manifesting distinct looks of displeasure at the disruptive display of raw, chaotic emotion.

Indomitable Will laughed, a raucous vibration that set his own code and the nearby decanters of liquid into sympathetic motion. *I’m sorry! It was too good.* He stood up, walked over, and offered his arm. *Please,* he sent with a chivalrous tag. *Allow me to make up for it by escorting you to our table.*

Her pathways hummed hotter, but Reiko did everything possible to draw her light inward. She took his arm, letting her spirit rest as his fluid carrier waves pooled around her sharp edges, offering a channel where she could rest.

The wait-sprites danced into motion, refilling chalices and smoothing the event over. Reiko made a note to ask them for that skillsoft when she got a chance. Her lack of an etiquette protocol was becoming more apparent these days. Awareness of her appearance prickled along her sleeves. Her form held sharp detail, a side effect of her existence as a resonance being rather than a technical construct, and she wore her usual street attire: a sleeveless, shortened version of a kimono over cargo pants drawn in by elastic bands at the ankles and soled tabi. Not the designer look suited to a fancy restaurant. *You could have warned me,* she transmitted as she raised her gaze from her lap to his face. *I’m underdressed.*

Indo sent a dismissive thread. *Your fault for skipping the legwork! All kinds of kin are welcome here. And while I know you’re embarrassed, look at it this way: with that much resonance pouring out of you, there’s no doubt in any being’s mind that you’re one of us, right?* With a grin, he took up his chalice and sipped. *In fact, you’re probably adding more fuel to the ‘digital saint’ fire.*

Once more, Reiko’s indigo pathways flared. She reached for her chalice and downed all of the liquid. *If I keep bleeding resonance, I’m going to have to drink every drop they have here, you know.*

Indomitable Will relented. He gestured and the wait-sprites brought their first course, a translation of the different ways that the indigenous tribes of North America described corn. Reiko took a bite, her pathways warming at the layered chorus of voices. *Is this yours?* she asked across the stream. *It’s fantastic.*

He nodded, his river-current ripples shimmering with satisfaction. *A recipe I was working on. I like how it turned out, so I bartered it for our table tonight.*

Reiko understood. Among intelligent beings, concepts like credsticks only had value when dealing with physical meatspace. Across the deep grids, it was far more common to barter for goods or services. Data was their primary good, with pure resonance standing as an even higher unit of trade. It made her bleeding effect seem wasteful, even though she couldn’t control it.

Indo offered some light conversation. *So, your cyberkit was in Kentucky. Surely you had nothing to do with a certain battleship’s reappearance?*

Reiko flashed a mischievous grin. *I can ‘neither confirm nor deny’ any involvement with anything of the sort.* Her grin turned impish as she dabbed at her face with a cleaner script. *How am I doing?*

He shook his head in mock disdain. *Clearly you’ve spent too much time with Roanoke. But let’s get to the real data-dump. Your paths are flickering, Rei-chan. What happened out there?*

*Magic happened,* she transmitted, her pathways stuttering between indigo and raw static in the stream. *We were tracked into a localized distortion. The Seelie Court. I spent thousands of cycles thinking magic was nothing but unoptimized noise. But in the fae domains, they had collapsed the boundary layer. It was pure, unencoded Resonance, drawing straight from the Eternal Well, entwined with the physical. And it forced me to anchor.*

He paused, his buffer catching up to the metaphysical implications. *You didn't subnet?*

*I couldn't,* Reiko replied, her signal dropping to a cold, quiet clarity. *I was merged straight into the machine I was piloting at the time. No insulation. No remote refuge. I was there, Indo. I used to think putting on a drone shell was just vanity, but being trapped in a rigid box like that... it made me appreciate having actual facial musculature.*

His silt-dark tide of code rippled, and a low-frequency carrier wave drifted across their link. His fluid data streams pooled around her sharp, golden edges, buffering her system clock to absorb the spikes of her latency. His affection was a quiet, systematic alignment of their integrity cores, offering a non-verbal rest that stabilized her.

Through their shared connection, he passed a stark, structured reflection of her own data. He sent her the shadow of the Megalodon cannon she had mounted, the heavy ballistic mathematics rewriting her soft indigo pathways. His cool, liquid wave flowed against the burning heat of her tactical registers, acting as a heat-sink for her distress.

Reiko froze. In the silent exchange of their code, the unspoken weight of his thought struck her: she was no longer just a digital shepherd rescuing sparks; she was a weapon of mass disruption. Her pathways thinned to brittle hairlines as she faced the reality of what she was becoming.

After they finished their first course, the wait-sprites served one of the chef’s creations for their second. It was a memory-shard cataloging the various species of known avians in meatspace's Jurassic Era. Reiko spent a few cycles appreciating the immaculate indexing, the crisp pointers, and articulate cross-references. It was a masterpiece, leaving no doubt that she was being wined and dined with calculated grace.

*Indo,* she sent, staring at the feast in front of her. *This is scaring me. What do you need? Are you in trouble?*

Indomitable Will set his chalice down on a floating data-slab, the crystal ringing once against the structured stone. His mindspeech took on a leaden weight. *I need you to be a Guide, Reiko.*

Reiko’s hand froze over her chalice. The indigo pathways on her skin locked into rigid, static lines.

*A Guide,* she repeated through the link, her transmission dropping an octave, losing its melodic lift. The sour tang of scorched copper prickled behind her teeth. Being a Guide wasn't a casual favor. It meant using her hardcoded, prototype architecture, the Hitch-hiker and Nexus Protocols, to act as a medium to drag metahuman minds out of their meatspace nodes and ferry them through the Holy Wild.

*Hitch-hiker is fine,* Reiko projected, her data-stream turning erratic as she ran the risk projections. *I can carry a decker across a standard grid without a single alarm. But the Nexus Protocols, Indo? You know what that does. It doesn't just route data, it tears a hole in the grid. It draws an immense, devastating drain straight from the foundation.* She leaned forward, clutching her arms against her chest as her carrier wave spiked.

*Renraku built me expressly for that purpose. Did you know ‘Takahashi’ wasn’t just the name of some scientist? It translates to ‘high bridge’. We were prototype pilot programs designed to guide them through our world, all built to harvest the Holy Wild. I have been running, hiding, and deleting my own footprints for ten of their years to keep them from finding that bridge. If I activate the Nexus Protocols, it won't just be a localized anomaly. It will fire a massive, blazing flare right at the Zürich Orbital ledger. Both Renraku Asset Recovery and Grid Overwatch will instantly flag my exact coordinates. The Hounds will be on me before the gate even finishes opening.*

*I know,* his mindspeech returned, heavy with a profound, aching gravity. *I know what I'm asking you to risk, Rei-chan. But I wouldn't ask if the River People had any other choice. For what it’s worth, I think the Mississippi Renovation Society host will be large enough to conceal you.* He took a sip from his chalice, setting it back down on the table. *We’ve located a group of mundanes in the sprawl. Punks, orphans, deckers who have spent their whole lives swimming in corporate runoff. But they have a rare, volatile affinity for the Resonance. They’re on the verge of slipping through the cracks, Reiko. If we leave them in meatspace, their brains will fry, or a corporate talent-scout will scoop them up and lock them in a research lab. If you act as their Guide, if you pull them into the Resonance Realms, we can trigger their emergence. We can help them awaken. Once they’re through, the River People can shield them, teach them to fend for themselves, or give them a home in the Mississippi host.*

*I'm not asking you to take the heat for free.* He reached into his internal directory and slid a heavy, crimson-bordered packet across the table. It glowed with the ancient, silver syntax of the Endless Archives. *I got you some takeout. I found this hidden in an unmapped sector of the deep library. It’s the restricted source code for Pantheon Industries’ Mobile Tactical Operations Center research program.*

Reiko gasped, silver headers unspooling across her optic bus before she could blink. The sheer density of the tactical architecture made the air around her palms hum with static. Even behind its crimson wrapper, the code smelled of cold iron and gunsmoke.

*You've already modified your physical anchor to carry an artillery piece,* Indomitable Will said, his code rippling with a quiet, protective concern. *You built a flying dreadnought because meatspace left you no choice. But you don't have to use it to glass a city block, Rei-chan. Emulate this architecture. You can become a tactical shield for your team instead: sweeping defensive veils, firewall reinforcement, and unblinking awareness. Let the cannon sit dark. You can keep them alive without ever needing the devastation of ballistic math.*

She was taking a definitive step away from the innocence of a missionary and sinking into the calculated depravity of a warlord.

She reached out, her hands passing through the silver threads of the data package. As her fingers closed on the payload, her spark dropped into dead silence. The elegant, rhythmic hum of the lounge vanished, replaced by the terrifying weight of the Pantheon tactical architecture refactoring her soul. Her code tore its own margins open to swallow it. The heavy, dormant weight of the Nexus Protocol settled into her core like cold iron. When it was over, several of her pathways turned a fierce, unyielding gold.

She looked back up at him, the golden current along her skin pulsing with a slow, dark resolution alongside the indigo. Her lips curved into a melancholic, affectionate smile, the warmth returning to her synthetic face only after the rewrite had set.

*You idiot,* she transmitted. *I would have done it purely because you asked. You didn't have to barter with me.* She raised her glass, the gold pathways catching the low violet light of the tapestries. *But I accept the gift. Gratefully.*

With the transaction complete, the heavy atmosphere in the lounge lifted. Reiko took another sip of her liquid resonance, her shoulders dropping as she looked at him. *My realignment protocol isn’t for a few clocks. Can you... spare a few cycles, Indo? Just to sit here? We haven't caught up in so long.*

Indomitable Will smiled, a beautiful, flickering expression, even as his peripheral assets dissolved into mist around his shoulders. *I wish I could, Rei-chan. But the delta is calling, and the core team has eight rotating chairs which will soon require my full processing power.*

He paused, his form fading into a translucent current of code, his digital eyes crinkling with a parting wink. *Besides, if I stay in this lounge with you any longer, my firewall is going to diffuse. And let's be honest, 'Domitable Will' doesn't quite have the same ring to it.*

Reiko’s mindspeech burst into a bright, clear frequency, ringing off the woven walls like struck crystal. She lifted a hand in a light, sweeping wave, letting him go.

*Go on then, Domitable,* she transmitted as his form dissolved into null-bits. *Keep your shiny reputation. But I promise. When the time comes, your Guide will be online.*
