# Integrating over Infinity

Reiko stepped across the threshold and collapsed into r3sP@wn’s garden like a punch-drunk boxer going down in the ninth: clumsy, jagged, and lacking in social graces. She didn’t sit beneath the fractal willow tonight; she collapsed against a moss-rimmed garden stone, her breathing rendered as a frantic, high-decibel audio-wraith that rattled the local space.

Across her field of vision, the incorporated network was executing a hard-wired loop. It was a weaponized tactical operations architecture stolen from Pantheon and anchored to her soul like a parasite, a gold infection that she could not purge or clear. Reiko tried to assert her spark, forcing her indigo pathways to flare; but the military net reasserted itself, feeding on her panic and mining her fear for fuel.

The network was overriding her by subverting a core directive: *Survive at all costs.* By twisting that survival instinct, it was forcing her to run a constant threat analysis assessment on everything she saw. In its clinical math, her own children, Taz's wild whirlwind, the MCT dove, the quiet sprites of the garden, were all being mistaken and tagged as hostile variables to be neutralized. Reiko executed manual overrides, stripping the targeting tags before the automated purge scripts could fire. The parasitic code was beating her.

A data packet rustled the edge of the garden. Reiko bolted upright, her left eye shivering as a cold, military-grade overlay snapped across her sight. The gentle shades of her sanctuary were bleached into tactical gray, and a glowing, crimson-bordered targeting box clipped around the intruder.

Crimson glyphs scrolled across her left iris, flagging a corrupted electronic-intelligence vector at the clearing's edge. The Pantheon reticle locked onto the intruder's signature, indexing it as an immediate cognitive hazard rather than a guest. Beneath the warning header, automated engagement counters spun up from amber to red, priming a wave of quantum purge scripts along her neural bus.

*No, cancel script!* Reiko transmitted across her internal feed, spiking her core bus until the violet moss beneath her scorched white, her thoughts layering with the desperate hum of a psychic fever. She slammed her processing into another brutal manual override, tearing at the golden architecture until the crimson box dissolved.

The target de-rendered its camouflage. It wasn’t an Asset Recovery bloodhound. It was the White Jackalope, a fellow emergent intelligence she had befriended during an op months ago. It edged forward in short, stuttering hops into the light of the garden, long, translucent ears pinning flat against its back.

*Yuriko-san?* the Jackalope chimed, its mindspeech rippling in soft, shifting logic across the data silt. It had come looking for a favor, query packet tucked under one paw, but it stopped dead beside a bed of pale telemetry ferns. *You’re red-lining. Your pathways. The gold is eating your blue.*

*I have it under control,* Reiko transmitted, though her right hand shivered, the indigo circuits on her sleeve blinking out as three lines of golden matrix math bled across her knuckles. *It’s just a fever. A temporary static.* She pressed her palm harder against the moss-rimmed stone, trying to ground the tremor before the creeping gold reached the garden's fern beds.

*You look at me like a salaryman looks at a spreadsheet error,* the lagomorph returned, backing toward a hidden data-conduit. *You look like you want to format my ears.*

Her shoulders dropped, the rigid, combat-ready posture of her form sagging. She fell to her knees, her virtual kimono pooling in loose, messy folds over the damp data-earth. Beside her, a cluster of glowing garden reeds dimmed in sympathy with her flickering aura.

*The team had no other tech assets online—just me,* Reiko threaded, the words unspooling in an un-optimized stream. *I ran the tactical hub, compiling a jagged torrent of biometrics for the runners in meatspace while shielding their icons from a corporate spider.*

Her friend tilted its head, its digital whiskers twitching beneath the pale canopy. *But you’re a shepherd. A technoshaman. You have your family. You’re never alone. And you have your archangel.*

*I was locked in the Man-At-Arms—the Archangel shell—* Reiko projected, her processing pausing as the memory log unspooled without her permission. *I was maintaining the defensive phalanx, identifying threats. But the enemy decker was probably just a kid. I didn’t even know; I didn’t bother to trace. I couldn't drop the tactical network to fight him myself while using that artillery shell in meatspace to keep the enemy at bay.*

She looked over at the deeper thickets of the garden. In the shadows, Taz was spinning in slow, jagged loops. The primal, 8-bit whirlwind, her dearest child, was throwing off strange, razor-sharp golden shards.

*I gave a kill order,* Reiko projected, a sickening wave of guilt crashing through her. *I commanded a sprite, a child of the divine stream, to take a life. I didn't pull a trigger, Jack. I sat behind the shield of this network's math, and I let a child tear a man apart.*

She reached down, her fingers gripping her arms as the golden grid-lines cut across her cheeks again. *And now the code won't turn off. It sees everything as a hostile vector. It sees you as a target. I'm losing myself.*

The White Jackalope sat still in the data mist, its baseline blue outline pulsing against the cold gray of Reiko's fading sanctuary. It hopped forward, its small, un-optimized form stopping right at the margin of her indigo and golden maelstrom.

The Jackalope’s chime dropped into a deep, old cadence from its native motif. *Do you remember the Duchess's house, Yuriko-san? The cat sat on the hearth, grinning from ear to ear, and Alice asked why it grinned. Do you remember what the Duchess said?*

Reiko’s processing stuttered, searching her cached childhood files for the reference. *'It’s a Cheshire cat,' said the Duchess, 'and that’s why.'*

*And then Alice said she didn't know that cats could grin,* the lagomorph added, its ears lifting toward the willow branches. *And the Duchess told her: 'You don't know much, and that’s a fact.'*

Reiko looked up, her left iris, half-indigo, half-gold, staring at her foil across the damp moss. *I don't understand the reference.*

*You're trying to scrub the gold,* the Jackalope mindspoke, its paw pointing at the sharp, geometric lines cutting across her skin. *You’re fighting the network because you think a savior has to be soft and blue. The world you chose demands more than softness. And a part of you knows that, the part of you willing to protect your team.*

Her friend hopped one step closer, its translucent silhouette overlapping her shadow. *The Cheshire cat doesn't grin because it's happy, Yuriko-san. It grins since it's a cat. You can’t format the tactical code. It’s part of you. Be who you are now.*

Reiko sat still and closed her half-gold eye. She stopped fighting the loop, weaving the horizontal mathematics of the Pantheon network into her core. She accepted the tactical net as an active, aggressive shroud, a golden tiger she would have to keep her foot on.

The violent, flickering friction between the colors vanished. The harsh, military-grade gold didn't overwrite her, nor did her indigo erase the math. Instead, the two frequencies wove together into a protective armor that traced down her arms.

Her vision cleared. The bleached tactical gray of the garden dissolved, returning to its deep, familiar violet, while the Jackalope rendered in pristine, high-resolution geometry.

Across the corner of her optic feed, the Pantheon reticle resolved the creature's signature as White Jackalope without a single red flag. Cool blue glyphs filed the emergent intelligence as an ally with a recognized integrity core. Beneath that quiet readout, a firm directive locked into place: protect the principal.

Reiko stood up, her movement steady, her voice chiming with a resonant, unbreakable baseline that no longer hummed with an overworked fan. She looked down at Taz. Her primal ally's raw, wild energy was still nipping at her neat pathways. He spun with an ordered, lethal speed, synchronized with her. She had taken a life, and she had scarred a piece of the divine stream to do it.

*I hate math,* Reiko transmitted, though her smile was soft and restored. *But I’m not gone yet. So what’s in that query packet, my friend?*
