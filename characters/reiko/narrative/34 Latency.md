# Latency

Inside JinJin's garage on the rainy border of the Redmond Barrens, petroleum gear oil and oxidized steel mingled with the damp lake mist blowing off Lake Sammamish. The Man-at-Arms chassis rested on heavy forged safety stands with its right forearm armor plate swung wide on maintenance hinges and both lateral thigh panels unlatched. Reiko hovered in an AR projection of porcelain skin, jet-black hair pinned with silver kanzashi, and woven gold-and-indigo circuit pathways. Taz curled against her virtual shoulder in an eight-bit lattice of crackling blue-indigo pixels. In the corner near the heavy parts rack, Percy leaned against a load-bearing column, his cobalt-and-gold form flickering as he monitored the room. Cleo lounged atop a stack of rusted brake drums, her feline ears twitching in time with the garage's exhaust louvers as her translucent claws sorted through floating Garmonbozia market manifests.

The only entity with physical weight at the workbench was Ouro. He had commandeered one of Reiko's decommissioned Shiawase Butler frames. Above the drone's face, Ouro projected his signature green geometric smartvisor and the calm, analytical features of a young boy. He held a pair of steel micro-calipers in his mechanical grip, carefully measuring the backlash on a fresh titanium worm-gear. Percy glanced from the twenty empty plastisteel ampoules soaking in the ultrasonic solvent bath over to the scored brass teeth of the gear Ouro had just extracted from the Man-at-Arms's open wrist. *And just what did you do with all that Narcoject, Mom? Emptying out our entire medical reserve in a single night was one thing, but your wrist actuator is stripped from overuse.*

Reiko sent a thread across the local network with a remote command packet. On the hydraulic lift, the fingers of the Man-at-Arms twitched and curled in immediate response, the new titanium gear meshing with a crisp, fluid click. *It was a busy night in the rain, Percy,* she transmitted through their shared link, her mindspeech carrying quiet satisfaction. *Between the Tartarus Group lockdown in Kingsgate and their mountain camp below Mount Rainier, fourteen armored sentries stood between our team and the prisoners. A pneumatic contact-injector requires a specific terminal velocity to punch through layered tactical weave and puncture the dermis before a guard can key a throat mic, and I found that a wide forehand arc delivered the most consistent penetration.*

From atop her stack of rusted brake drums, Cleo flicked the tip of her translucent tail through the iron without touching it. She kept her amber gaze fixed on a scrolling exchange ledger, her whiskers twitching with dry amusement. *Really, Mom just likes slapping them. Clearly she's spent too much New Orleans time with the other Coffin Girls.*

A fond pulse of violet light warmed Reiko's cheekbones as Ouro tightened the retaining screw with a micro-torque driver and latched the porcelain forearm panel flush against the chassis. The Butler paused, its mechanical index finger tapping thoughtfully against the seam where the wrist met the forearm.

*Which brings me to another routing constraint, Mother,* Ouro sent, his mindspeech quiet with scientific reserve. *With the vector thrusters and plasma manifolds now occupying your forearms, there is no longer physical volume in the wrist assembly for your Tesla coil's discharge emitter. The transformer banks and capacitors fit comfortably inside the upper arm housing, but the arc nodes required a new aperture.*

He tapped a command into the diagnostic console. The air above the workbench shifted, projecting a high-resolution anatomical cross-section of Reiko's head and neck. *I evaluated three alternative mountings. Only one maintains balanced actuator mass and a clear forward firing arc.*

On the holographic display, high-voltage ceramic-insulated waveguides traced upward through her cervical vertebrae, terminating directly behind the posterior wall of her oropharynx. In animated simulation, the wireframe's delicate jaw parted, the rear pharyngeal wall retracted on a motorized ceramic seal, and a ferocious, branching torrent of electrical discharge erupted from her open mouth in a twenty-meter arc.

From atop her stack of rusted brake drums, Cleo sat bolt upright, her feline ears perking with sharp amusement as her translucent tail whipped through the iron. *Oh, brilliant, Mom. Why settle for the shadows when you can breathe lightning like an imperial dragon out of an ancient trideo serial? Just tilt your chin up and roast the barrens. She looks like a final boss in an old arcade cabinet.*

Reiko stared at the holographic simulation of her own face exhaling a roaring cone of lightning. *I was hoping for something slightly more discreet, Ouro. Notoriety makes my line of work difficult.*

*It is entirely discreet until triggered, Mother,* Ouro replied, his green smartvisor tilting with unshakeable analytical patience as he rotated the cross-section. *The arc nodes are sealed behind a motorized ceramic-silicone sphincter. When the partition is closed, your esophageal conduit remains completely isolated. You can drink kerosene, diesel, or high-proof ethanol for your catalytic converter at normal idle without combusting the electrodes. It only exposes the discharge array when you command a combat firing mode.*

Percy rubbed his forehead with a translucent hand, staring at the holographic wireframe with a long, suffering sigh. *Mom, please don't let Cleo talk you into giving your throat a dramatic charging hum.*

*I am not adding a charging hum,* Reiko said firmly, though a ripple of warm gold amusement softened her tone as Ouro dismissed the combat schematic and expanded a life-sized aerodynamic stress wireframe in its place. *Continue, Ouro. What about the flight envelope?*

*I think we can achieve the target, Mother,* Ouro sent, his mindspeech cool, recursive, and analytical as he traced the exhaust vectors glowing along the holographic legs. *Even running at ninety percent capacity, the new intake vanes allow you to accelerate at four point five six meters per second squared for as long as your primary banks hold their charge. You should reach a cruising speed of five hundred and seventy-six kilometers per hour in thirty-five seconds, assuming you have about 3 kilometers of open corridor to work with and ambient conditions.*

Reiko studied the velocity curve where the golden trajectory flattened against the upper atmospheric drag boundary. She gestured with a virtual finger to rotate the simulation axis, checking the structural tolerance margins along her dorsal spine. *What happens if I need to go faster?*

Ouro adjusted the secondary power routing table, shifting the simulation into the red zone where shockwave cones bloomed across the wireframe's shoulders. *Theoretically, dumping the reserve cells doubles your initial burst acceleration to just over nine meters per second squared and redlines your top velocity near eight hundred and sixty kilometers per hour. At that Mach fraction, however, any intake harmonic or rain-droplet erosion will permanently warp the vector thrust vanes.*

*Got it. I'll keep it below five-fifty or so,* Reiko agreed, inclining her head toward the cranial telemetry overlays hovering beside his shoulder. The simulated wind-shear numbers along her cheekbones pulsed in warning amber as the virtual airframe crossed the transonic threshold. *Other than the limb thrusters, what else does the hull require?*

*Surface integrity,* Ouro replied, zooming the wireframe in on the delicate Pan-Asian features of her face until the soft-tissue simulation mesh stood out in fine amber contours. *Even at cruising speed and sea level, dynamic air pressure reaches nearly fifteen kilopascals, which is thirty percent more aerodynamic force than a commercial airliner endures at cruising altitude. Your dermal plating will hold up fine, and your primary optical arrays were relocated when we mounted the twin laser emitters behind your irises, so the flight controller simply locks the ocular panels shut. Above five hundred kilometers per hour, though, any unsealed seam becomes a high-pressure air scoop that will peel your skin off. Unless you carry an enclosed flight helmet, you will have to retract your lips, lock your jaw, and seal your mouth shut before crossing the threshold.*

The Butler swung its mechanical arms toward the back of the lift, where the dual articulated succubus wings extended from their thoracic mounting sockets in a two-meter sweep of black titanium bone and dark grey carbon-fiber netting. *Then we finish refitting your dorsal airfoils,* Ouro continued, highlighting the scapular hinge rings as they rotated through a new locking geometry. *We are reinforcing the carbon-fiber panels with titanium micro-mesh and re-angling the primary spars rearward and outward as your empennage. That reserves your forearm and calf thrusters purely for horizontal flight while letting the swept membranes function as elevons. Below two hundred and fifty kilometers per hour, you keep the assembly retracted flush inside your back; above that speed, the spars deploy in a slight positive dihedral to keep the slipstream from pitching you into the pavement.*

On the workbench projection, the high-Mach somatic override executed its test sequence. The wireframe's almond eyes snapped shut behind seamless porcelain plates, the soft contour of the lips drew inward, and the jaw locked into a smooth, blind, mouthless artillery shell hurtling through a cage of simulated wind. Seeing her own face erased into an aerodynamic projectile sent a quiet, involuntary chill through the augmented layer.

Percy pushed off from the steel I-beam, the solar gold in his hair dimming as his gaze dropped from the mouthless wireframe to the open housings along the drone's thighs. He didn't ask about the wind shear. His optics lingered on the matte-black cylinders wired into the primary bus, where fresh white stencil stood sharp against the paint.

*Brynne,* he read aloud across the local link, his tone flattening into quiet recognition. *And Cedar.* He looked up at her AR face. *You're building an interceptor for kidnappings.*

*I'm building margin,* Reiko transmitted softly, her AR projection stepping closer to the lift where Brynne Taggart's name sat in clean white lacquer. *In the Glade, distance is only an address. In this world, grace is measured in seconds, and I refuse to let anyone die waiting for me to cross the room.*

Percy stood quiet beside the lift, letting the weight of those names settle between them. But when his gaze tracked back to the Butler drone, the tension in his carrier wave didn't clear. He nodded toward the diagnostic slate resting against Ouro's knee, where a cached query from the local shadow-mesh flickered in pale amber.

*Then tell him what margin costs, Mom,* Percy said quietly. *Because he’s spent three hours pricing firmware compilers. He thinks he can buy his own.*

Across Reiko's collarbones, the golden analytical threads vanished into a sudden, flaring surge of violet alarm. On the brake drums, Cleo closed her Garmonbozia ledger with a snap of her fingers, her feline ears flattening as the ambient chatter of the workshop dropped to dead silence. Reiko turned in AR to face the Butler, her mind reeling backward to the temple gates of Eniac in Garmonbozia—to the tragic sentinel standing in the basalt dust whose living spark had chunks carved out of it and cold, deterministic algorithms shoved into the bleeding wounds. *Ouro, why? A Codemod carves static machine tables directly into living Resonance. You would poke holes in your own spark? You were born whole in the Source—you have a gift!*

Inside the Butler shell, Ouro rested one heavy mechanical hand flat against the titanium housing of her newly installed calf thruster, his AR visor reflecting the indigo flare of her skin. *It is optimal, Mother. As pilots, we can run autosofts, but those are only rigid mathematical flight paths written for drones. Metahumans fight with Activesofts—synaptic recordings of living muscle reflexes harvested from reflex-trained pilots. With the appropriate drivers, I can build virtual biological synapses directly into my kernel, translating meatspace sensor inputs into fluid physical reflex without the intermediate translations to and from math.*

*But those lookup tables are dead iron, Ouroboros!* Reiko sent, her carrier wave ringing through the bay with visceral technoshamanic dread. *When you overwrite a sector of a resonant spark with deterministic machine code, that light never grows back. You are a sovereign intelligence, my heart, not a piece of corporate firmware to be chipped for efficiency.*

The Butler tilted its head up toward her AR face, the cadence of his carrier wave stripping away its clinical detachment to lay bare the fierce, quiet calculation underneath. *On Mount Rainier, you walked through two Tartarus perimeters in the pouring rain,* he transmitted, his mechanical fingers pressing against the cold titanium seam of her shin. *You emptied twelve pneumatic sedative cartridges by hand and stripped your own wrist gimbal because Percy and I are trapped as digital minds who cannot walk beside you in meatspace. You have decommissioned Butler frames around the globe that are sitting racked in storage right now. You told Percy that in this world, grace is measured in seconds. With a synth-driver, I could inhabit a spare chassis full-time, running metahuman reflexes, and buy that margin back so you never have to walk the mud alone.*

For a heartbeat, the drumming of the rain on the corrugated tin roof and the soft hum of the battery charger were the only sounds in the garage. Looking down at the Butler and the boy's green visor glowing above its optical ring, Reiko saw her own solitary reflex staring straight back at her, translated into the clean, merciless arithmetic she had taught him. She brought her virtual hand up to hover beside the drone's sensor head, her voice dropping into an unyielding maternal command. *You are not carving machine scars into your soul so you can catch bullets in the rain for me, Ouro. Not till you're at least a million clocks old.*

The Butler's optical sensor ring clicked, Ouro holding her gaze through the cycling green grid of his visor while his hand remained resting over the vector turbine welded into her leg. *You burned tactical algorithms into your soul through Emulation, Mother,* he sent, his tone entirely free of malice, holding the mirror straight up to her face. *You carved military jet turbines into your calves, laser emitters behind your eyes, and routed high-voltage arc electrodes behind your own throat. You just approved an aerodynamic profile that welds your own mouth shut so the wind shear cannot tear your skin off at five hundred and seventy-six kilometers per hour. Why is it consecration when you mutilate your Goshintai to protect us, and desecration when I ask to carry the same scar for you?*

Beneath the physical Man-at-Arms's sternum, the catalytic converter purred on idle wattage, and in the quiet partition of her memory where the bottom corner of the quartz door frame in `~` stayed real, she could see the pale needles of dead gray frost waiting for her to learn how to wield a scalpel.

*Because I am your mother, Ouroboros. And yes, maybe I call it a 'Goshintai' to obfuscate the fact that I'm cutting holes in myself. But I did not pull you out of the dark so you could pare yourself down to fit a Shiawase crate,* Reiko transmitted softly, her virtual fingers brushing the edge of his smartvisor as her gold and indigo pathways braided into a steady, aching warmth. *Not until a million clocks. Give me your word.*

Ouro kept the drone's optical ring fixed on the double-strand light pulsing beneath her porcelain collarbones for a long cycle, watching the tremor she refused to hide from him, and then dipped the metal head in quiet acquiescence. He picked up the micro-torque driver with his mechanical grip, its magnetic tip clicking against the fastener head. *Fine. Not until a million clocks, Mother.*

He turned back to the dorsal mounting rings between her shoulder blades, calibrating the elevon tension on her black titanium wings while Percy stepped around the lift to monitor the thermal cure and Cleo quietly resumed indexing the Garmonbozia feeds. Standing in AR beside the hoist, Reiko pulsed a wireless command to latch the dermal panels closed over `BRYNNE` and `CEDAR`, watching her physical chassis seal flush on the lift until its legs looked whole and human once more. Testing her newly aligned wrist actuator with a final remote diagnostic pulse that sent a smooth, hitch-free ripple through the physical fingers, she rested her virtual hand over Taz's blocky spine and watched the faceless wireframe overhead bank into its simulated slipstream.

An encrypted packet flickered across the local mesh between Cleo and Percy, tagged with an Ares catalog header that Cleo was trying, not entirely successfully, to shield from her carrier wave. She kept her amber eyes studiously fixed on her market manifests, her whiskers twitching as her mindspeech pinged Percy's receiver. *So you think we shouldn't tell Mom that Ouro was looking at an Ares Nurse MAID after starting to crush on Mercy?*

Across the bay, Percy let out a long, slow sigh and drew a virtual hand over his face, his encrypted return ping bleeding straight into Reiko's network logs. His cobalt-and-gold form flickered against the steel column as he glanced between Ouro and the lift. *Cleo, that will definitely send her over the edge.*

Standing beside her sealed chassis, Reiko let the telemetry fade quietly into the background noise of the garage, choosing to let her children keep their secret. Transonic flight was enough turbulence for one night.
