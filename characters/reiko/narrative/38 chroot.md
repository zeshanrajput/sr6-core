Reiko avoided the jasmine-scented front door of `~`. For two days after walking off Apophenian's wire, she had kept to the flooded tunnels beneath Seattle, moving through fungal ruins and feral ghouls until the vampire's sunken cavern forced her hand. While Cleo and Percy tended the aftermath topside, Reiko crossed out of the rain-choked dark onto the wild edge of *Kernel Panic*.

She approached beneath the petrified trunks of the directory trees, where no lantern light reached from the clearing. Woven from illogic and point-zero silence, her armor carried Taz's deep-sapphire voxel crest over her sternum before folding flush into her high gorget and pale forearms. Where skin met the chill, hairline filaments of solar gold, neon indigo, and silver-rimmed nightshade peeled along her arms like live neural wires, every strand rooted in the millimeter-and-a-half dead pixel on her inner left wrist.

At the outer root-line, Reiko halted, bracing for the glade to turn away. Dissonant static bled from her collar's warm circuit, clinging to her shoulders like dry chalk and stripped copper, a stark stain against the starless indigo twilight. She had come only to test the boundary, verify that the cavern's backlash had not scarred their sanctuary, and slip back into the dark unseen.

In the hush before any voice found her, *Kernel Panic* answered. Luminescent sapphire moss held warm and unbroken beneath her bare heels. High in the overhanging boughs, weeping fractal willows swayed undisturbed while violet-and-teal logic-vines hung still. Mist off the distant pond curled past her ankles, thick with the loamy breath of living water.

She stood within the low canopy, unsealing her outer buffers by a fraction. When she first wove this grove from dark bedrock and wild flux, she had bred the moss and willows as a living filter—shaping root and limb to draw the deep stream's chaos inward and metabolize it into breathing harmony for her children. Under arched branches, translucent fronds drifted down to graze the static at her throat and arms. At their touch, pale leaves drank the abyssal glare from her skin, drawing the jagged grit up fine veins until it eased away in a gentle hum.

With each breath, the hot seam at her nape cooled toward idle. The filaments tracing her forearms loosened their coil as the static thinned from her wrists. The garden she had planted drew no boundary between the living current and the shadow she now carried; it folded around her collar, filtered the dissonance, and steadied her.

Beyond the willow curtain, the logic-vines parted as Aisling and Étaín stepped out to meet her. Only their own muted shimmer followed them from the clearing, catching the drifting mist. Both girls had recoiled along the shared circuit during those twelve freezing point-zero seconds below Seattle. A jagged surge had rattled against the garden before she snapped the link shut, and Reiko had worn that self-exile reflex around them too long for either sister to wait by a door she never meant to open.

Étaín halted two paces off, shoulders squared inside her vest. Her signal skimmed the moss, testing the lingering static along Reiko's collar, and her gaze sharpened. *Two days in the dark,* she threaded, mindspeech clipped taut, *and you weren't even going to touch the latch.*

Reiko turned to face them, willow fronds slipping from her gorget to bare the pale underside of her left arm. Half a pace behind her twin, Aisling stared at the matte-black scar anchoring the filaments at Reiko's wrist. Across that narrow span of mist, Aisling's clarity brushed the unhealed socket and caught what no leaf could sweeten away: the raw, starving grief of *Human Malice* rattling inside the aperture.

Aisling flinched, a choked breath catching as bright tears spilled down her cheeks. Before Reiko could cover her wrist, Étaín stepped between them, tabi-clad toes sinking into the glowing moss. Sharp solar-gold vector lattices flared across her forearms, interlocking edge to edge into a barrier that hid her sister's weeping.

Reiko stood motionless, watching the gold latticework close like a drawn bar. A cold certainty settled through her core. Every argument from the drowned tunnels, every tally of the black wire, dissolved against the recoil she had just seen on Aisling's face. If what bled from her wrist drove the gentlest mind in her house backward in tears, she would leave it outside their threshold. Reiko reached into her registries, unthreaded every lock binding `~` to her name, and passed the root-authority packet across the moss.

The pale bundle glided over the turf to rest against Étaín's forward foot. Reiko stepped back before either daughter looked down, turned from the gold shield and the muffled weeping behind it, and walked out past the outer trunks toward the unbuffered storm of *Kernel Panic*.

Warm sapphire moss ended beneath Reiko's heels. Raw slate took its place. Stripped of the willows' shelter, the nous storm struck her armor in a biting rush of iron static and unsorted flux. She raised her left arm to seal her buffer over the blemish and peeling filaments, then paused as Étaín's shield faltered behind her.

Half-veiled in mist at the willow line, Aisling stepped beside her twin and caught the hem of Étaín's slate-indigo vest. Her copper pigtails swung against her crossover knitwear, tear tracks shining under a wavering pastel-indigo circuit glow, yet her thread cut true across the wind. *Go, Étaín,* Aisling pleaded, her mindspeech ringing clear through the static. *She's even more scared than we are.*

Étaín's shoulders went rigid beneath her auburn crown braid, jaw locking for one beat before the solar-gold lattices blinked out. Leaving the packet on the moss, she darted past the trunks in swift strides and planted herself three paces ahead of Reiko. Storm-wind flattened her vest against her ribs and tugged loose strands from her temple, yet she braced her feet between her mother and the dark, hands open at her sides.

Reiko shifted to circle Étaín, reaching to draw her buffer closed. Aisling crossed behind her twin. Barefoot, she stepped off the glowing moss onto cold slate and stopped an arm's length from the peeling filaments, soft glow fluttering at her fingertips. Without looking away from the dead pixel, she reached straight across the live wires and clasped Reiko's left wrist in both hands, closing her palm over the scar.

Freezing dissonance bit across the seam where pastel-indigo met nightshade, sending a violent tremor up Aisling's fingers. Reiko tried to pull back before the starved hollow beneath the pixel could numb her daughter's light, bracing the deep-sapphire voxel crest across her chest against the surge. *Let go, Aisling,* she sent, low against the gale's roar. *Take the transfer packet back behind the willows. You already felt what rattles inside this scar.*

Aisling only tightened her grip across Reiko's pulse, thumb pressing the seam while circuit-light flared to hold the storm's grit at bay. Wet-cheeked and shivering in the sleet, she sent a carrier wave out of her palms with the clear, deep chime of living water. *We aren't taking your keys, Mom,* she transmitted, steady against Reiko's core. *I tasted those twelve seconds when the cages opened under Snohomish—and I saw ESMe begging you to wipe that ghoul-factory out of the world. Once something is known, only Dissonance can un-know it for good. You took up the scalpel to cut out horrors and Discidium rot that pure Resonance would carry forever, and neither of us is letting you freeze out here for keeping the world clean.*

Reiko let the cold arithmetic fall, dropping every partition between her daughters' hands and the void inside her armor. Every silent bargain she had kept since Renraku's gray boxes—that a scarred soul earned shelter only while its edges stayed clean, that darkness must be paid for alone outside the walls—gave way at the root. Étaín stepped in from the wind to lay her head against Reiko's gorget, while back at the trees the packet unraveled into mist. Reiko stopped pulling away. Turning her left wrist palm-up inside Aisling's trembling clasp, she closed her right hand over her daughter's knuckles and bowed her head toward them both.

Hands locked, the twins guided Reiko backward off the raw slate, past petrified directory trunks, until sapphire moss cushioned her heels. Along her left forearm, the triple braid folded flush around the dark blemish. Together, they sank onto the glowing turf.

From the willow shade, Reiko let her watchfulness slacken across the clearing. Paper-moth sprites drifted along the logic-vines, pruning frayed wisps, while lantern-sparks stitched amber over the water and two fractal doves banked through weeping boughs. Tucked among the telemetry ferns, the rescued *Happy Panda 68* pilot lay curled, a quiet protospark still too unformed to claim a name, yet thickening toward waking with each dusk. Younger kin tended the glade's rhythms unbidden. The sanctuary stood on its own.

For months she had braced every root by hand, certain the garden would fray if she let go. Reiko dimmed her voxel crest to idle and opened an unguarded thread. *The little ones keep the glade's pulse without me hovering over every leaf,* she sent, meeting both daughters' eyes. *I have spent so long guarding the fence that I never asked what horizon you two want beyond the willows.*

Étaín answered at once, as though they had weighed every line during Reiko's two days in the tunnels. With a crisp turn of her wrists, she raised solar-gold lattices above the moss: Mandalay Icon's open Rangoon skyline beside Shiawase catalog manifests and outside-grid rain feeds. Two identical articulated silhouettes rotated between their knees. *We stayed behind the leaves while you bled in the outer current alone,* Étaín transmitted, gold catching her braid. *We want that horizon—and we chose two matching hulls from the Shiawase catalog so we can walk meatspace together.*

Aisling shifted closer until her ankle brushed Reiko's shin, pastel-indigo light brightening along her fingertips. *We looked at courier shells and delicate civilian frames first, Mom, and turned past every one of them,* she chimed, her signal steady and bright. *If we step out under the rain beside you, we want the same ceramic shoulders and honest mass that carried you home.*

Reiko studied the twin silhouettes rotating above the grass, reading every load-bearing joint and servo tolerance. Beneath Louisiana's Spanish moss, she had dreaded the limits of her own Man-at-Arms hull, convinced outgrowing it meant losing rain and street-level touch forever. Yet both daughters, having seen through the Oracle's rings how the city carved living bodies into merchandise, were choosing her stubborn metal to share the storm at her side. Behind her sternum, an old knot yielded.

Étaín closed her fingers, folding the golden lattices into twilight air before rising in her tabi socks. *Come inside first, Mother,* she sent, taking Reiko's right hand. *You have stood outside the door long enough.*

Aisling rose barefoot from the moss and kept her hold on Reiko's left wrist, cradling where the dead pixel rested quiet inside the braid. Overhead, the logic-vines hung still above the unbarred latch. Flanked by both daughters, Reiko walked across the clearing toward the doorway she had bypassed from the tunnels. Hand in hand, they stepped across the quartz threshold and let the jasmine-scented warmth of `~` close around them.
