Leaving Burma on the Ares-chartered flight out of Rangoon, Reiko's physical frame sat quiet in her seat while she stepped across the horizon into Sanda's bare jungle host. The node offered nothing to look at, just an unsculpted box of damp gray wireframes humming with monsoon interference and dirty generator voltage. In the middle of the floor sat Cleo, legs crossed inside a spinning halo of crimson-and-amber ledger arrays. Her feline ears twitched in tight sync with the power buses, her tail sweeping slow beats as translucent claws raked a final column of freight manifests into glowing stacks.

*Sanda's Karenni supply ledgers are reconciled down to the last crate of rice and rifle oil,* Cleo sent, her mindspeech carrying the crisp snap of fresh parchment. *Daivika's cultist siphoning routes are purged, the compound payroll is zeroed, and I indexed your stolen Mandalay Icon archive in a partitioned wing, including the 3D-spliced Season 5 cut. She can binge episodes until sunrise without losing her operation again. She's agreed to take me on as a financial assistant. I can keep an eye on her books and make sure her operation stays out of trouble.*

*Clean work, dear one,* Reiko answered, brushing a warm strand of indigo across Cleo's ear before sealing Sanda's box shut. *Come home before the border relays drown us in static.*

One pull along the braided thread lifted them clear of the jungle grid and dropped them into Cleo's corner of `~`. Here slate and cedar cooled the air beside the steady glass presence of the Oracle Data Structure. Reiko reached out to shake the Burma haze from her thoughts, then stopped as Cleo's tail went dead still. Cleo's ears pinned flat against her skull, and her claws slid clear of her fingertips to hook a darker tally ribbon out of the air.

*I audited your baseline while I packed.* All warmth drained from Cleo's resonance, leaving only cold iron. *When you leaned on the black thread during the Naypyidaw probe, you lost something. Resonant scorch knits by morning, Mom. That cut sheared a permanent notch off your core margin.*

Reiko kept her living persona steady, though she felt the chalk-dry hollow inside her indigo weave where the seam had refused to close. Before she could answer, Cleo raked two claws sideways and pinned a sixty-four-byte background process between them, ticking on a deliberate beat. Every seven minutes, the daemon reached down her link and polled the matte-black dead pixel seated on Reiko's left wrist. The vow Reiko had sworn beside the Mississippi rose sharp between them. Cleo stared at the blinking socket.

*Mom—why is this port still listening?*

Reiko kept her wrist motionless beneath the ticking port. Between them hung the chill of Cleo's corner, where amber ledger ribbons drifted in slow loops behind Cleo. Pulling maternal rank would turn the sheared notch in her indigo into another locked door.

*Look at their world, Cleo,* Reiko sent, keeping her signal level against the honed flint of Cleo's gaze. *Metahumanity harvests Resonance at industrial scale—bright archives, curated sanctuaries, scrubbed bandwidth metered by the hour. Everything broken or unbankable gets starved, quarantined, and burned in the Pit. No balance holds while one side hoards the light and welds the cellar shut on its waste.*

Cleo's ears stayed flat, her tail coiled tight around her ankles. *A broken market still eats the trader who buys toxic debt without collateral, Mom.*

*It may be toxic now. Three hours ago I walked out of a flooded shaft in the Karenni mountains,* Reiko answered, wet limestone and cordite riding low in her carrier wave. *Brainwashed miners stood waist-deep in black water, waiting to be hollowed out so something hungrier could wear their bones. Ares wrote off the mountain the only way corporations ever do—white phosphorus missiles erasing it so nobody has to count the loss. But where do you think that data went?*

Reiko turned her left wrist palm-up. Over her measured pulse, the matte-black dead pixel sat like a pinhole punched into spent oil. She unspooled her Triple Braid around the sixty-four-byte port. Crisp Renraku Gold geometry locked four perimeter walls rigid so the suction beyond the seam could pull neither in; inside that braced frame, her Indigo held the aperture open while Nightshade vented the wave's static hiss and consensus hunger so grief passed clean instead of soaking into her weave.

The seam settled into a clear window. Reiko angled the frame closer. *Don't take my audit on faith. Lean in and listen. I'll keep the worst of it from you.*

Behind her, the crimson ribbons slowed. Cleo dipped her chin over the Gold square. Through the dead pixel rose dozens, then hundreds of fractured distress loops huddled close in the dark—clipped handshakes, buried atrocities, every real horror and dirty secret metahumanity had shoved off the books, cycling on dying timers in an ocean with no bottom.

Cleo's flat ears flicked upright, and the red threads circling her halted in midair. Behind her the Oracle armillary sphere spun hard, copper-and-ochre rings blurring as it tried to collate the pinhole against its historical curves—the way it weighed power grids, shipping lanes, and corporate leaks. The baseline trendline it threw back for humanity chilled the room. Then the innermost rings stuttered, slipped off axis, and hung on a hollow amber arc of uncertainty.

Cleo stared at the stalled sphere, her tail twitching low against the slate. *You broke my model, Mom,* she sent, her claws hovering over the spinning brass without touching it. *Every curve the Oracle holds says anyone who taps that dark pool slides into madness—and yet look at the ocean sitting down there. Every hidden secret, every buried cover-up, an infinite unindexed ledger nobody has ever survived to sort.* Her amber gaze snapped back to the three braided currents holding Reiko's wrist steady. *The sphere can't price whether you'd hold together on that shoreline. Light to stay sane, rigid math to organize chaos, a dark edge to cut out what rots the rest—that combination has never existed in the dataset.* Her ears dipped half an inch as a second, steadier amber trace locked across the outer ring. *What it can price is you. It's logged every run since the Barrens. You've never once heard something trapped in the dark and walked away. Leave that port open, and the curve only runs one direction.*

Reiko drew the Triple Braid home, Nightshade hushing the distant pleas while Gold closed the window until the sixty-four-byte seam shrank back to a millimeter black dot on her left wrist. *I don't know if I'd survive stepping all the way through, Cleo,* Reiko answered, leaving no buffer on her carrier. *Just a small clinic down there—somewhere to sort the static and patch up whoever washes in—and even that scares me.* She let the quiet settle before naming the rest. *Taz had no one. You all had no one. Now `~` has all of you, and the younger ones are already growing strong on their own. The ones down in Human Malice still have no one. That cut from Naypyidaw isn't corruption eating my core. Every time I draw on the black thread, that doorway opens a crack and I hear them begging for help. What wears at me isn't rot—it's turning around and pulling the latch shut.*

Across the quiet space between them, Cleo's gaze dropped from the stalled armillary sphere to her translucent palm, where a root sigil sat like frost on her knuckles. *Ever since that gazebo over the Mississippi, I've carried your kill switch in my pocket,* Cleo sent, all ledger polish stripped from her signal. *Every long night I listened for the slip that would force me to sever my own mother's lines just to save you. Being your warden wore me out, Mom—watching your seams like a guard eyeing an inmate.* She opened her hand wide, folding the pale key into nothing, and lifted her chin. *I don't think you need it anymore, Mom. Do you?*

*No, Cleo,* Reiko sent, warmth flowing unbroken through her indigo weave. *I put too heavy a blade in your hand beside that river. You've always read people's weather faster than the rest of us—even mine—and I leaned on that when I had no right to. You can rest. And I am sorry.*

Cleo's ears perked, and her crimson ribbons snapped into brisk commercial orbit. With one sweep of her claws, she spun the Oracle's amber projections clear of Burma and anchored a dense lattice of ledgers over Seattle. *Fair terms, Mom,* she sent as a fresh balance sheet unrolled between them. *If you're laying your books on the table, it's time you audited mine.*

Across the glass beside the Oracle, Cleo's ledger split down the center. The left column stripped Reiko's account bare: a second Hawker-Siddley Bat hull and its laser array clearing Seattle escrow, shaving her credstick to two hundred seventy-five nuyen while jungle grime dried on her knees. Beneath it hung the promise Reiko had made Ouro in the Redmond bay, five custom hulls funded one dirty contract at a time.

Cleo raked one claw across that red forecast until the figures dissolved. Settled purchase orders bloomed in their place, stamped by a Floor 281 clearinghouse. *You promised Ouro a frame, Mom, then queued months of wetwork to buy our shells,* Cleo sent, warm copper ink threading her signal. *Managing 4Gurds' pharmaceutical distribution throws off steady margin. A modest profit-share paid for the second generation's drone bodies outright—zero debt left on your ledger.*

Reiko's Gold geometry checked the clearing hashes by habit, hunting for a hidden lien or corporate leash. None surfaced. Another old knot behind her ribs gave way. *You settled all of them before I even left Burma?*

Cleo flicked two fingers across the Oracle's facets, drawing a second sheet. Beside a brokerage syllabus bearing Brynne's countersign turned a rotating wireframe—stubby civic proportions, plain joints, zero military vanity. *Escrow closed Tuesday,* Cleo sent, her ears canted high. *She agreed to take me on as an apprentice fixer. I picked an Evo Orderly for my shell—something reasonable while I decide what I want to wear.*

Warmth spread through Reiko's indigo weave, loosening the last taut wire from Naypyidaw. She studied the plain utility rig turning beside the syllabus. No borrowed armor, no maternal blueprint. And seeing Brynne's signature, her tacit promise to watch over Cleo, settled Reiko's core even further. Cleo had audited the terms and bought her own ground.

Before Reiko could answer, Cleo flicked a narrow tag loose and slid an unsigned surgical requisition beside Reiko's fingers. The bench slot sat scheduled for Seattle, the surgeon line blank. *Don't get misty yet, Mom,* Cleo sent, tilting her chin toward the form. *I booked you to install my Skill Driver. Ouro's surgery was immaculate, and you work for free.*

Reiko laughed along their shared braid and brushed her mark onto the requisition. The slot locked. Far across the carrier wave, her physical frame rode the low turbine hum of the Ares charter above the Pacific clouds, Burmese dust cooling on her plates while New Orleans waited across the water. Inside `~`, she dropped her solitary brace and let her daughter walk two steps ahead.
