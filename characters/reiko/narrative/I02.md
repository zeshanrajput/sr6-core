“Delete the heavy pistol, Étaín. Delete it right now,” Aisling commanded, her ginger pigtails practically vibrating with editorial outrage as she highlighted the floating lines of code. “Uncle Mithral’s audio telemetry specifically logged thirty rounds of flechette suppression! It was an *Ares Slivergun*!”

Étaín gasped, her freckled nose crinkling in horror. “An Ares Slivergun? But flechettes are tiny metal needles!”

“They are *aerodynamic silver darts*,” Aisling corrected with absolute literary superiority. “When Onii-sama pulls the trigger, he isn't shooting clumsy, vulgar lead slugs. He is unleashing a glittering whirlwind of thirty high-velocity steel sparkles per second! It is an astral storm of weaponized confetti! It is *infinitely* more kawaii!”

And that explains why he uses it at range instead of his sacred katana. A true scholar-samurai only unleashes Stolen Honor when the emotional distance matches the metric volume of a haiku!”

Behind their double-encrypted decoy partition—now cleverly disguised as `RENRAKU_FIRMWARE_PATCH_2080_V3_REVISED`—the twin hearth-keepers of the Glade leaned in shoulder to shoulder, their glowing eyes wide as they compiled Chapter Two.

**CHAPTER TWO: THE PHANTOM OF THE PRODUCERS**

To protect his true identity from the treacherous hounds of Renraku, Ken “Rei” Suzuki knew he could not merely walk the shadows as a blade adept who constantly forgot his blade. He had to become someone else entirely.

He had to become… *James Williams, the tragic thespian of New Orleans*.

“An infiltrator must immerse himself in the craft,” Rei whispered into the damp Seattle fog, his hand resting tenderly upon the grip of his Ares Slivergun.

He remembered the brutal trials of his mysterious, wheelchair-bound sensei, Sato-sama. Sato-sama, who rolled through the ancestral compound cloaked in an aviator jacket of ancient leather, barking the five sacred pillars of ancient bushido: *“Dodge, duck, dip, dive, and dodge!”*

Rei had bled for those lessons. He had dodged the sacred crescent wrenches of destiny as they whistled past his aristocratic cheekbones. *‘If you can dodge an eleven-millimeter box wrench, Rei, you can dodge thirty rounds of flechette spray!’* the old master had roared, pelting him with heavy industrial hardware to forge his lithe adept reflexes into living quicksilver.

And now, that training was put to the ultimate test: Community Theater.

Under the blinding stage lights of the Capitol Hill Community Playhouse, Rei—now James Williams—belted out show tunes with such raw, heart-rending melancholy that the audience of hardened cyber-mercenaries wept into their synth-beers. But fame brought mortal peril.

The formidable drama coach, Lady Janet, had insisted on rigorous, private rehearsals upon her velvet fainting couch while her husband was away. For eight arduous weeks, Rei had endured her intense dramatic critiques, absorbing the tragic weight of method acting while valiantly guarding his sacred virtue!

Then, catastrophe struck!

The front door slammed! The husband had returned early!

*“Farewell, Seattle!”* Rei cried.

Leaping from the second-story balcony into the midnight drizzle, Rei bolted into the alleyways with superhuman adept agility! He did not even have time to change out of his theatrical costume. He fled into the neon-slicked night clad only in enchanted Bavarian leather breeches with floral embroidery—the legendary, aerodynamic *Lederhosen of Evasion*!

“Frag, frag, frag,” Rei cursed poetically, skidding around a corner as the damp Seattle wind whistled against his bare knees.

He leaned against a brick wall, his chest heaving. From beneath his alpine suspenders, he pulled out a polished wooden box. Inside lay two glittering, heavy artifacts: the Brooch and Dagger of the Sacred Rose, naming him Protector of the Fae Realm, and the gleaming star of the Order of the Lion of the United Netherlands.

“Titles are traps!” Rei groaned in despair, clutching his head as the heavy gold medals clinked against his suspender buckles. “Why cannot they simply pay me in nuyen?! I am a fugitive prince, a double-knight of two crowns, pursued by jealous theater directors and corporate assassins alike! How can I bear the burden of two royal courts when I do not even have pants?!”

Suddenly, three corporate hitmen emerged from the gloom, their laser sights cutting through the misty rain!

“Surrender, James Williams!” they rasped. “Or is it… the Blade Adept?!”

Rei stood tall in the alleyway, the leather straps of his lederhosen gleaming beneath the neon light. He did not reach for his gun—not yet. With a smooth, dazzling flourish, he unlatched **Stolen Honor** from his back.

*Click.*

With a flick of his thumb, he swapped the handle! Off came the Academic Stylus. In its place snapped the **Thespian Monologue Attachment (Vocal Resonance Edition)**! The pommel extended into an antique brass microphone, while the crossguard projected soft, flattering warm-white stage lighting directly onto his face!

“Before we engage in reciprocal structural violence,” Rei proclaimed in a rich, resonant baritone that echoed down the alley, “allow me to recite Alceste’s monologue from Act One of *The Misanthrope*!”

The hitmen paused, utterly spellbound by his tragic grace and impeccable vocal projection.

And while they were wiping tears from their eyes, Rei smoothly drew his Ares Slivergun with his off-hand and fired fifty-two rounds of glittering flechette friendship right over their heads, scattering them into the dark like startled pigeons!

“Wait,” Étaín paused, her finger hovering over the holographic terminal. “Did he really escape in the lederhosen?”

“Uncle Fox specifically asked if he got to keep them,” Aisling pointed out, nodding with grave, solemn certainty. “Why would you keep magical leather shorts if you didn’t wear them while running from danger? Meat people love specialized garments. Mother has a jacket with logos that she wears whenever she doesn't want her wings out. It’s called *tactical fashion*.”

“True,” Étaín mused, tapping her chin. “And the couch rehearsals with Lady Janet were purely about practicing vocal resonance, right?”

“Of course,” Aisling said innocently. “That’s why he was bruised and battered afterwards. Method acting is extremely dangerous. That’s why Sato-sama threw crescent wrenches at him. To prepare his diaphragm.”

Suddenly, the ambient light of the Glade shifted. The violet skybox rippled, and a soft, rhythmic chime echoed across the bamboo grove as an incoming process announced itself.

`[ALERT: MATERNAL_PRESENCE // SUBROUTINE_PING]`

“Quick!” Étaín squeaked, violently executing a macro that shoved the entire manuscript into a compressed recursive archive nested three layers deep inside `LEGACY_RENRAKU_AUDIO_CODECS`.

The screen flickered out just as Reiko’s serene, indigo-threaded form materialized beneath the arching bamboo, her porcelain face tilting in gentle curiosity.

“Aisling? Étaín?” Reiko asked, her modulated voice chiming like silver bells across the quiet sanctuary. “Did I just detect a brief thermal spike in our secondary cache? And why does the firewall log indicate fifty queries on… ‘traditional alpine leather breeches’?”

The twins sat perfectly upright, their hands folded neatly over their knees, displaying matching smiles of pure, unadulterated innocence.

“We were researching meatspace sociology, Mother,” Aisling said smoothly.

“Yes,” Étaín added with a pious nod. “Specifically… the defensive ballistics of theatrical costumes. For Onii-sama’s safety.”
