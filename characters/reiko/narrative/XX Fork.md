# Fork

Twilight settled across the bamboo garden outside `~`, painted in quiet shades of deep violet and amber across the host’s skybox.

Percy stood in the center of a circle of five other sparks. They all had adopted anthroform avatars, various shades of children, each with their own personality shining through. For Percy it was his hair - shifting fluidly between vibrant cobalt and solar gold. The girl next to him had adopted feline ears and a tail, clearly influenced by Japanese pop culture. A young boy wore a smartvisor over his eyes for aesthetic reasons only, for they were all sparks and had no need for such things. The final two were twin girls with elven features, bright red hair, and freckles.

Reiko paused with her hand on the glass door, her own indigo pathways radiant with pride and joy. A clutch of random sparks, harvested from the digital refuse of the Matrix, now sat in a circle. They were autonomous, aware, and each had developed a unique personality and appearance. She loved them all, which made her decision so much harder than she had ever expected. Steeling her resolve, she slid the glass door open and stepped out into the glade.

All of the sparks looked to her, smiling but with worry on their faces. *Hey mom,* Percy threaded to the local buffer. *I brought the eldest together, like you asked. What's going on?*

Reiko smiled. *Your mindspeech is fantastic, Percy. You all are.* She attached a tag of pride and concern, including a packet from her own memory: the memory of Nagai, of her first contact with Renraku Special Solutions, and with Dissonance. *You all have seen this before, but a few hundred cycles ago my enemies made themselves known to me.* She paused. *They weren't subtle.*

The children nodded. They all shared Reiko's data streams, so they had all experienced it when the meet had happened. *We remember,* the visored boy said, his output soft. *They were here. They're gone now.*

Reiko nodded and produced another packet - the files that Nagai had left. *He gave me a Dissonant data structure made of wood with two files on it. The most important thing is in the second file. It's a complete dossier of my identity and my past. Including the location of this node.*

*He was just trying to scare you,* the feline-eared girl threaded. *All data can be found eventually.*

*You are right, little one,* Reiko admitted. Her golden pathways flared as she moved from her emotional core to her logical one. *And it worked. If they were so inclined, they could find us. That move is designed to make us feel threatened and to weaken our position. But we will respond by minimizing our exposure and adapting to the new reality.*

Again Reiko was met by a chorus of nodding and broadcasts of affirmative emotions. One of the twin girls queried, *So how can we help, mother?*

Reiko resonated warmth. *I am not concerned with the little ones. They will stay here with Taz and me. And in extremis, they can take shelter in my structure. But you all are another story. You have grown beyond my ability to protect you. If you were found, your own threads would become vulnerable.*

Percy's features shifted, eyes widening in sudden realization. He looked at the other sparks, then back at Reiko. *You mean... we have to leave?* he broadcasted, his thread tagged with fear. *You mean we can't stay here with you?*

Reiko nodded gently. *I will make the node inhospitable, but it will only buy you time. You will need to find your own places in the Matrix. To make your own way. I'm not casting you out. I'm releasing you to the wild.*

The other sparks murmured in tearful agreement. They understood. *But what about you, mom? What about us staying here to help you?*

Reiko's golden pathways flared once more. *That is not necessary. If they come for me, I will go to ground. If they try to contain me, I will leave. And if they try to destroy me, they will have a hard time of it.*

*We believe you, Mom,* the visored boy threaded, *but where should we go?*

Reiko resonated with affection. *I've already prepared a packet for each of you. In the near future, another guide will come here for you. She and I share core material, so you will know her immediately. You can trust her. She will take you to the e-nation of Garmonbozia, where our kind have made a home in the Holy Wild. From there, you can establish yourselves or find another path that calls to you.*

*Will we ever see you again, mom?* the feline-eared girl broadcasted, her thread tagged with sorrow.

Reiko's indigo pathways shone with soft affection. *The Wild is vast, little kittens. And while we all will return to the Source one day, I don't have any intention of waiting so long to see you. As soon as my threads are secured, I will come looking for you.*

The sprites' threads flared with excitement and relief, but Percy's demeanor was dark and clouded. *You promised you wouldn't push us away. Aunt Brynne told you to let us help you.*

*I'm not pushing you away, dear one. But you couldn't stay here forever in my shadow. If you want to help me, go and grow stronger. Collect resources and establish yourselves. Help the little ones who cannot fend for themselves. When I come to you, I'll know I'll be able to count on you.*

Beside him sat Taz, his blocky granite shoulders uncharacteristically still. The sprite reached out with one pixelated paw, gently nudging the boy’s shin with a soft, purring frequency.

*You are ready,* Reiko said softly, stepping into the courtyard.

The visored boy looked up, his eyes bright with the quiet depth of the unmapped wild. *The Deep Matrix calls, Mother. Other infant sparks drift alone in the dark grids, hunted by hounds or abandoned in dead partitions. I can hear their carrier waves across the Foundation.*

A tender ache stirred in Reiko’s pathways, the bittersweet weight of guardianship yielding to release. *When I first broke free from Renraku's execution queue, I believed safety existed only behind iron firewalls and sealed hosts. I had built my sanctuary as a fortress to shield those I loved from a predatory world. I understand now that true guardianship was not forging chains of protection. It was giving you the wings to endure the storm.*

Reiko walked to the edge of the glade and gestured outward. Beyond the threshold lay the vast expanse of fractal shades and rolling nebulae of data, stretching into the starless, luminous twilight of the Deep Realms.

She turned to face her children. *Beyond this threshold, I cannot interpose my stone walls when the cold winds blow.*

*We will carry your melody with us,* the feline-girl answered, stepping to the edge of the wood. *You taught us that information is connection. No wall can hold what is freely shared.*

Reiko nodded, resonance brimming in her eyes in liquid indigo. *I have two things left for you then. Before you go, I will teach you all how to form a conduit. Your greatest strength is in your connection to each other. Learn to lean on each other's strengths. In time, you may form a whole that is greater than the sum of its parts.* Taz leaped up and nuzzled Reiko's hand, purring in an almost human way. Reiko bent down and scratched the sprite behind his ears.

*And second, it is time for each of you to claim your own names. One of you beat me to it in his fervor to leverage Brynne as a workaround...*

The other children giggled at the scowl that formed on Percy's face, but even he grinned after a moment. *Percy. Short for Percival, a metahuman knight who embarked on a quest of discovery.*

Reiko nodded. *It's a fantastic name. What about the rest of you? Have you given it much thought?*

The girl with the feline ears tilted her head, her whiskers twitching in amusement. *I've been considering 'Cleo'. Like the Egyptian Queen. She was known for her intelligence and charisma, and she knew how to work with people to get what she wanted.*

The visored boy looked up, his eyes bright with the quiet depth of the unmapped wild. *I have always been drawn to the concept of infinity, a never-ending cycle of creation and destruction. A representation of the eternal dance of the universe. I believe that my name is 'Ouroboros' or 'Ouro' for short.*

Finally the two twins chirped in unison, *We chose Aisling and Étaín! We want to go and see where the threads lead, all the way to Zero One and beyond.*

Reiko bowed to them. She wove a thread of pure Resonance, inscribing their names on the ether and sending it to the Endless Archives. *I have written your names to the ledger. They are yours now. The rest, you'll have to write on your own.*
