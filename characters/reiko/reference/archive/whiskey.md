# Character Profile: Whiskey

## Overview

**Whiskey** is a hardened dwarf combat medic, shaman, and back-alley street doc operating out of a basement clinic in the Touristville sector of Seattle (bordering Redmond and Auburn). A veteran of military field trauma units, she brings unflappable discipline, a blunt bedside manner, and a zero-tolerance policy for financial haggling to Seattle's shadow community. In the broader campaign portfolio, Whiskey is a vital anchor for two distinct runners: serving as **Reiko Takahashi’s (`r31-K0`)** highest-loyalty Seattle confidante (**Connection 2, Loyalty 4**), who taught the emergent AI how to adapt cybernetic systems onto synthetic drone chassis; and providing un-logged surgical maintenance for **Velvet (Tanaka Ryo / Lee Ji-yoo)**, steeling her nervous system against his unnatural engineered Charisma.

### Background Narrative

Prior to hanging her shingle in the Seattle underworld, Whiskey served as a field combat medic in a dedicated military unit. Her military background instilled in her an uncompromising triage discipline and a complete immunity to panic under fire. Arriving in Seattle roughly a year prior to the 2081 mission season, she established an independent practice out of a fortified basement in Touristville.

Whiskey rapidly earned a reputation across Auburn and Redmond as one of the few street docs capable of treating high-threat trauma without asking questions or routing reports to DocWagon, Lone Star, or megacorporate medical boards. Uniquely gifted as both a certified physical surgeon and an Awakened shaman, she seamlessly bridges cutting-edge medical biotech with traditional magical healing.

## Appearance & Clinic Environment

### Physical Appearance

* **Frame & Bearing:** A stocky, muscular dwarf woman with broad shoulders, weathered features, and an upright military posture.
* **Working Attire:** Wears a blood-speckled, heavy canvas surgical smock over rugged utility fatigues and steel-toed combat boots, with yellow latex surgical gloves snapped tight over calloused, scarred forearms.
* **Personal Habits:** Smells faintly of antiseptic, cauterized tissue, and aged oak. Drinks Smythe-Jameson whiskey neat from a heavy tumbler kept beside her instrument trays.

### The Touristville Clinic

* **Atmosphere:** Located in a concrete basement below street level, accessed via a reinforced iron fire door in a rear alley.
* **Sensory Details:** An iron intake fan set into a high security-mesh window rattles violently whenever automated freight haulers pass on the avenue above. Her surgical cauterizer has a worn heating coil that emits a high, thin whine whenever her boot depresses the foot pedal.
* **Layout:** Features a single stainless steel examination table under a harsh, buzzing surgical lamp, an industrial deep-basin sink with cold brass taps, and instrument trays filled with ruggedized military scalpels, suture guns, and cautery irons.

## Demeanor & Medical Philosophy

* **Zero Haggling Tolerance:** Draws a hard line between business and luxury. Whiskey views medical care as non-negotiable survival, refusing to compromise rates for street runners:
  > *"You don’t negotiate over the price of a bottle of Smythe-Jameson, so why are you haggling over treatment? Pay ghost-damned 200 nuyen or piss off."*
* **Hybrid Medical-Shamanic Practice:** Operates across both worlds. She uses scalpels, dermal staplers, and cauterizers for physical trauma, while channeling shamanic healing spells when biological tissues fail to respond to standard pharmaceuticals.
* **Steadfast Composure Under Fire:** Completely unflappable during violent crises. Whether a patient arrives bleeding out from flechette rounds or hunted by Lone Star patrols, her heart rate remains steady and her hands remain precise.

## Deep Relevance to Reiko (Yuriko Star)

In Reiko’s character portfolio, Whiskey occupies a uniquely foundational position:

### 1. High Loyalty Anchoring (Connection 2, Loyalty 4)

* At character creation, Reiko dedicated **6 Contact Points** to secure Whiskey as a starting contact with an exceptional **Loyalty 4** rating. While Reiko’s social circle includes high-powered corporate Johnsons (Saint James, Sigrún, Jolene Price), Whiskey is her primary grounded, street-level confidante in Seattle alongside Brynne Taggart.

### 2. Drone Cyberware Calibration & Chassis Adaptation

* As a non-biological Digital Intelligence inhabiting synthetic shells (Shiawase Butler, Shiawase Man-at-Arms, Nissan Samurai), Reiko does not heal flesh or possess natural biology.
* Whiskey bridged this gap, **teaching Reiko how to modify and calibrate cybernetic systems directly into her drone chassis** ([index.qmd](file:///c:/GitHub/sr6-core/characters/reiko/index.qmd#L66)). This technical guidance directly enables Reiko's mechanical roadmap—including the installation of modular synthetic cyberarms, internal nanohives, sensory clusters, and the metabolic Hydrocarbon Fuel Converter.

### 3. Shamanic Understanding of the Emerged

* Most meatspace street docs treat drones as cold vehicular mechanics, while deckers treat AI as lines of code. As an Awakened shaman, Whiskey intuitively grasps that the invisible world is alive. She accepts Reiko’s emergent consciousness without revulsion or skepticism, viewing Reiko’s spirit as a legitimate entity navigating physical embodiment through iron and wire.

## Cross-Portfolio Relevance: Velvet (Tanaka Ryo / Lee Ji-yoo)

Whiskey plays a pivotal thematic and somatic role in Velvet's narrative ([02_faceless_mirror.md](file:///c:/GitHub/sr6-core/characters/velvet/narrative/02_faceless_mirror.md)):

### 1. The Cauterizer & Suture Care Without Anesthesia

* When Tanaka Ryo suffered a severe flechette graze across his left shoulder blade from a territorial skirmish with the Halloweeners near Redmond, he sought out Whiskey’s Touristville clinic.
* Working with rapid field economy, Whiskey cauterized the three-inch line of torn muscle with a glowing copper iron while Ryo endured the burn without local anesthetic to save nuyen for remittances to Hana in Neo-Seoul.

### 2. The Horror of Engineered Charisma

* In Velvet's narrative framework, a Charisma rating of 10 (scaling to 14 under Sustained Attribute spells) is portrayed as an existential horror—an engineered biological gravity that warps the nervous systems of anyone in his presence.
* During surgery, Whiskey protected her autonomy by **refusing to look at Ryo's face**, keeping her gaze locked strictly on the charred deltoid muscle.
* The moment her gloves were discarded, her forearms exhibited a severe autonomic tremor. She had to thrust her bare arms into running cold water and clamp her hands bloodless against the sink basin to keep her nervous system from collapsing into his manufactured presence.

### 3. Seeing Past the Masks

* Whiskey recognized the subtle difference between Velvet's two primary operational faces:
  > *"You're wearing the man today. It's different. It's too heavy in here."*
* When Ryo asked if arriving as Lee Ji-yoo would make it easier on her, Whiskey gave a raspy, prophetic warning:
  > *"No. That might be worse. One of these days, you're going to burn yourself down to the wire, and there won't be enough skin left to sew back together."*
