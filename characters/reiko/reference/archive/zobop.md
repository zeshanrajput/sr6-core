# Faction & Contact Profile: The Zobop & The Old Man (Miles Pillar)

## Executive Overview

The **Zobop** is a formidable, ruthless criminal syndicate and secretive Vodou magical society based in New Orleans. Operating across the murky intersection of organized crime, Caribbean smuggling corridors, and dark bokor sorcery, the Zobop is one of the dominant underworld powers in the Big Easy, locked in perpetual conflict with the Mafia (Romero and Kozlowski families) and rival syndicates.

In Reiko's campaign portfolio, the Zobop is recorded as an elite **Connection 6, Loyalty 3 (1 Favor Point banked)** organizational contact acquired during **SRM 2083-18 (*Some Kind of Voodoo?*)**. Reiko’s primary liaison and personal connection to the syndicate is **The Old Man** (alias **Miles Pillar**), a veteran hobgoblin lieutenant, fixer, and conflicted traditionalist practitioner. Furthermore, through her role as an apprentice hostess and sensory architect at the **Coffin Girls** salon, Reiko has cultivated a trusted, protective relationship with rank-and-file Zobop practitioners and youth who frequent the sanctuary to escape the internal brutality of their syndicate's escalating civil war.

### Key Figures & Operatives

1. **Papa Zobop:** The enigmatic, shadowy supreme leader of the syndicate whose true identity and whereabouts remain tightly guarded.
2. **Mama Eliza (Mambo Eliza):** High priestess of the **Red Tide** (*Marée Rouge*), the dominant, bloodthirsty faction within the Red College. Ruthless, megalomaniacal, and unbound by traditional Vodou ethics, she utilizes forbidden ritual grimoires, human sacrifices, and specialized "zombie dust" to warp subjects into monstrous creations or leave victims permanently vulnerable to possession.
3. **Old Man (Miles Pillar):** Senior hobgoblin lieutenant, fixer, and veteran Vodou shaman. Functions as Reiko’s direct contact and one of the few high-ranking Zobop leaders seeking to preserve traditional spiritual balance against Mama Eliza’s descent into blood madness.
4. **Mackenson "Mr. Smiley" Salomon:** Notorious adept, hitman, and ritual torturer for the Red Tide. Known for hosting ritual sacrifice gatherings at isolated plantation houses; recently fled NOLA after incriminating blood magic evidence was leaked to NOMS.
5. **Harriet Noseman:** Cunning Zobop reconnaissance shaman deployed for espionage, infiltration, and ledger extraction against rival syndicates.
6. **Limper:** Old Man’s one-armed nephew, operating as an expendable Caribbean drug mule and courier.

## Detailed Profile: The Old Man (Miles Pillar)

### 1. Identity & Tabletop Stats

* **True Name / Moniker:** Old Man
* **Cover Identity:** Miles Pillar (used when operating as an independent fixer)
* **Metatype:** Hobgoblin
* **Role:** Zobop Lieutenant, Underworld Broker, Vodou Practitioner / Shaman
* **Negotiation Pool:** 14 dice (hard-nosed, perceptive bargainer).
* **Arcane & Combat Capabilities:** High-circle Vodou spellcraft, talismanic preparation, and the ability to deploy and direct squads of **Force 6 Zombies** (reanimated former street thugs) as heavy muscle.

### 2. Physical Description & Presence

* **Appearance:** A grizzled, weathered hobgoblin whose leathery skin is etched with intricate, ancient Vodou *vévé* tattoos that faint-glow with eldritch energy during spellcasting. He wears a scraggly beard, sharp filed hobgoblin tusks, and an unkempt silver-gray ponytail.
* **The Gaze:** Naturally deep-set dark eyes that transition into absolute, obsidian voids when channeling the *lwa* or issuing lethal syndicate decrees.
* **Attire:** A pragmatic hybrid of ceremonial practitioner and bayou survivalist—scuffed combat boots, camouflage trousers, and a faded patchwork coat embroidered with protective wards, alligator teeth, and chicken bones. He wears a broad-brimmed traveler's hat adorned with feathers and talismans that cast dark shadows across his brow.

### 3. Personality & Ideology

* **Spiritual Devotion vs. Moral Revulsion:** A devout practitioner who reveres the *lwa* as divine conduits of order, fate, and balance. While comfortable with traditional underworld violence, extortion, and smuggling, he is sickened by Mama Eliza’s sadistic atrocities, blood sacrifices, and the transformation of the syndicate into a mindless slaughter engine. He bides his time, obeying protocol to protect his family and crew while quietly backing third-party assets to counter Red Tide extremism.
* **Demeanor:** Calm, laconic, and unnervingly composed. Speaks in low, gravelly riddles heavy with French-Creole patois, frequently calling friends and business partners *"chère"* or *"mon ami"*.

## Syndicate Operations & Methods

* **Extortion, Loan Sharking, & Debt Traps:** Preys upon vulnerable sprawl denizens and bayou communities, trapping targets in inescapable financial webs (such as the 30,000¥ debt weaponized against Kirby Bosko in *Blackwater Delta*).
* **Maritime & River Smuggling:** Coordinates fast-boat contraband runs across the Gulf and Mississippi delta, moving illegal alchemical reagents, street drugs, and untraceable weapons through shallow bayou waterways.
* **Necromantic Muscle & Blood Sorcery:** Unlike conventional syndicates, the Zobop routinely augments its frontline muscle with mindless **Force 6 Zombies**. Red Tide bokors experiment with necro-toxins, animalistic gene-craft, and non-consensual spirit possession to terrorize enemies.

## Reiko’s Relationship & Coffin Girls Synergy

### 1. The SRM 2083-18 Investigation (*Some Kind of Voodoo?*)

Reiko's bond with the Zobop was forged during the missing-practitioners crisis:

* **The Joint Contract:** Faced with the mysterious disappearance of several promising young Zobop initiates alongside members of the League of Laveau, the **Old Man** formed an uneasy, covert truce with **Renée Martin** (League liaison) to hire Reiko's team to investigate.
* **The Trail to the Salon:** Reiko traced the missing youths to the **Coffin Girls** sanctuary, uncovering that the victims were innocent patrons of the salon who were targeted outside its walls.
* **Breaking the Humanis Siege:** Reiko arrived just as Humanis Policlub extremists were storming the convent. Her team neutralized the assault, preserved the sanctuary, and captured the riot leaders.
* **Syndicate Recognition:** By recovering the trail of the missing youths without sparking an all-out turf war between the League and the Zobop, Reiko earned the profound gratitude of the Old Man and the syndicate's traditionalist wing, resulting in her permanent **Connection 6, Loyalty 3** standing.

### 2. Safe Haven at the Coffin Girls Salon

* **Acolyte Hospitality & Neutral Ground:** Zobop soldiers, smugglers, and young initiates frequently visit the Coffin Girls salon to decompress. As an acolyte hostess, Reiko serves them tea (*Chanoyu* hospitality), monitors room acoustics, and dampens corporate/matrix surveillance.
* **Mutual Respect:** The Zobop recognizes Reiko not as an expendable mercenary, but as a trusted guardian of the salon who respects the *lwa* and shields their rank-and-file from unnecessary corporate crossfire.

## Complete List of Module Appearances

1. **`SRM 2083-01: Gaining Grounds`**
   * The Zobop dispatches shaman **Harriet Noseman** to infiltrate a Romero family business compound to steal and copy critical financial and illicit transaction ledgers.
2. **`SRM 2083-03: Unofficial Dunkelzahn Fan Club`**
   * Historical revelation: Seven years prior, **Mama Eliza** tested an experimental batch of zombie dust on a group of children, permanently destabilizing their auras and leaving them chronically vulnerable to spirit possession.
3. **`SRM 2083-04: Gantillon’s Last Run`**
   * The **Old Man**, operating incognito as fixer **Miles Pillar**, arranges a covert meet at *Murph’s Portside* to hire shadowrunners to retrieve nine stolen items for Luis Duval—an operation orchestrated to spring a trap for the Red College.
4. **`SRM 2083-08: Dragon’s Gambit`**
   * **Old Man** hires shadowrunners out of *The Cursed Oasis* in Gretna for 9,000¥ to kidnap scion Brian Romero ("The Little Prince") from Hotel Ebonshade during a masquerade gala. The operation serves Mama Eliza's plot to break the Romero family via a dark blood ritual. Old Man is seen coordinating a side deal with **Jolene Price** (Voice of the Crime Dragon Terasca).
5. **`SRM 2083-10: Wasted Talent`**
   * Zobop enforcers enforce long-standing grudges against the Du Pré family, culminating in a brutal urban firefight around Claire Du Pré’s family home in Holy Cross.
6. **`SRM 2083-11: Blackwater Delta`**
   * The Zobop aggressively pursues Kirby Bosko over an unpaid 30,000¥ gambling debt, raiding his secluded bayou cabin to capture and enslave him.
7. **`SRM 2083-12: When You're Smiling`**
   * Focuses on **Mackenson "Mr. Smiley" Salomon**, the sadistic Red Tide adept and hitman who hosts a gruesome ritual human sacrifice gathering at an abandoned antebellum plantation.
8. **`SRM 2083-14: Deepest Roots`**
   * Zobop muscle accosts shadowrunners outside a restaurant to interrogate them and verify they are not corporate or syndical spies surveilling Daniel Fortune.
9. **`SRM 2083-18: Some Kind of Voodoo?` (Cornerstone Reiko Contact Run)**
   * **Old Man** and **Renée Martin** co-hire Reiko to find missing voodoo youth. Reiko defends the Coffin Girls sanctuary from a Humanis assault, receives guidance from the Comtesse and the Bone Man (Seneschal of Baron Samedi), and earns her **Connection 6, Loyalty 3** contact rating with the Zobop.
