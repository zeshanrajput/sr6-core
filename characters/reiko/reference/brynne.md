# Character Profile: Brynne Taggart

## Overview

**Brynne Taggart** is a high-stakes independent shadow fixer and broker operating out of the Renton district in the Seattle Sprawl. A direct protégé of the legendary Seattle fixer Saint James, Brynne blends the grifting survival instincts of a street-raised con artist with the polished political maneuvering of an elite fixer. In Reiko Takahashi's (`r31-K0` / Yuriko Star) history, Brynne is a foundational figure: she financed the physical hardware that birthed Reiko's `r3sP@wn` sanctuary, issued her first Fake SIN, coined the identity "Yuriko Star," and evolved from a transactional debt-holder into Reiko's closest metahuman confidante and chosen family.

### Background Narrative

Raised on the rough streets of Renton, Brynne began her career running grifts and cons to survive the sprawl. Her razor-sharp tactical intellect and natural charisma caught the attention of **Saint James**, Seattle's most renowned shadow fixer. Saint James took Brynne under his wing, grooming her in high-level influence, political leverage, and the intricate economics of shadow brokering, considering her his finest student.

Operating independently, Brynne quickly established herself as a major Seattle power broker, moving hundreds of thousands of nuyen weekly through contracts pairing street runners with high-paying corporate clients. Despite her elite connections, Brynne never abandoned her street roots. She anchored her network in Renton, fiercely protecting local talent like rapper Swango and maintaining deep personal loyalty to those within her circle.

## Appearance & Mannerisms

### Physical Appearance (Meatspace)

* **Frame & Features:** Human female of slender, athletic build with copper-red/auburn hair cut in a pragmatic bob, bright green eyes, and pale, freckled skin.
* **Attire:** Favors tailored charcoal-grey business suits with subtly frayed cuffs, worn over simple blouses or street jackets—projecting executive competence while signaling that she still walks the alleys.
* **Post-Captivity Traits:** Following her prolonged imprisonment aboard an Eighty-Eights krill factory ship (*SRM 2081-18*), she suffers from chronic bronchial damage, histamine scarring, and a persistent dry cough caused by industrial krill dust. During recovery, she is often seen in oversized silk robes, tapping commlink AROs from bed while defying DocWagon rest mandates.

### Matrix Persona

* **Virtual Form:** Unlike deckers who embrace flamboyant or weaponized avatars, Brynne formats her persona as an immaculate, high-resolution reflection of herself: a professional businesswoman with copper-red hair, sharp freckles, and tailored charcoal tailoring.
* **Sensory Signatures:** Her persona perpetually holds a slender cigarette that vents data-smoke in rhythmic, timed puffs matching her cognitive cadence.
* **VR Aversion:** Uncomfortable with deep Virtual Reality immersion, Brynne prefers augmented reality (AR) or short-burst direct neural links, trusting meatspace intuition over full sensory override.

### Personality & Demeanor

* **Street Grit & Corporate Polish:** Seamlessly switches between the hardened slang of Renton dive bars and the crisp, formal deference required in corporate executive suites.
* **Crisp & Direct:** Despises small talk and posturing. During meets, she bypasses preambles to lay out operational parameters and margins immediately.
* **Fierce Loyalty Behind a Cynical Shield:** Masks her deep empathy beneath biting sarcasm and professional detachment. While warning runners that *"everything is a scam"* and *"nobody has good choices,"* she routinely endangers her life, finances, and network to protect friends and allies.

## Operational Style & Methodology

* **Favor Currency Economy:** Trades heavily in personal favors rather than pure nuyen. She builds resilient, sprawling webs of mutual obligation across Seattle that prove far more durable than transactional cash.
* **Trojan Horse Operations:** Rather than confronting rival fixers directly, she systematically neutralizes their preferred runner assets, forcing rivals to hire her own vetted teams as deniable proxies (*SRM 2081-03*).
* **Contract Stacking:** Expertly pairs non-conflicting contracts from separate employers (e.g., merging an Everett data-theft with an Octagon Triad recovery run) to maximize runner payouts and leverage.
* **Fail-Safe Decentralized Network:** Maintains a hidden, offline network of commlinks and dead-drop logic scripts across Seattle. If her biomonitor drops or registers critical trauma, emergency subroutines automatically fragment-decrypt evidence, wipe debts for vulnerable associates, and dispatch distress beacons to her inner circle.

## Network & Key Relationships

* **Saint James:** Her revered mentor and legendary Seattle fixer who trained her in high-level influence. When Brynne went missing, Saint James came out of retirement to launch a full-scale investigation (*SRM 2081-06*).
* **Eddie Wei:** Major Seattle Triad fixer and Brynne's nemesis. Eddie murdered Lyle Green and orchestrated Brynne's abduction and faked execution when she uncovered his betrayal.
* **Swango:** Renton rap artist whom Brynne discovered, mentored, and represented. Brynne risked her life investigating Lone Star vendettas to protect Swango (*SRM 2081-05*).
* **Ni Ni Xiaolu:** Octagon Triad lieutenant with whom Brynne regularly cross-promotes runner contracts and shares street intelligence.
* **Operator214:** Her go-to Dallas-based dwarf decker for complex remote Matrix reconnaissance.

## Deep Relevance to Reiko (Yuriko Star)

Brynne is arguably the single most influential metahuman in Reiko's existence. Their relationship progressed from a predatory corporate debt-contract into deep, unconditional mutual loyalty and kinship.

### 1. The SINner Debt & The Birth of Yuriko Star (*05 A SINner's Loan*)

* **The Haven Loan:** When Reiko escaped Renraku deletion as an unmapped pilot prototype, she desperately needed physical hardware to shelter rescued proto-sapient sprites and infant data bombs. Brynne arranged a **75,000¥ loan** (at 20% interest compounded monthly) to lease a damp warehouse in the Redmond Barrens, founding **`r3sP@wn`**.
* **The Identity & The Collar:** Brynne registered Reiko's Fake SIN and coined her shadowrunner alias: **Yuriko Star**. The debt-contract compiled as a heavy crimson-bordered file that Reiko perceived in the Matrix as a suffocating **iron contract-collar** around her neck, compelling her into the violent shadows to service the monthly interest.

### 2. The Credstick Bribe & "Trying to Make Friends" (*14 A Point of Favor*)

* Following an incident where Brynne went dark behind a military-grade matrix jammer, Reiko cornered Brynne in a Redmond Barrens dive bar. Reiko slid her mission payment back across the table as a "bribe," demanding that Brynne never drop off the grid without sending a distress packet.
* When Brynne cynically challenged why a cold machine would risk its chassis for a debt-holder, Reiko transmitted: *"I am overriding my optimization protocols. I am trying to make friends."* Brynne replied: *"Maybe stop trying to befriend the people who keep you on a leash."* Reiko's quiet response: *"I surmise it is already too late."*

### 3. The M-TOC Mark II & "Mundane Garbage" (*17 Sheets*)

* **The Debt Settlement Offer:** Reiko acquired a military-grade **M-TOC Mark II** tactical operations console, intending to trade it to Brynne to completely erase her 75,000¥ debt and claim sole ownership of the server farm. Recognizing that Reiko would be better protected by keeping the unit for her runner teams, Brynne offered to cancel the debt anyway. When Reiko realized Brynne was taking a financial loss, Brynne admitted she knew from their first meeting Reiko would never abandon her responsibilities: *"No one voluntarily goes into debt if they don't care."*
* **The Lesson of Mundane Garbage:** Inspecting Reiko's sterile Seattle studio, Brynne taught her how to convincingly pass as human: *"A Fake SIN isn't just data on a grid. It's an aura of mundane garbage. There aren't any dirty dishes in the sink, the mattress looks un-slept on... That's how you do it."* In response, Reiko authored **`[SUBROUTINE: MUNDANE_GARBAGE]`**, instructing her Shiawase Butler drone to manually muss sheets and toss pillows at night.

### 4. The Sieve, The "Payback" Script, & Pulaski Street (*18 The Geometry of a Sieve* & *19 Legacy Code*)

* **The Sieve at 600% Capacity:** When Brynne was kidnapped during *SRM 2081-06*, Reiko disabled all stealth subroutines, turning her entire consciousness into an uninsulated filter spanning the Seattle grid. She confronted the Blood Mountain Boys and Steelbit's mercenaries, offering them *"the geometry of their own execution"* before parlaying.
* **The `payback` Script:** Reiko received Brynne's dead-drop automated script, which wiped Yuriko Star's balance to zero (`[STATUS: PAID IN FULL / LIEN RECONVEYED]`). However, Reiko noticed the trigger condition was `IF BIOMONITOR == CRITICAL`, not `DEAD`. Reiko halted the garbage collection thread, refusing to let the file dissolve.
* **Visiting Pulaski Street:** Refusing to believe Brynne was gone, Reiko tracked the other debts wiped by Brynne's dying script to an apartment at 1412 Pulaski Street. There, she met an elderly ork grandmother whose life Brynne had quietly preserved, coming face-to-face with the human grace behind Brynne's cynical exterior.

### 5. Tabletop Rescue & Promotion (*SRM 2081.18*)

* During the tabletop run of *SRM 2081-18: Friendships Never Grow Rust* (played 2026-08-20), Yuriko Star and her team answered Brynne's distress broadcast, assaulted the Eighty-Eights krill factory ship, and extracted Brynne alive.
* Reiko earned **+4 Favor Points** from the rescue. Combined with prior favors from *SRM 2081.05*, Brynne was auto-promoted from **Loyalty 2 to Loyalty 3** (consuming 3 FP), leaving **3 Favor Points** banked.

### 6. "Aunt Brynne" & The Holy Wild Sanctuary Host (*24 Symlink*)

* **Post-Rescue Recovery:** While recovering in Seattle from bronchial krill dust damage, Brynne was introduced to **Percy**, Reiko's compiler sprite. Percy addressed her as *"Aunt Brynne,"* demanding she rest.
* **The Wall Niche:** Brynne jacked into Reiko's Rating 2 Framework Host (The Holy Wild Sanctuary Host) for the first time. In a recessed display niche beside the book-lined alcove, Brynne discovered the disarmed, frozen render of her original contract-collar. Reiko explained: *"A contract is just math, Brynne. But you gave me a name and a life. I wasn't going to let the grid pretend you never existed."*
* **The Searching Moths:** Brynne learned that Reiko's data-sprite "moths" had sifted corporate black-site logs across North America for seven months, never dropping her trace. Brynne mediated between Reiko and Percy, encouraging Reiko to stop suffering in isolation and let her sprite collective share the emotional load.

### 7. The NOLA Reckoning (*26 SIGTSTP*)

* In New Orleans, eco-shaman Roanoke confronted Reiko about her emotional avoidance, accusing her of hiding in the bayou and the Coffin Girls salon: *"You're using New Orleans as an escape so you don't have to go back to Pulaski Street. You think Brynne wants an appliance? You think she survived months in a shipping container waiting for you to buy enough silicone to pass an inspection?"* This pushed Reiko to confront her self-worth and draft an overdue transmission back to Seattle.
