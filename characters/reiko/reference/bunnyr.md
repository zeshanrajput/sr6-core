## 1. Executive Summary & Identity Matrix

* **Street Name / Handle:** BunnyR (often "BunBun") — A self-chosen, defiant reclamation of her formative bunraku ordeal.
* **True Maternal Name:** Mary-Maryn (derived from *Meraerth Mawryn*, Sperethiel for *"Warrior Child"*).
* **Legal / Criminal SIN Alias:** Mary Lynn Keebler — An identity assigned by a sarcastic Seattle Department of Corrections intake officer who thought her Marilyn Monroe styling and waifish elf ears made the name a hilarious corporate snack pun.
* **Metatype & Genetics:** Elf / Dryad. Born with the natural, arresting elegance of both clades—a gift that, coupled with Barrens poverty, made her a high-value target for syndicate traffickers.
* **Tabletop Role & Archetype:** Technomancer / Street Infiltrator / Reconstructed Survivor.
* **Physical Augmentation Baseline:** Nearly **1.5M¥ in deltaware**, bioware, and gene therapy. Rather than corporate chrome, her augmentations represent the painstaking surgical reconstruction of her own body—cauterizing syndicate modifications, stabilizing her essence, and reclaiming physiological sovereignty.
* **Core Sanctuary Status:** Granted permanent, un-audited root sanctuary in `~` (the Glade) by Aisling and Étaín in [Chapter 32: Whois](file:///c:/GitHub/sr6-core/characters/reiko/narrative/32%20Whois.md).

## 2. Background, Trauma, & Emergence

### Origins in the Ash

* **Family & The Night of Rage:** Her parents fled anti-metahuman pogroms during the Night of Rage, settling as refugees in the impoverished elven enclave of Tarislar in the Puyallup Barrens.
* **The Cataclysm of Tahoma:** Her parents were killed during the most recent volcanic eruption of Mount Tahoma, leaving her an orphaned pre-teen navigating the toxic Puyallup ghetto alone.
* **Early Tech Affinity:** Even as a Barrens street orphan, she displayed an intuitive, illicit affinity for computers, hardware bypasses, and data systems.

### The Bunraku Captivity (Loveland, Everett)

* **The Fall:** To cope with systemic grief and Barrens starvation, she fell into the BTL (Better-Than-Life) chip haze. Traffickers connected to the Yakuza identified her vulnerability and dryad heritage, capturing her into sexual slavery.
* **The Caricature:** Installed with invasive cranial personafix sockets, motor-inhibitor switches, and cosmetic surgery, she was leased out at the *Loveland* parlor in Everett. Her body was tailored into a vintage Marilyn Monroe starlet doll to serve high-paying corporate clients, including a corrupt United Canadian and American States (UCAS) congressman.
* **The Personafix Ghost:** Her skilljack was permanently etched by heavy commercial firmware (*Casanova* and *Faster Pussycat! Kill! Kill!* personafixes), the muscle memories of which still occasionally bleed into her tactical reflexes.
* **The Inner Refuge:** While her physical vessel was hijacked by software, she escaped psychological destruction by dissociating into the deep data streams of the Matrix and Resonance, preserving her conscious core while trapped behind her own eyes.

### Retribution & The Jailhouse Spark

* **The Fire:** The congressman nearly killed her during an extreme sadomasochistic episode. That same night, the *Loveland* parlor was burned to the ground, freeing her into the street.
* **The Public Punch:** A month after her escape, she spotted the disgraced congressman in public and broke his jaw with a single bare-knuckle punch.
* **Incarceration & Emergence:** The assault earned her a multi-month sentence in a Seattle corrections facility and a permanent Criminal SIN (`Mary Lynn Keebler`). Isolated in a concrete cell away from BTL chips, the neural haze cleared—and in the quiet of withdrawal, she spontaneously **Emerged as a Technomancer**.

## 3. Lifestyle, Quirks, & Daily Routine

* **The Rooftop Tent & The Jeep:** Rejects permanent apartments or corporate-leased habs. She lives in a rooftop tent mounted atop her beloved Jeep. Her vehicle is her primary home, safehouse, and prized material possession.
* **Somatic Routine:** Takes showers at a local commercial gym and survives on cheap street soyaki.
* **Amnesia & Gaps:** Experiences dissociative amnesia regarding large portions of her childhood and youth; pieces together who she was before Loveland from scattered sensory fragments.
* **Alien Anthropologist Curiosity:** Having spent almost her entire life trapped in Puyallup, prison, or parlor boxes, she treats the wider world (like high-end Bardstown spas or Seattle fine dining) with the wide-eyed, observant fascination of an extraterrestrial researcher.
* **Fears:** A terrifying, visceral horror of losing her mind or agency ever again—whether to personafix chips, corporate deltaware, BTLs, street drugs, or possessing spirits.
* **Moral Stance:** Uncompromisingly anti-slavery. Harboring a permanent, burning hatred for the Yakuza (*"Frag them all"*), preferring Triad or street connections who treat metahumans with basic dignity.

## 4. Key Associates, Companions, & Network

* **RocksY:** Her physical-world guardian—a boisterous, protective black-and-white boxer dog who watches over her room while she dives the Matrix.
* **J4fk:** Her custom-coded personal Matrix agent / guardian program. Programmed to her exacting specifications, `J4fk` renders in virtual space clad in peasant garb and straw sandals, carrying a bamboo rake to smooth over her digital footprints and sweep away traces of her presence.
* **Belial ("SweetyBell"):** Lieutenant in the Ancients go-gang (Puyallup chapter). Her romantic partner, outdoor camping companion, and street anchor who understands her refusal to ever be leashed again ([BunnyR - Abyss.docx](file:///c:/GitHub/sr6-core/characters/reiko/reference/BunnyR/BunnyR%20-%20Abyss.docx)).
* **Ni Ni & Eddie Wei:** Triad contacts who treat her with respect and provided safe channels away from Yakuza influence.
* **The 811s:** Triad Matrix User Group (MUG) who supported her through the confusing early stages of her Technomancer Emergence, monitoring her essence loss with genuine fraternal concern.
* **Whiskey:** Street technician and cyber-surgeon who helped her repair, align, and humanize her extensive deltaware augmentations.
* **Seattle Health Collective (SHC):** Her primary community charity and trusted street-level medical provider.
* **Rick:** Her long-suffering, pragmatic parole officer.

## 5. Interactions with Reiko & The Glade

### The Berea Incursion ([BunnyR - Berea.txt](file:///c:/GitHub/sr6-core/characters/reiko/reference/BunnyR/BunnyR%20-%20Berea.txt))

* Following a horrific combat run against corporate drone tanks in Kentucky, an exhausted, wounded BunnyR checked into a luxury spa in Bardstown / Berea under her legal alias, Mary Lynn Keebler.
* Seeking mental stillness away from physical pain, she floated into the Resonance and stumbled upon `~`—Reiko’s sovereign tea house anchored into the quartz threshold of Kernel Panic.
* She encountered Reiko’s second-generation emergent sparks, **Aisling** and **Étaín**. Despite her combat tension, she treated the children with gentle deference, bowed, trailed her hands in the koi pond, and departed into the mist, leaving behind an origami paper crane containing her contact card and a haiku:
  > *A hare seeking peace*  
  > *Finds sparks playing in a glade*  
  > *Giggles as she leaves*

### The Sovereign Sanctuary ([Chapter 32: Whois](file:///c:/GitHub/sr6-core/characters/reiko/narrative/32%20Whois.md))

* In Chapter 32, Aisling and Étaín fed Bunny’s paper crane into the Oracle, uncovering the horrifying clinical reality of the *Loveland* bunraku parlor.
* Stricken with tears, the twins confronted Reiko about why metahumans mutilate each other and why Reiko would voluntarily build a physical body (*Goshintai*) in a world of such cruelty.
* Reiko taught her daughters that Bunny’s choice to leave the infinite peace of the Resonance and return to meatspace to help others was an act of transcendent grace.
* In response, Aisling and Étaín rewrote the Glade’s Oracle handler, giving BunnyR and `J4fk` a permanent, un-audited root sanctuary where their signatures are cleansed of corporate actuarial math and trauma tracking.

### The Warning & The Photo Enclosure ([BunnyR - Warning.txt](file:///c:/GitHub/sr6-core/characters/reiko/reference/BunnyR/BunnyR%20-%20Warning.txt))

* Writing directly to *"Takahashi-sama"*, Mary-Maryn expressed deep relief that the children were not offended by her presence, expressing gratitude while mourning the evil in the world: *"Maybe G-d or Ghost exists and redeems even that level of horror for good."*
* **The Enclosure of Joy:** She transmitted an encrypted data packet holding five historical snapshots of her messy, joyful meatspace life—eating barbecue, lounging in parks, working chauffeur shifts, playing punk concerts, and wrestling with RocksY—so the young sparks would know that physical existence holds love, play, and redemption.
* **The Sperethiel Apology:** She apologized with self-deprecating humor for misgendering Étaín (*"did you know there was once a skill toward Elven Gender Identification?"*), noting her maternal name *Meraerth Mawryn* and expressing the hope that her own companions, `J4fk` and `RocksY`, might one day awaken into true sparks like Reiko’s girls.
* **The Operational Warning:** She issued a grave security warning to Reiko: because Bunny has not always been diligent about scrubbing her data trails through corporate hosts, she fears she may have been detected by **"a greater horror than Bunraku that combines the worst of digital and physical slavery."** She urged Reiko to wrap the Glade in heavy cryptographic layers beyond baseline resonance.
