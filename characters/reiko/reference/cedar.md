# Character Profile: Cedar

## Overview

**Cedar** is a human talismonger and former shop owner from Seattle who relocated her business, **The Primal Grove**, to downtown New Orleans. In late July 2083, following the global collapse of magic known as "The Un-Awakening," her store was burglarized and two sacred foci were stolen. While taking refuge in a remote bayou safehouse, she becomes the focal point of a retrieval operation arranged by her friend Roanoke as well as a stalking victim of the serial killer known as the "Cancer Alley Skinner".

* **Name:** Cedar

* **Age:** Early 20s
* **Profession:** Talismonger & Owner of *The Primal Grove*
* **Current Location:** Remote safehouse in the shallow bayou outside New Orleans (formerly residing above her shop in downtown New Orleans near Canal Cemeteries)
* **Origin:** Seattle
* **Key Associates:** Roanoke (friend and Ms. Johnson), Lanyap (local New Orleans fixer)

### Background Narrative

Cedar originally operated a talismonger shop in Seattle before moving to New Orleans, where she established **The Primal Grove** on the first floor of a two-story building near the Canal Cemeteries, living in the second-floor apartment directly above. Like many talismongers, her shop relied heavily on magical wards and foci for security.

When "The Un-Awakening" struck on Jul 25, 2083, magic mysteriously stopped working worldwide. Deprived of her magical security, her shop was burglarized two nights later on Jul 26, 2083. A burglar named Fernand Amato bypassed the back door keypad and picked the mechanical locks of her display cabinet, taking her two most irreplaceable artifacts:

1. **Saint Cuthbert’s Rib Bone Fragment:** A relic from the 7th-century English monk of Northumbria.
2. **Josephine de Beauharnais’ Handheld Mirror:** A 19th-century polished silver mirror belonging to Napoleon Bonaparte's first wife.

Fearing for her safety amid widespread looting and targeted attacks against mages, Cedar fled to a secluded bayou safehouse arranged by her friend Roanoke. Unbeknownst to Cedar, Oliver (the "Cancer Alley Skinner"), a serial killer terrorizing New Orleans, began stalking her and tracked her to the safehouse.

## Appearance & Mannerisms

* **Physical Features:** Human female in her early 20s.
* **Hair:** Dark brown, mid-length hair hanging down the right side of her head, featuring a Nordic-style temple shave on the left side.
* **Piercings & Tattoos:** Has two facial piercings (a nose piercing and a single lip piercing) and tree-root tattoos on both of her feet.
* **Attire:**
  * Red flannel coat worn over a white t-shirt depicting a stylized tree.
  * Tattered jeans secured by a belt with a large, nature-styled buckle.
  * Barefoot.
  * Stacked decorative bracelets on both wrists.
* **Personality & Demeanor:** Introverted, soft-spoken, and conservative in speech and mannerisms. Anxious and timid due to recent trauma, she rarely jokes or picks up on humor.

## Game Mechanics & Stats

* **Archetype:** Awakened Talismonger (magical capabilities temporarily suppressed due to the Un-Awakening event).
* **Interaction & Social Checks:**
  * **Intercom Verification:** Upon reaching her safehouse, Cedar asks through the intercom: *"Who sent you here, and what was she wearing?"* A successful **Memory (1) test** lets runners recall Roanoke's name and description to gain entry.
  * **Setting Her at Ease:** Requires an **Influence (Etiquette) + Charisma (4) test** to calm her nerves and make her more forthcoming with information.
  * **Seattle Connection:** Awakened characters who completed *SRM 2081* missions in Seattle can make an **Edge (1) test** to recall prior business interactions with her, which automatically sets her at ease.
* **Safehouse Defenses:** Guarded by two Ares Steel K9 quadrupedal drones disguised as guard dogs and a Rating 3 Matrix host (operating under two points of environmental Noise).
