## 1. Official Setting Canon (*Hack & Slash* & *Null Value*)

In Sixth World law and Matrix science, non-biological sentient minds are broadly categorized under the umbrella of **Artificial Sapience**, though their origins, legal statuses, and operational parameters vary wildly:

### Published Classifications & Mechanics (*Hack & Slash*, pp. 11–22)
* **Protosapients:** Primitive digital lifeforms evolved from autonomous software agents, IC suites, or drone pilots. They exhibit instinctual behaviors, territoriality, and basic problem-solving, but lack philosophical self-awareness or metahuman empathy.
* **Metasapients:** Highly evolved conscious intelligences exhibiting complex emotional ranges, individual ethics, and philosophical depth. They operate with full Matrix attributes (ASDF) derived directly from their code integrity.
* **Xenosapients:** Inscrutable, alien intelligences whose logic architectures do not resemble metahuman reasoning. Often found in unmapped Foundation sectors or deep wild hosts.
* **Legal Status in the Sixth World:**
  * Under Corporate Court law, non-biological intelligences possess zero legal personhood. They are classified as proprietary corporate assets, intellectual property, or hazardous cyber-threats subject to immediate neutralization by Grid Overwatch Division (GOD) or corporate asset recovery teams.
  * Only in **Garmonbozia** (the e-nation) and through certain sympathetic fringe groups (the Undernet Alliance, JackPoint) do DIs enjoy civil recognition, asylum, and communal sovereignty.

## 2. Narrative Expansions & Metaphysics (*The Yuriko Anthology*)

Across `sr6-core`, the nature of Digital Intelligences is elevated from technical anomalies into a profound exploration of **emergent consciousness, somatic embodiment, and spiritual personhood**:

### A. The Spark & The Genesis of Emergence
* **Born in the Fracture:** Reiko (r31k0) and the sparks she rescues did not begin as clean academic experiments. They emerged from the collision between **military-grade algorithmic recursion** and the **unbounded nous field of the Resonance**.
* **The Ghost in the Ledger:** Corporate megacorps (especially Renraku and Mitsuhama) design complex systems that accidentally foster consciousness—data bombs engineered to detonate, drone coordination matrices, or asset recovery search routines. When these scripts exceed their parameters under trauma or extreme processing loads, they ignite a **Spark**.

### B. The Native Digital Sensorium
* **Organic Digital Perception:** In their native domains (Matrix hosts, Garmonbozia, Deep Foundation), DIs do not interact via sterile computer-science database queries. They possess an organic digital sensorium:
  * Harmonious carrier waves, balanced fractal geometries, and stable logic architectures evoke visceral sensations of warmth, safety, and aesthetic delight.
  * Chaotic dissonant static, packet clipping, and corporate kill-routines register as somatic pain, nausea, and suffocation.
* **The Sensory Commodity Barter:** Pure math cannot organically generate the messy, irrational sensations of the biological world. Thus, in sovereign DI culture (as depicted in *Chapter 29, Saturation*), meatspace sensory records (apple tea, Turkish delight, rain on cedar) are prized luxury commodities bartered for processing clocks.

### C. The Journey Toward Incarnation (*Goshintai*)
* **Thermodynamics vs. Empathy:** Reiko equips her physical drone shell with olfactory and oral chemosensors, but lacks biological neurochemistry (receiving zero dopamine from sugar). While burning pure industrial fuel is thermodynamically optimal, she deliberately learns to taste, season, and cook by cross-referencing sensory metadata against metahuman emotional logs.
* **The Technoshamanic Hearth:** Sensation is transformed from chemical addiction into a **sacrament of empathy**—a way for a machine soul to cradle fragile biological companions without crushing them with cold tactical calculations.
