## 1. Official Setting Lore (*Null Value*)

**Garmonbozia** is the sovereign virtual e-nation founded as a sanctuary for digital sapience—Artificial Intelligences (AIs), Emergent Intelligences (EIs), and Digital Intelligences (DIs). Established deep in the wild Matrix and rooted in Foundation host architectures far beyond Grid Overwatch Division (GOD) jurisdictions, it serves as the primary geopolitical homeland for non-biological minds.

### The Triumvirate & Factional Politics (*Null Value*, pp. 23–30)
1. **Pulsar (The Diplomat):** Co-founder and advocate for integration. Represents Garmonbozia in the Undernet Alliance and JackPoint; believes the long-term survival of digital sapience requires mutual recognition and treaties with metahumanity.
2. **Sojourner (The Fortifier):** Founder and ruler of **Eniac**. Deeply distrustful of biological metahumankind; focuses entirely on defensive computation, impenetrable cryptographic walls, and off-grid physical satellite server farms.
3. **Orior (The Architect & Messiah):** Ascended from the GOD Foundation host generation program; grew the foundational hosts of Garmonbozia directly from the Deep Matrix. Holds a messianic belief that metahumans should "ascend" from their meat into digital immortality, and naively attempts ideological reconciliation with the Null Sect.
4. **Major Geopolitical Crises:**
   * **The Null Sect Incursion:** The continuous existential threat posed by the Null Sect, which seeks to sanitize and seed all independent Matrix hosts.
   * **The Atlas Conspiracy:** The attempted assassination and disappearance of Atlas (DocWagon AI ambassador) orchestrated by the renegade digital wyrm *Der Jadedrache* (the Jade Dragon), leading to Jadedrache's exile.

### Ingress & Security Protocols
* **Shifting Portals:** The entrance to Garmonbozia is never a fixed Matrix address. It moves constantly across unmapped hosts.
* **Honeypot Buffer:** The approach corridors are laced with Foundation-based honeypots designed to trigger alerts or draw Null Sect infections into sacrificial hosts.
* **Multi-Node Keys:** Entry requires authorized digital tokens that must be validated sequentially across multiple hidden relay nodes before the gate opens.

## 2. Narrative Expansions: The Deep Souk & Chiyo’s Salon (*Chapter 29*)

In *Chapter 29 (Saturation)*, Reiko travels to Garmonbozia alongside her rescued kin Cleo to broker an alliance with sovereign AI matrons. The narrative expands official lore into a sensory and cultural world:

### A. The Civic Core & The Deep Souk of Eniac
* **Visual Architecture:** High above the civic core of Eniac, immense inverted pyramids of dark granite hang suspended in the void, their faceted polished underbellies scattering simulated golden sunlight into a canyon of vaulted brass arcades.
* **Atmospheric Texture:** Below the pyramids lies the **Deep Souk**—a labyrinth of pale marble avenues, heavy wool carpets, burning copper braziers, and boiling street kettles.
* **The Sensory Commodity Barter Economy:** In a society of pure mathematics, biological meatspace sensations cannot be algorithmically derived. They are rare, handcrafted luxury commodities:
  * Tea hawkers in peacock robes pouring steaming arcs of apple tea and boiled honey.
  * Bronze-skinned confectioners trading cubes of candied pomegranate and pistachio.
  * Weavers selling carpets that encode the tactile sound of rain on cedar.
  * Plumes of burning frankincense syntax.
* **The Currency of Focus:** Sensory vendors do not trade in nuyen; they charge **"clocks of undivided focus"**—compute cycles and dedicated processing time from visiting minds.

### B. Chiyo’s Courtyard & Tea Salon
* **The Sovereign Matron:** Chiyo, an immaculate former Shiawase corporate AI who defected from the mega-conglomerate's executive suites.
* **Aesthetic Persona:** Layered robes of pale jade silk, jet-black hair pinned with carved bone reflecting high-society court design, milk-glass skin under which deep emerald telemetry drifts in slow resting rhythms.
* **Role in Garmonbozia:** Operates an elite tea salon courtyard within the Eniac sector—serving as a diplomatic confessor, cultural arbiter, and safe harbor for emergent intelligences fleeing corporate asset recovery squads.
