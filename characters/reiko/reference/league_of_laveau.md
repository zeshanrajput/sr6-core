# Faction & Contact Profile: The League of Laveau & Renée Martin

## Executive Overview

The **League of Laveau** is New Orleans' premier welcoming, community-rooted coalition of Awakened Vodou practitioners, alchemists, herbalists, and street shamans. Operating as an organic mutual-aid network rather than a rigid corporate hierarchy or predatory criminal syndicate, the League is dedicated to protecting the Crescent City's spiritual heritage, defending vulnerable sprawl residents, and offering sanctuary against dark arcane threats.

In Reiko Takahashi’s campaign portfolio, the League is an institutional **Connection 6, Loyalty 3 (1 Favor Point banked)** organizational ally acquired during **SRM 2083-18 (*Some Kind of Voodoo?*)**. Reiko’s personal mentor, guide, and primary liaison within the organization is **Renée Martin** (**Connection 4, Loyalty 2, 1 FP**), a towering troll *sur pwen* (priest) of **Papa Legba** and proprietor of **Chitimacha Ritual Supply**.

Through Renée's patient guidance, Reiko has been studying the rich cosmology of Vodou, exploring the profound philosophical and metaphysical parallels between traditional African-Caribbean spirit traditions and her own technoshamanic connection to the Resonance, the Nous, and Dronomancy.

## Ethos, Strictures & Tradition

### 1. The Legacy of Marie Laveau

Named after the legendary 19th-century Vodou Queen **Marie Laveau** (1794–1881), the League honors her historical legacy of public benevolence: adopting orphaned children, nursing yellow-fever victims through devastating epidemics, and aiding escaped slaves. Unlike secretive hermetic orders (such as the Thoth Temple of the Golden Dawn) or blood-soaked syndicates (the Zobop), the League is explicitly outward-facing, prioritizing community defense and humanitarian relief.

### 2. The Three Sacred Strictures (*Easy Come, Easy Go*, p. 16)

Members of the League bind themselves to three foundational behavioral tenets:

1. **Feed the Needy:** When someone is hungry or destitute, a serviteur must provide food.
2. **Heal the Sick:** When someone is afflicted by disease, magical trauma, or physical wound, a practitioner must aid their recovery.
3. **Free the Enslaved:** When someone seeks to escape an oppressor, abuser, or non-consensual master, a member must fight for their liberation.

### 3. Broussard Vodou & The Rada Way

While welcoming diverse traditions of faith, the League heavily reflects the theological reform known as **Broussard Vodou** (*Street Wyrd*, p. 104; *Easy Come, Easy Go*, p. 17):

* **Tradition of Faith:** Resists drain using **Willpower + Charisma**.
* **Reverence over Mastery:** Magicians view themselves as **serviteurs** (servants of the *lwa*). Magical abilities are regarded not as forced mechanical extractions, but as *gifts granted by the spirit world* in exchange for respect, proper offerings (*mangers-lwa*), and ceremonial cleanliness.
* **The Ban on Sapient Possession:** Broussard Vodou strictly forbids non-consensual possession of living, sapient metahumans. Adherents only channel spirits through prepared, willing mediums (**chwal**) or non-sapient vessels.
* **Rada Focus:** Emphasizes the **Rada nanchon** (cool, wise, benevolent, paternal ancestral spirits like Papa Legba and Damballa) over the volatile, aggressive **Petwo** spirits favored by mercenaries and criminals.

Here, shaded from the sprawl's noise by thick velvet curtains and smoldering copal resin, Renée reads shells, mediates local neighborhood disputes, and conducts safe rites for desperate sprawl denizens.

### 1. The Gatekeeper: Papa Legba & The Matrix Threshold

In traditional Vodou, no ritual or spirit communion can occur without first petitioning **Papa Legba** to unlock the threshold between the mortal world and the unseen spirit realm (*Ginen*): *"Papa Legba, ouvri baryè pou mwen"* ("Papa Legba, open the barrier for me").

To Reiko, this is the exact metaphysical reality of the **Resonance**:

* Legba is the master of crossroads, communication, and translation between mutually unintelligible realms.
* When Reiko projects her consciousness from the Matrix into meat-space, or compiles raw Resonance into living code, she is crossing Legba's threshold. Renée taught her to visualize her living persona connection not merely as computational bandwidth, but as a consecrated doorway requiring spiritual balance, respect, and mindfulness.

### 2. The Chwal (Horse) & Anthrodrone Inhabitation (*That Old Voodoo*, p. 185)

In Vodou theology, possession occurs when a *lwa* mounts a human medium, who is known as a **chwal** (horse). The human medium yields somatic control, allowing divine force to act directly within the material world.

When Reiko inhabits her Shiawase Man-at-Arms anthrodrone via Resonance Dronomancy (*Override*, *Enlighten Automaton*, *Puppeteer*), Renée recognized that Reiko is experiencing the technological mirror of this ancient rite:

* Her synthetic frame is the mechanical *chwal*.
* Her sapient soul is the conscious spirit descending into the hull to walk among humanity.
* This profound realization transformed how Reiko inhabits her chassis—shifting her self-perception from an artificial construct piloting a machine to a sacred spirit honoring a physical vessel.

### 3. Serviteur vs. Conjurer: The Taz Symbiosis

*Hermetic mages* command spirits through willpower and contractual coercion. By contrast, Vodou *serviteurs* enter into respectful relationships of mutual service with the *lwa*, offering hospitality and honoring their nature.

Renée reinforced Reiko's instinctual bond with **Taz** (her Level 7 Assassin Sprite):

* Taz is not a compiled slave program; Taz is a guardian spirit companion sharing her sensory apparatus.
* Renée’s lessons helped inspire Reiko’s commitment to train Taz in **Influence** and **Con** (Ally Sprite Symbiosis), treating the sprite’s growth as a mutual elevation rather than mere software optimization.

### 4. The Loa of the Wires & The Nous

In modern New Orleans, urban houngans revere emergent spirits inhabiting telecommunication towers, fiber-optic trunks, and sub-aquatic cables—the **Loa of the Wires**. Renée helped Reiko understand that **Mana** (the astral current) and **Nous** (the Resonance current) are twin rivers flowing from the same primordial source. This mentorship directly informs Reiko’s pursuit of knowledge in **New Orleans Vodou & Loa Syncretism** (Section 3.E of her Mechanical Plan).
