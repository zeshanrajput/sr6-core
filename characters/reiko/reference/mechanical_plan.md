### Drone Chassis Upgrades (Shiawase Man-at-Arms)

#### 1. Second Cyberarm & Internal Nanohive Architecture

To integrate an internal nanohive into her custom Shiawase Man-at-Arms anthrodrone (matching the narrative developments from Chapter 25 *DelTree*), Reiko requires a second cyberarm to supply the necessary capacity:

- **Used Synthetic Cyberarm (Left)**
  - **Base Capacity**: 8
  - **Estimated Cost**: 10,000¥ (Used/Omegaware 50% discount)
  - **Function**: Mirrors her existing Right Cyberarm (which houses her Tesla Coil), providing dedicated internal chassis capacity to host her nanite infrastructure.

- **Nanohive (Rating 6)**
  - **Capacity Cost**: [6 Cap] (Installed directly inside the Left Cyberarm; consumes capacity only)
  - **Cost**: 45,000¥ (Base 90,000¥; 50% used cyberware )
  - **Function**: Internal biomechanical/nanite incubator. Manages up to 6 nanite colonies, supports up to 18 Nanite Volume (NV), and replenishes depleted nanites at a rate of 6 NV per hour.

- **Hunter Nanites**
  - **Colony Type**: Defensive Hunter / Counter-Intrusion Strain ("Leukocytes")
  - **Function**: Deployed inside the chassis to actively detect, isolate, consume, and dismantle foreign nanite swarms, pathogens, and parasitic micro-systems before they penetrate deeper actuators or core processors.

- **Tech Infestation**
  - **Strain / System**: Aggressive counter-hardware nanite payload
  - **Function**: Deployed offensively or counter-intrusively to break down, disrupt, and corrupt enemy electronics, drones, cyberware, and host systems through physical-layer sabotage.

#### 2. Power & Engine Modifications

- **Hydrocarbon Fuel Converter**
  - **Capacity / Category**: `[2 Powertrain Capacity]` (*Double Clutch*, p. 124)
  - **Cost**: 7,500¥ (Base: Body 10 × 1,500¥ = 15,000¥; 50% self-install discount per DC p. 120)
  - **Placement & Flavor**: Installed at the base of the anthrodrone's esophagus, linking her oral chemoreceptors and digestive tract directly to the chassis auxiliary power cell.
  - **Function**: Enables the internal power plant to run on anything organic (food, alcohol, bio-matter, sugar, plant matter, standard hydrocarbon fuels). Provides indefinite metabolic/operational endurance in deep-bayou or rural environments away from electrical charging grids while allowing her to physically consume real food and drink as actual fuel.
