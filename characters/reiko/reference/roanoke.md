# Roanoke: Character Profile

## Overview

Roanoke is an elven Ms. Johnson and anti-corporate activist operating in New Orleans, known for her intense ideological focus, disregard for collateral damage, and protective loyalty toward her allies.1Physical Appearance

Metatype & Frame: Female elf of petite build with sun-damaged skin.1

Attire: Pragmatic street gear consisting of a dark green t-shirt, brown cargo pants, and black combat boots.1

Personality & Disposition

Anti-Corporate Fanaticism: Described by local fixer Lanyap as an anti-corporate fanatic who generally shows little concern for money or collateral damage.1

Direct & No-Nonsense: Conducts business with immediate focus, skipping preambles or small talk during meetings.1

Loyal & Protective: Demonstrates strong personal loyalty to her friends. Following the burglary of Cedar's talismonger shop, Roanoke secured a bayou safehouse for Cedar and hired shadowrunners out of her own funds to recover the stolen items.1

Abilities & Network

Negotiation & Social Expertise: A formidable negotiator with a high level of social influence (Social Rating 7; Influence & Negotiation pool of 14).1

Spirit Allies: Holds strong connections with local toxic free spirits. If attacked, eight spirits will come to her defense, including four Force 5 toxic earth spirits (spirits of barrens) and four Force 6 toxic water spirits (spirits of pollution).1

Appearances & Campaign Role

Featured Mission: SRM 2083-14: Deepest Roots.1

Role: Primary Ms. Johnson hiring the runner team for 6,000 to 7,000 nuyen per runner to retrieve two stolen foci (Saint Cuthbert's rib bone fragment and Josephine de Beauharnais's handheld mirror).1

Primary Meeting Location: Plantation Greens Vegan Restaurant in Marrero, located within the Free West Bank (Security Zone Z).

Plantation Greens is described in the document as follows:

* **Location:** Marrero, New Orleans (Free West Bank, Security Zone Z).
* **Type & Atmosphere:** A small, casual, low-key vegan restaurant.
* **Ownership:** Owned and operated by Jose Madrigal and his wife, Bianca.
* **Hours:** Open only on weekdays from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
* **Reputation:** Popular lunch spot for New Orleans residents due to high-quality food and organic fruit smoothies rated as the best in the area.
* **Neighborhood Context:** Located in an area populated by down-on-their-luck locals, adjacent to bayou land saturated with toxic runoff—described as the closest thing New Orleans has to a barrens.

## Relevance to Reiko

Based on the combination of loyalty rating and favor points, Roanoke is among the top 'friends' or acquaintences for Reiko. Roanoke is potentially closer to Reiko than other such notable characters like Indomitable Will or Brynne.

Much of this comes from the initial character creation purchase of Roanoke as a high loyalty contact, but more has occurred in game play to solidify this.

* SRM.2083.17 - Reiko helped Roanoke by taking a canister of spirit infested toxic waste and introducing it to a target's air circulation system. Roanoke really enjoyed the sequellae.
* SRM.2083.20 - The Ghoul of Gentilly had actually kidnapped Roanoke **and** Cedar. In the final scene, Reiko rescued them both and protected them from harm with her own body while her team eliminated threats and got everyone out.
* SRM.2083.14 - The rest of the team was making Cedar uncomfortable with their legwork questions, and Cedar quietly asked Reiko for help. Reiko actually stayed with Cedar and asked her team to go on to the next legwork location. This resulted in me (the player) missing the next (combat) scene because Reiko was with Cedar making tea. Then when Reiko left to go help with the investigation, Oliver kidnapped Cedar. Reiko flew at top speed, leaving her team behind to try and rescue Cedar. She would have gotten their first if not for the nanite infestation, which she triggered the events remarked on in 25_DelTree. This compassion is why Cedar (and potentially Roanoke) uniquely value Reiko's contributions (the additional favor points in the log).
