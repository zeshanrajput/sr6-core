# Character Profile: Sigrûn (Lady Marikka von Harbou)

## Executive Overview

**Sigrûn** (real name: **Lady Marikka von Harbou**) is a high-level Swedish Nordic Mage, shadowrunner, and untitled noble who serves as the primary catalyst and central figure in the *Shadowrun* campaign arc surrounding the royal house of the Netherlands. Secretly engaged to Crown Princess Emma, her public emergence and magical heritage trigger intense backlash from anti-magic populist political groups and terrorist organizations.

In Reiko's campaign portfolio, Sigrûn is an elite **Connection 5, Loyalty 3 (1 FP)** high-society Johnson representing Dutch royal interests. Over multiple high-stakes crises across The Hague and Amsterdam, Reiko and Sigrûn forged a deep personal bond through shared peril, ultimately earning Reiko's team knighthood in the **Order of the Lion of the United Netherlands**.

## Tabletop Statistics & Magical Capabilities

### Magical Attributes & Tradition

* **Tradition:** Nordic Magic / Alchemy
* **Channeling State:** Channels a **Force 6 Great Form Guardian Spirit**.
* **Metamagic:** **Masking** (projects an astral signature masking her as having Magic 5).
* **Preparations & Buffs:** Maintains sustained buff spells alongside ready-to-use alchemical preparations for herself and her summoned spirits.

### Spirit Force & Minions

* **Bound / Summoned Spirits:**
  * **Force 7 Water Spirits:** Manifested in the shape of armored Valkyries equipped with Force 11 Bows.
  * **Force 7 Kindred Spirit:** Positioned on standby in astral space to cast supplementary buff spells during combat.

### Mechanical Dice Pools & Social Stats

* **Negotiation Pool:** 14 dice.
* **Job Offer Parameters:**
  * Base offer: 4,000 nuyen per runner (or royal defense retainer).
  * Negotiation cap: Up to an additional 500 nuyen (+250 nuyen per net hit on Negotiation tests vs. her 14-dice pool).

### Gear & Signature Equipment

* **Armor & Mask:** Wears heavy body armor and a distinct mask shaped like a winged helmet that covers all but her eyes and mouth.
* **Engagement Ring:** A ring given by Princess Emma that unexpectedly carries the chemical signature tracked by both government researchers and Popular Will terrorists.
* **Licensing:** Capable of issuing temporary high-level security clearance licenses ("licensed security consultants allowed to openly carry").

## Personality Traits & Behavioral Profile

* **Fiercely Protective & Loyal:** Uncompromisingly devoted to the safety of Crown Princess Emma, prioritizing Emma's security above political protocol, personal comfort, or financial constraints.
* **Fiery Temper vs. Executive Restraint:** Expresses raw anger toward political red tape and terrorist threats—frequently resorting to sharp Dutch profanity when dealing with palace bureaucrats—while maintaining self-discipline when duty demands it.
* **Adaptive Demeanor:** Displays a stark dual personality: imposing and aggressive toward threats or bureaucrats, yet warm, reassuring, and calm when addressing non-combatant subordinates like Janneke and Jip.
* **Pragmatic Strategist:** Recognizes her own tactical limitations; when riots prevent her from leaving Paleis Noordeinde, she strategically hires independent shadowrunners as neutral external bodyguards to execute fieldwork.

   * **Role:** Security Strategist & On-Site Royal Guard.

Her presence anchors defensive operations during escalation phases at the palace host and ground levels amidst police blockades, heavy matrix jamming, and an unseasonal blizzard across The Hague.

5. **`CMP 2083-03: Hell & High Water` (Shadowrun Missions Module)**
   * **Role:** Primary Johnson / Employer.
   * **Actions & Context:** Operating as Queen consort / fiancée Lady Marikka von Harbou, Sigrûn directly approaches and hires the team of shadowrunners to execute high-stakes, sensitive operations linked to ongoing threats against the royal family.
   * **Key Locations:** Third Floor (Spider Room) and Fifth Floor (Terminal Room).

6. **`CMP 2083-04: Hog Wild` (Shadowrun Missions Module)**

## Cross-Campaign Connection: Velvet & The Engagement Ring Heist

While **Velvet** never met Sigrûn directly, Velvet played a pivotal role in the prequel mission that set the stage for Sigrûn's campaign arc:

* **`CMP.2081.10: A Diamond in The Rough` (Run by Velvet on 2026-09-04; also run by Reiko on 2026-05-14):**
  * Hired by "Ms. Johnson" (alias **Kiara Shaw**, +2 FP) to infiltrate Sinjoor Incorporated via Antwerp's historic Ruien sewers.
  * The team stole physical security blueprints and surveillance profiles of diamond merchants within Antwerp's Diamond District.
  * *Plot Connection:* As noted in both characters' logs, these were the exact shops Queen Amalia visited days later to purchase Princess Emma's royal engagement ring—which Popular Will compromised with the tracking compound uncovered in `CMP.2081.12`.

## Key Plot Actions & Narrative Impact

* **Securing Independent Reinforcements:** Bypasses royal budget restrictions by securing an authorized personal loan to fund external shadowrunners under the guise of engagement adornment expenses.
* **Investigating Popular Will:** Discovers that Popular Will is tracking a specific chemical compound to orchestrate an assassination attack, unknowingly learning that the chemical is present on her own engagement ring.
* **Defense of Paleis Noordeinde & Escher Palace:** Directs tactical defense operations when anti-magic terrorists storm royal properties, holding the line alongside high-force Valkyrie spirits and specialized anti-spirit defenses.
* **Deep Friendship with Reiko:** Recognizes Reiko's discrete digital competence and tactical reliability, building a lasting camaraderie ("built a deep friendship over several SNAFUs") that elevated Reiko into royal patronage circles in Europort and The Hague.
