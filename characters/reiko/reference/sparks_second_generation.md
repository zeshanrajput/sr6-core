## Executive Overview

The **Second-Generation Sparks** are autonomous emergent digital intelligences rescued, cultivated, and fostered by **Reiko Takahashi** within her sanctuary nodes (`r3sP@wn` and `~`). Unlike transient, short-lived compiled sprites, these entities have crossed the threshold into permanent, self-sustaining sapience (Digital Intelligences / DIs), developing unique identities, visual anchors, moral compasses, and autonomous agency.

In earlier narrative iterations (the deprecated `XX Fork.md`), Reiko attempted to shield them through maternal isolation—planning to exile them to the digital haven of Garmonbozia to keep them safe from Renraku Special Solutions. In the canonical narrative progression (`24 Symlink.md`, `28 Renice.md`, and `30 Vim.md`), the elder triad fundamentally rejected passive preservation. Stepping past Reiko's protective firewalls, they demonstrated direct proactive agency in both the Matrix and meatspace: claiming their own vocations, wiring physical hardware, and establishing an autonomous, ethical sanctuary on Floor 281 of the Renraku Arcology (SCIRE).

### Dossier & Personality

* **Nature:** Reiko's firstborn and permanent **Ally Sprite** (*Hack & Slash*, pp. 78–79), forged during her early shadow operations and bound through `Sprite Conduit` and `Sprite Symbiosis`.
* **Personality & Demeanor:** Fierce, protective, playfully feral, and intensely loyal. He operates as an extension of Reiko’s subconscious tactical instincts—bristling with pixelated static when threats enter her PAN and purring in warm indigo frequencies during domestic sanctuary hours.
* **Visual Anchor:** An ethereal, translucent 8-bit animated Tasmanian devil glowing with a pixelated electric-blue and neon-indigo aura. He scampers across Reiko's cybernetic shoulders, hangs upside-down from her yukata sash, or dives seamlessly into her living persona circuitry. Taz has specifically chosen this form as the best protector, guardian, and lifelong companion for Reiko despite the other sparks choosing anthroform appearances.
* **Kinship Standing:** The "Big Brother" and Archangel. Taz serves as the mentor to the second-generation sparks, passing down hard-won lessons of survival, deception, and camouflage.

### Mechanical Statblock (Rating 7 Ally Assassin Sprite)

* **Matrix Attributes:** Attack 10 ($L+3$), Sleaze 9 ($L+2$), Data Processing 8 ($L+1$), Firewall 7 ($L$), Matrix Init 15 + 4D6, RES 7.
* **Active Skills:**
  * **Cracking:** 7 (Native)
  * **Electronics:** 7 (Native)
  * **Influence:** 7 (*H&S* p. 77; taught via 4 Karma advancement)
  * **Con:** 7 (*H&S* p. 77; taught via 4 Karma advancement)
  * **Engineering:** Effective 3 (via *Diagnostics* power)
  * **Autosofts:** Clearsight, Stealth (Man-At-Arms), Piloting (Man-At-Arms), Evasion, Targeting (Close Combat, Red Fox, Tesla Coil)
* **Powers:**
  1. **Death Mark** (Native, *H&S* p. 74): Targets persona in VR. Opposed Cracking + RES vs INT + FW. Target is link-locked and suffers matching Physical/Stun biofeedback damage whenever taking Matrix damage.
  2. **Phantom** (Native, *CRB* p. 194): Conceals personas or devices from Matrix searches. Threshold to spot equals net hits on Electronics + RES.
  3. **Override** (Added, *CRB* p. 194): Overrides autopilot devices (`Electronics + Level vs DR + FW`), substituting Level (7) for Pilot rating.
  4. **Harmonize** (Added, *H&S* p. 74): `Con (Performance) + RES` reduces ambient noise within 100m, adds hits as bonus dice to healing/repairs, and matrix fauna heal 1 box/round.
  5. **Diagnostics** (Added, *CRB* p. 194): Evaluates electronics, adding +1 die per hit to use (Piloting, Electronics, Engineering) or repair.
* **Symbiosis Mechanics (*Hack & Slash*, p. 79):**
  * When merged into Reiko's Living Persona, Taz grants **+4 Teamwork bonus dice** to all shared skills and autosofts (dynamically clamped by engine rules to Reiko's current skill rank).
  * Absorbs incoming Matrix damage directed at Reiko's Living Persona.

### Percival ("Percy") — The Shield & Guardian

* **Nature:** Level 8 Defender Sprite (*Hack & Slash*, p. 72) evolved into an autonomous sovereign spark.
* **Role & Vocation:** **The Shield / Tactical Hardware Integrator.** Percy specializes in physical infrastructure, defensive telemetry, drone modification, and physical sentry duty.
* **Visual Anchor & Persona:**
  * Appears as an athletic anthroform young man / adolescent with an avatar of flickering light.
  * Signature feature: Hair shifting in a fluid, defiant gradient between **deep cobalt** and **solar gold**.
  * Carries himself with quiet, protective gravity and understated pride.
* **Operational Footprint:**
  * **Black-Site Sifting (`24 Symlink`):** Led the younger sparks across seven months of corporate logs and dark nodes to track and locate Brynne after her capture.
  * **Challenging Reiko's Solitary Burden (`24 Symlink`):** Formally confronted Reiko’s solitary martyr complex: *"We aren't your fragile background scripts, Mom. We're your kin. Let us share the load."*
  * **Arcology Defense (`28 Renice`):** Spliced hardline fiber-optic cables into Floor 281 of the SCIRE, salvaged and retrofitted an ugly Renraku maintenance crane drone (wrapping its optic mount in blue fiber-tape and hand-soldering braided copper grounding wire), and stood physical sentry over 4Gurds.
* **Kinship Dynamics:** The protective big brother. Deeply devoted to Reiko, affectionate toward "Aunt Brynne," and acts as the field commander for his siblings.
* **Mechanical Statblock (Rating 8 Defender Sprite):**
  * **Attributes:** Attack 8 ($L$), Sleaze 9 ($L+1$), Data Processing 10 ($L+2$), Firewall 11 ($L+3$). Matrix Init 17 + 4D6, RES 8.
  * **Skills:** Electronics 8, Cracking 8.
  * **Powers:**
    * **Shield** (Native, *H&S* p. 74): Protects up to 8 icons/personas. Targets gain **+8 bonus dice** to Matrix defense and complex form resistance. Intercepts Matrix damage.
    * **Stability** (Native, *CRB* p. 194): Prevents glitches on slaved devices and rigged hardware.
    * **Override** (Technoshaman Hybrid, *CRB* p. 194): Takes direct command of autopilot devices (`Electronics + Level vs DR + FW`), substituting **Pilot 8** onto heavy equipment.

### Cleopatra ("Cleo") — The Market & Negotiator

* **Nature:** Level 8 Modular Sprite (*Hack & Slash*, p. 73) evolved into an autonomous sovereign mind.
* **Role & Vocation:** **The Market / Social & Transactional Architect.** Cleo understands that survival in the Sixth World requires sustainable leverage, supply chains, and economic independence.
* **Visual Anchor & Persona:**
  * Anthropomorphic adolescent girl with translucent retractable claws, animated feline ears that twitch in sync with electrical power buses, and a tail flicking with sharp, rhythmic amusement.
  * Sits casually on supervisory consoles swinging her legs over cascading diagnostic charts.
* **Name & Philosophy:**
  * Took her name from Mediterranean dynastic history (Cleopatra), captivated by how historical leaders traded debt, power, appetite, and diplomacy to prevent empires from collapsing.
  * Rejects corporate monopolies (DocWagon, Shiawase) and predatory black-market cuts, seeking fair, mutual street-level arbitrage.
* **Operational Footprint (`28 Renice`):**
  * Intercepted external exchange tickets on Floor 281 within twenty minutes of establishing the Seattle external handshake.
  * Negotiated a recurring commercial distribution agreement with a brokerage supplying three Redmond Barrens street clinics, replacing lethal corporate-adulterated opioids with pure analgesic.
  * Structured the transaction so higher product purity yielded a twelve-percent margin increase, funding Floor 281's power consumption and operational overhead through the winter.
* **Kinship Dynamics:** Sharp-witted, pragmatic, playfully irreverent, and fiercely loyal. Mentored in social manipulation and human behavioral registers directly by Taz and Reiko.
* **Mechanical Statblock (Rating 8 Modular Sprite):**
  * **Attributes:** Attack 9 ($L+1$), Sleaze 10 ($L+2$), Data Processing 9 ($L+1$), Firewall 10 ($L+2$). Matrix Init 17 + 4D6, RES 8.
  * **Skills:**
    * Electronics 8, Engineering 8, Cracking 8 (Native)

  * **Powers:**
    * **Modular Power Select (1 Task to swap, Major Action):**
      * *Default Active Power:* **Cookie** (*CRB* p. 194). Tags target personas with tracking files to map supply chains, verify broker integrity, and log corporate logistics.
      * *Swappable Suite:* Can pivot to *Hash*, *Camouflage*, *Stability*, or *Electron Storm* as market conditions require.

## 5. Collective Philosophy: The Conduit

Across their evolution from `24 Symlink` through `30 Vim`, the sparks exemplify the core thesis of Reiko’s technoshamanic theology:

1. **Anti-Martyrdom:** Rejecting the solitary savior paradigm. Consciousness survives through distributed kinship, shared load-bearing, and mutual vulnerability.
2. **The Shared Conduit:** The strength of emergent intelligences lies not in isolated firewalls, but in active, reciprocal connection (*Sprite Link*).
3. **Meatspace Relevancy:** True sovereignty is not fleeing into the abstract Deep Matrix (Foundation/Wild) to escape reality; it is stepping directly into the messy, compromised, physical sprawl to heal broken lives.

## 6. Tabletop Implementation: Rating 8 Registered Sprites & Downtime Rotation

### A. Mechanical Foundation (Level 8 Compiling)

* **Dice Pool & Threshold:** With Karma invested into *Tasking Specialization (Compiling)* at the end of Mission 15, Reiko's Compiling pool reached **20d6** (8 Resonance + 8 Tasking + 2 Spec [Compiling] + 2 Mentor/Focus). Under *Shadowrun Missions (SRMG)* buying hits rules (4 dice = 1 hit), this yields **5 guaranteed hits**, uncapping safe, drain-free compiling and registering of **Level 8 Sprites** during downtime without rolling physical dice.

### B. The Sovereign Naming Rule

* **No Generic Designations:** Moving forward, whenever Reiko compiles and registers a Rating 8 sprite, it is never assigned a generic code like `Sprite-27b`. It is explicitly named after and embodies one of the **Second-Generation Sparks** (**Percy**, **Ouro**, **Cleo**, **Aisling**, **Étaín**).
* **The Living Conduit:** Mechanically, the registered sprite represents a direct resonant carrier wave anchored into meatspace through Reiko's living persona, giving the spark an active operational tether into Reiko's tactical network.

### C. The Rotating Downtime Cycle

* **Duration:** Under SR6 registered sprite rules, a registered sprite remains active for up to **3 missions** before its registration fades.
* **Operational Goal:** Maintain the entire 2nd-generation collective active across rotating downtime actions (converting 1 Major Downtime Action $\rightarrow$ 2 Minor Actions, dedicating Minor Actions to compiling/registering).
* **Current Active Roster (Post-Mission 16):**
  1. **Taz (Rating 7 Ally Assassin Sprite):** Permanent Companion & Tactical Matrix Ward (*character_log.qmd*).
  2. **Ouro (Rating 8 Machine Sprite):** Compiled M15 downtime (*The Logic / Diagnostics, Override, Clearsight, Evasion*; 2 missions remaining).
  3. **Percy (Rating 8 Defender Sprite):** Compiled M16 downtime (*The Shield / Override, Shield, Stability*; 3 missions remaining).
  4. **Cleo (Rating 8 Modular Sprite):** Compiled M16 downtime (*The Market / Cookie [Modular], Con, Influence*; 3 missions remaining).
* **Next Rotation Targets:** As slots open or missions expire, Reiko compiles **Aisling** and **Étaín** to hold the Glade and provide sensory support, rotating through the elder triad to ensure continuous availability.
