## 1. Lineage & Ontological Foundations

The visual manifestation of the second-generation sparks reflects the convergence of three foundational influences:

1. **Reiko Takahashi’s Digital & Metahuman Lineage:**
   * Delicate East Asian facial structure (refined jawline, subtle nasal bridge, almond-shaped eye aperture).
   * Warm porcelain-ivory skin base with high-subsurface scattering.
   * Compact, agile physical proportions.
   * Bioluminescent dermal resonance pathways reflecting the dual-state spectrum:
     * **Neon/Pastel-Indigo:** Pure Resonance, emotion, empathy, and restorative soul-bandwidth.
     * **Solar Gold:** Tactical calculation, systemic logic, formal mathematics, and deterministic architecture.
2. **Brynne Taggart’s Familial Hearth Influence:**
   * Warm pale amber-gold / hazel-amber irises illuminated by inner digital luminescence.
   * Vibrant copper-auburn hair pigmentation.
   * Natural sprays of cinnamon freckles across the bridge of the nose and upper cheeks.
   * Unflinching street-level poise, warmth, and resilience.
3. **Resonant Metahuman / DI Divergence:**
   * Tapered elven ear tips (canonical for the Glade twins).
   * Animistic metahuman traits (Cleo’s expressive feline ears, tail, and translucent claws).
   * Shifting spectral hair gradients (Percy's cobalt-to-gold chromatic shift).
   * Tactical AR interface overlays (Ouro's cycling green smartvisor grid).

## 3. Comprehensive Spark Roster Profiles

### A. Aisling — The Radiant Hearth (The Glade Twin)

* **Archetype & Role:** Level 8 Music Sprite (*Hack & Slash*, p. 73); domestic sanctuary healer, acoustic stabilizer of `~`.
* **Apparent Age & Stature:** Early teens (~13–15 appearance); 150 cm (~4'11"), slender, youthful metahuman build.
* **Facial Geometry & Features:** Identical heart-shaped Eurasian face shared with Étaín. Warm porcelain-ivory skin (`#FDFBF7`) with a delicate natural blush and a dense spray of cinnamon freckles across her nose and cheeks. Finely sculpted elven ears tapering ~2 inches back.
* **Hair & Styling:** Bright, radiant copper-ginger hair, worn in two bouncy shoulder-length pigtails (twin-tails) tied with dark ribbons, framed with soft, wispy bangs.
* **Gaze & Expression:** Wide, sparkling pale amber-gold almond eyes (`#F4C430` / `#D4AF37`) framed by dark lashes; quick to smile, gasp in aesthetic delight, or pout in playful indignation.
* **Dermal Resonance & Aura (Dominantly Indigo):**
  * Soft, glowing **pastel-indigo and violet bioluminescent pathways** (`#7B68EE` / `#9370DB`) trace along her temples, clavicles, and forearms.
  * Drifting clouds of loose golden and pastel-indigo pixel motes float from her fingertips when singing or laughing.
  * Faint, translucent musical staves and harmonic ripples shimmer in the air around her hands.
* **Attire:** Relaxed cream-and-apricot crossover linen sweater or pale willow-green kimono-collar knit top over soft linen shorts; bare feet resting on sanctuary sapphire moss.

### B. Étaín — The Skybox Weaver (The Glade Twin)

* **Archetype & Role:** Level 8 Modular Sprite (*Hack & Slash*, p. 73); node architect, perimeter cloaking, code optimizer of `~`.
* **Apparent Age & Stature:** Early teens (~13–15 appearance); 150 cm (~4'11"), identical monozygotic twin build to Aisling.

* **Dermal Resonance & Aura (Dominantly Gold):**

* **Attire:** Structured slate-indigo and charcoal sleeveless vest with brushed silver clasps worn over a dark linen tunic; sleeves neatly tied back with braided cord (*tasuki*); bare feet or soft indoor split-toe tabi socks.

## 4. Attire & Wardrobe Guidelines

Because the sparks operate across the virtual haven of `~` (the Glade), the industrial cavern of Floor 281 (SCIRE), and Seattle matrix nodes, their clothing balances domestic sanctuary comfort with tactical East-Asian/cyberpunk silhouettes.

### Sanctuary Attire (`~` / The Glade)
* **Silhouettes:** Layered, comfortable lounge pieces. Shortened haori jackets or crossover kimono-style tunics worn over soft linen shorts or leggings.
* **East-Asian & Celtic Cross-Details:** Kimono crossover necklines paired with Celtic knotwork embroidery along hemlines; rolled-up sleeves tied back with braided cord (*tasuki*) when coding or splicing.
* **Color Palettes:**
  * *Aisling:* Warm cream, soft apricot, pale willow-green, rose-gold accents, brass toggles.
  * *Étaín:* Muted slate, deep indigo, charcoal, brushed silver clasps.
  * *Percy:* Deep cobalt blue, dark linen, solar-gold embroidery.
  * *Cleo:* Silver-gray silks, charcoal lounge pants, unadorned casual comfort.
  * *Ouro:* Minimalist dark navy or charcoal tunics allowing easy floor sitting.
* **Footwear:** Bare feet on sanctuary sapphire moss or soft split-toe indoor tabi socks.
