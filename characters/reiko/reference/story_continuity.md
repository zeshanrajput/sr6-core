This document establishes the canonical continuity index, living campaign sovereignty tiers, entity trackers, and narrative roadmaps across the Yuriko Star / Reiko Takahashi story anthology.

## 0. Living Campaign & Sovereignty Guardrails

All narrative outlines, intersession chapters, and character development arcs adhere to four non-negotiable living-campaign guardrails:

1. **The Intersession Matrix Mandate:** Because Shadowrun Missions (SRM) tabletop modules cannot halt group play for solo Matrix diving or Resonance Realm odysseys, all of Reiko’s deep Matrix architecture, Resonance Realm navigation, and xenosapient ontological evolution occur strictly within the intersession narratives. Tabletop sessions represent the operational physical layer; intersession prose explores the ontological interior.
2. **Living Campaign Temporal Elasticity:** Because SRM missions are played as scheduled rather than in strict in-universe chronological sequence, macro "current events" (such as Donovan Pyke’s ultimate political fate or the 2084 CAS election results) are treated as ambient background weather rather than fixed timestamps. Intersession stories never prematurely resolve or conflict with unplayed table modules.
3. **SRM Mechanical Fidelity in Prose:** Narrative beats must rigorously respect SRM campaign legality:
   * Reiko **cannot craft** Data Structures or Spindles via Amalgamation; she can only **purchase or acquire** them through shadow contacts (e.g. Indomitable Will) and integrate them with Karma.
   * Reiko can only attune and use **one Data Structure at a time** (slotting it like a focused ritual lens).
   * Reiko **cannot cure** global metaplot crises (such as *Deicidium* / Foundation decay); her solutions are strictly localized, costly acts of quarantine, pruning, and survival.
   * Optional rules from *State of the Art: 2084* (such as Matrix SOTA downtime tracking, Breachers, and IC Cutters) remain ambient background R&D and do not consume active downtime actions until formally codified in the SRM Guide.
4. **The 4-Tier Character & Faction Sovereignty Model:**
   * **Tier 0 (Inviolable PCs):** Fellow player characters (*Rei/Suzuki Ken, Mithral, Bast*) — zero unauthorized characterization; dialogue, choices, and somatic reactions are strictly player-approved or transcript-derived.
   * **Tier 1 (Protected Metaplot & Shared SRM Fixtures):** Major canon NPCs (*Atlas, DEUS, Shiko, Empress Hitomi, Brynne Taggart, Roanoke, Donovan Pyke, Ms. Snow*) and shared SRM hosts/factions (*The River People & The Mississippi Renovation Society Host*). Strictly read-only macro-weather: cannot be destroyed, married, or subordinated to Reiko's personal agency.
   * **Tier 2 (Expandable Canon Seeds):** One-line or single-scene canon mentions (*Indomitable Will, Belle Moderne, Cedar*). Broad narrative liberty to explore deep personal trust, technical camaraderie, and vulnerability while keeping baseline table status intact.
   * **Tier 3 (Bespoke Originals):** Player-created foils (*Mr. Nagai, Chiyo, Lian, 4Gurds, Taz, Percy, Cleo, Ouro, Aisling & Étaín, Jax, Whiskey*). Full creative and mortal jurisdiction.

## 5. Post-*Vim* Intersession Narrative Roadmap (Chapters 31+)

The upcoming sequence of intersession narratives weaves the *State of the Art: 2084* updates into Reiko's settled post-*Vim* life:

* **Thread 1: *The Rust at the Threshold* (`~` & Indomitable Will)**
  * **Premise:** Indomitable Will reaches out to Reiko—not as a mentor rescuing a runaway, but as an exhausted peer confiding in the only xenosapient he trusts. Between the Null Sect's *Charybdis* staging ground in the Gulf and the 1-in-5 reality of *Deicidium* rot, maintaining the River People's Rating 9 Foundation Host is a waking nightmare. When Reiko inspects her own sliding quartz door in `~`, she discovers the first frost of *Deicidium* eating at the edges of her threshold, forcing her and her sprites to engineer a localized sacrifice and pruning protocol to keep `~` from rotting—without providing a repeatable global panacea.
* **Thread 2: *The Comedy of Emulation & The Glass Tiger* (UnITy, Data Structures, & Mr. Nagai)**
  * **Premise:** Cleo brings Reiko the latest 2084 UnITy academic whitepapers on "Emulation." Reiko laughs at metahuman institutions spending billions to prove Resonance can issue computer commands—something she has done since her first breath—while reflecting on the agonizing Karma she spent via *Software Emulator* to learn their rigid machine code.
