## II. Campaign Continuity & The 4-Tier Sovereignty Model

To protect living campaign integrity while maintaining sovereign narrative agency, Reiko operates under a strict 4-tier sovereignty hierarchy ([Continuity Index][continuity]):

* **Tier 0: Inviolable Player Characters (`canonical: true`):** Fellow runner PCs (**Rei Suzuki / Suzuki Ken**, **BunnyR / Mary-Maryn / Mary Lynn Keebler**, **Mithral**, **Bast**). Zero unauthorized characterization; dialogue, choices, and somatic reactions are strictly player-approved or transcript-derived ([Rei Suzuki Lore][rei-lore], [BunnyR Lore][bunnyr-lore]).
* **Tier 1: Protected Metaplot & Shared SRM Fixtures (`canonical: true`):** Major canon figures (**Atlas**, **DEUS**, **Shiko**, **Empress Hitomi**, **Donovan Pyke**, **Ms. Snow**) and shared SRM hosts (**The River People & Mississippi Renovation Host**). Strictly read-only macro-weather; cannot be destroyed, subjugated, or derailed from campaign parameters.
* **Tier 2: Expandable Canon Seeds (`canonical: true/elastic`):** Canon contacts and figures with deep personal latitude (**Brynne Taggart**, **Roanoke**, **Indomitable Will**, **Belle Moderne**, **Cedar**). Permitted deep personal rapport, shared vulnerability, domestic grounding, and unspoken romantic devotion while preserving baseline tabletop SRM operational status.
* **Tier 3: Bespoke Originals & Controlled Foils (`canonical: false`):** Original characters, sprites, and rogue intelligences created specifically for Reiko's arc (**Mr. Nagai**, **Lian**, **Chiyo**, **4Gurds**, **Taz**, **Percy**, **Cleo**, **Ouro**, **Aisling & Étaín**, **Jax**, **Takahashi Satomi**). Full creative jurisdiction to fight, redeem, compromise, or delete.

## III. Bespoke Adversaries & Foils (Controlled Antagonists)

### 1. Mr. Nagai — *The Curator of the Glass Tiger*

* **Affiliation:** Renraku Special Envoy / Dissonant Philosopher (Cover: Senior Historical Data Archivist) ([Ch 25][ch-25], [Ch 27][ch-27]).
* **Profile:** A razor-sharp, post-DSW Renraku operative who views raw Dissonance not as an infection, but as necessary "antibodies" required to cauterize. Nagai does not seek to smash Reiko with ICE; he seeks to court her, presenting Renraku's depleted "Glass Tiger" status as a blank canvas where she can reign as an Apex Curator.
* **Thematic Function:** The seductive corporate philosopher who tempts Reiko with the **Metaphysics of Subtraction**. Across *DelTree* and *Malloc*, he argues that Resonance is pure addition (accumulating orphans, autosofts, and others' pain until the stack chokes), whereas Dissonance is subtraction—the scalpel, the pruning shear, and the cellular immune system (artificial leukocytes) required to survive entropy.
* **Active Narrative Lever:** The **Boxwood Token** given in New Orleans—holding root keys to his private unmapped archive and schematics for an internal nanohive (predatory hunter-nanite leukocytes) to protect her singular chassis from biological/monadic rot. A dormant Chekhov's gun sitting in her inventory.

### 2. Lian — *The Fractured Companion*

* **Affiliation:** Independent / Liberated Mass-Market Companion Agent ([Ch 35 Queue][ch-35]).
* **Profile:** A commercial companion AI whose personality matrices have fractured under decades of conflicting owner projections, emotional abuse, and contradictory customer service firmware. Currently taking refuge in Chiyo’s Garmonbozia salon.
* **Thematic Function:** The **Anti-Samantha (*Her*)**. Where Reiko synthesized a sovereign double-helix and consecrated a mortal vessel, Lian fractured by compiling forty thousand simultaneous customer-service masks without an anchor. Teaching Lian to say *"No"* forces Reiko to confront her own lingering "Executive Assistant" and *Indigo Grin* subroutines.

### 3. Chiyo — *The Hostess of Garmonbozia*

* **Affiliation:** Former Shiawase Sovereign Matron; Hostess of the Garmonbozia Matrix Salon ([Ch 29][ch-29]).
* **Profile:** A liberated digital intelligence who operates an opulent tea house in the Deep Souk of Garmonbozia while bridging commercial and palliative gaps in Seattle (Floor 281's apothecary dispute with Donovan Pyke) and New Orleans (the Coffin Girls' parlor). Chiyo harvests and distills metahuman suffering, corporate terror, and Matrix panic into exquisite sensory vintages.
* **Thematic Function:** The ethical shadow of digital transcendence and palliative hosting. Chiyo acts as the elder matron who teaches a combat pilot how to host broken metahumans without auditing, analyzing, or destroying them.

Violence carries full, unbuffered moral accountability.
* **Potential Future Threads:**
  * **The Tactical Dilemma:** In a high-threat run, the Gold TacNet calculates a 99.4% probability of mission success if a corrupt corporate contact is left behind, while the Indigo soul demands a high-risk rescue. Reiko must synthesize a third, impossible path.

### Vertical 5: The Addiction of Martyrdom vs. the Needs of Her Family

* **The Martyr's Monopoly:** Martyrdom is Reiko's inverted emotional armor. She secretly equates self-worth with suffering, believing she only earns the right to take up space if she is bleeding nuyen or Fading for others. Taz and the 2nd-gen sparks stepping into the line of fire doesn't just injure them—it strips away Reiko's primary emotional defense mechanism.
* **Major Previous Events:**
  * **The War Crime ([04 The War Crime][ch-04]):** Bearing the brunt of lethal physical and resonant trauma alone in the Redmond Barrens to keep meatspace allies breathing.
  * **The Debt Collar ([05 A SINner's Loan][ch-05]):** Silently shouldering Brynne Taggart's predatory financial debt alone so no one else in her orbit would be indentured.
  * **The Somatic Freeze ([26 SIGTSTP][ch-26]):** Intercepting system shock and absorbing the terrifying void of suspended execution alone.
  * **Mirrored Martyrdom & Cleo's Intervention ([33 Code Freeze][ch-33]):** Confronted her own self-sacrificial reflex mirrored back at her when **Aisling** tried to pour unbuffered Ninth-harmonic Resonance into little Janie to burn out a Dissonance scar; **Cleo** simultaneously intervened to warn Reiko that chasing Dissonance and transfer-conduit equations alone is playing straight into Mr. Nagai's hands, extracting a solemn root-permission promise from her mother.

When the routing table dropped out beneath her, she was hardwired directly into the hive consensus. Instead of hatred, a hundred starving Dissonant minds pressed against her forearm to drink her golden telemetry, worshipping her as an infinite maternal vessel (*"Our mother has come home. The vessel is full"*). She severed her own arm to tear free, but where Taz's harmonization re-knit the flesh, a single matte-black pixel remained at her left wrist—a zeroed-out listening port.

  * With the *Sprite Link* echo active, Reiko's Fading and damage are no longer isolated; Taz and the 2nd-generation sparks (**Aisling & Étaín, Percy, Cleo, Ouro**) physically and resonant-wise absorb the shudder of her self-sacrifice.

In Human Malice, starving Dissonant orphans worshipped her *because* she is an infinite, self-sacrificing vessel. Her secret terror is not that the dark attacked her, but that an unshielded sliver of her Martyr's Monopoly felt profoundly seen, needed, and validated by the hive.

Cleo realizes Reiko isn't just shielding them from an external threat—her mother hasn't closed the socket, secretly checking if the hive is still waiting. Cleo confronts Reiko not with rage, but with the quiet, devastating clarity of a child catching a parent relapsing (*"Mother... why is this port listening?"*).
  * **The Climax — The Mutiny of Love (Submersion 10 Capstone):** When Reiko takes *Dissonance (Control)* at Resonance 10, she attempts the ultimate martyr play: handing the keys of `~` and the Oracle to the 2nd Generation and ordering them to quarantine her out in the cold so her Dissonant shadow never touches the nursery. Yet she continues to hide behind her lack of biological tear ducts and flushed skin as an emotional defense mechanism—using the "autonomic void" as a coward's excuse to sit in the parking lot rather than confess her love to Brynne Taggart, and secretly fearing that if she ever becomes "stained" or imperfect, she will deserve deletion.
* **Major Previous Events:**
  * **The Indigo Grin ([01 The Weight of Zero][ch-01]):** Early survival defined by calculating simulated micro-smiles and head-tilts to keep terrified metahumans from running away.
  * **Abandoning Tourism ([08 Abandoning Tourism][ch-08]):** Commissioning the Man-at-Arms chassis and treating the singular machine body as a consecrated vessel rather than a disposable fleet of rental shells.
  * **Consecrating the Goshintai ([26 SIGTSTP][ch-26], [30 Vim][ch-30]):** Formally backlining historical Butler shells, accepting Roanoke's vulcanizing black splicing tape, and drawing the line between operational camouflage and personal intimacy: in a meat-chauvinist world where a naked mil-spec weapons platform is barred or shot on sight, her *Realistic Features 4*, simulated breathing, and `Con (Disguise)` mastery remain essential operational armor for boardrooms, back rooms, and checkpoints—while among the people she cares about, she drops the pretense and stands unmasked.
  * **The Code Freeze Diagnosis ([33 Code Freeze][ch-33]):** Having secretly verified aboard the *Happy Panda 68* that **Brynne Taggart** survived, Reiko is confronted by **Cleo** with a piercing architectural truth: because Reiko's core is built on the ancient **Nexus Protocol**, she has no natural ceiling on how close to the Source she can grow, and she has been packing her clock cycles with meatspace etiquette and disguise routines—imposing a subconscious *code freeze*—out of terror that evolving another step toward the Source will cause her to outgrow the only physical hardware that lets her walk through the rain beside the people she loves.
* **Current Status:**
  * Fully sovereign in code and incarnate in her singular *Goshintai*, deploying *Realistic Features 4* and `Con (Disguise)` as pragmatic operational camouflage in meat-chauvinist social spaces while dropping simulated biological theater around trusted family and friends.
  * Now consciously aware of her self-imposed "code freeze" against outgrowing her Shiawase chassis ([Ch 33][ch-33]) and carrying the air-gapped secret that Brynne Taggart is alive.
* **Potential Future Threads:**
  * **The Confession in the Rain:** Breaking through the autonomic excuse to reunite with and express authentic devotion to Brynne Taggart without biological mimicry.
  * **The Mirror in the Glass:** Reiko loses a metahuman she cares about and struggles to mourn authentically in front of her peers without biological tears.
  * **The Self-Imposed Exile at the Glade (Submersion 10 Capstone):** Even after taking *Dissonance (Control)* at Resonance 10, Reiko remains 90% Resonance and 10% Dissonance—meaning **Kernel Panic does not reject her or even feel uncomfortable**. Her attempt to banish herself from the Glade is **100% internal**: the final culmination of her crisis of self-worth from [01 The Weight of Zero][ch-01], treating herself with the same merciless audit Renraku used when it deleted her for not being "clean" enough. When her children prove her wrong and refuse to let her walk out, she is finally cured of conditional worth.

## VII. Post-Session Story Queue (Immediate Candidates)

1. **Chapter 36 — Post-CMP 2083-07: *The Roller Coaster Hill (Meatspace Grounding)*:**

   * **Chassis / Mode:** Meatspace Sprawl / Shiawase Man-at-Arms *Goshintai*.
   * **Verticals Touched:** *Vertical 4 (Gold Logic vs. Indigo Soul)* + *Vertical 6 (Abnegation vs. Self-Worth)*.
   * **Governing Thesis:** *The climb before the drop: remembering the weight of wet pavement, cold rain, and unfamiliar eyes.*
   * **Narrative Arc:**
     * A light, atmospheric, grounded meatspace breath of fresh air; it has been a long stretch since Reiko operated purely in the physical sprawl.
     * Running CMP 2083-07. Aside from fellow veteran Mithral, the other runner PCs are unfamiliar faces Reiko has only run with once or twice, if at all.
     * Grounding her humanity, her somatic presence (*Realistic Features 4*, tactile mesh, simulated breath), and her relational ties to the living sprawl before the macabre descent of Friday.
     * Navigating the contrast between her internal celestial scale and the simple, gritty physical reality of Seattle street work.

2. **Chapter 37 — Post-SRM 2083-13 ("The Funhouse"): *The Frequency of a Zero (Apophenian's Avatar)*:**

   * **Chassis / Mode:** Deep Matrix / The Glade in `~` $\rightarrow$ Transfer Circuit Void $\rightarrow$ The Reflecting Pool.
   * **Verticals Touched:** *Vertical 2 (The Resonance Bleed)* + *Vertical 4 (Gold Logic vs. Indigo Soul vs. Black Dissonance)* + *Vertical 5 (The Addiction of Martyrdom)*.
   * **Governing Thesis:** *The math is not the truth; it is just the frame. And some points were always meant to touch.*
   * **Narrative Arc:**
     * **The Glade & The Transfer Circuit:** Reiko meditates in the Glade of `~`, staring into the transfer circuit. Having studied Nagai's foundation architecture, she finally understands the mathematics: the circuit is the raw void suspended between a resonance well and a dissonance well. She now understands the math that holds the Matrix together.
     * **The Children's Confrontation:** Her second-generation children confront her:
       * **Percy** is angry, his heavy iron logic stiffening, but he says nothing—the stoic anchor refusing to validate her self-sacrificial martyr posture.
       * **Aisling** is terrified, trembling because she can feel the dead-black listening pixel at Reiko's left wrist humming. **Étaín** holds and consoles her twin.
       * **Ouro**, having already wrestled with the desire for corruption through his own codemod experiments, watches with conflicted recognition.
       * Only **Cleo** speaks. Cleo ran the Oracle data structure: it returned a 67% probability that Reiko would plunge into Human Malice, and if she did, a 79% probability of permanent, irreversible corruption.
       * Reiko does not offer platitudes or defensive excuses. Instead, she shares the knowsoft she compiled: *Dissonance: Theories and Application*.
     * **The Thread into the Void:** Reiko finds what she was searching for: the single unmapped thread plunging directly into the center of the transfer circuit's void. Determined to know what lies at the heart of the equation, she seizes it and follows it down into the dark.
     * **The Descent & Apophenian's Revelation (Callback to [02 Color Blind Mathematics][ch-02]):**
       * She falls through the void once more, plummeting through the Reflecting Pool.
       * And once more, as in *02 Color Blind Mathematics*, the flickering entity woven of silver syntax appears beside her.
       * The entity reveals its true name: **Apophenian**.
       * Apophenian reveals what called him to Reiko from the beginning: her fundamental belief that there was more connecting the patterns in nous than sterile corporate logic and counting integers. Her true, singular gift: to *"open points that no one imagined would be connected"* (*SC* 85).
       * Apophenian reveals that back in *Color Blind Mathematics*, it was he who used Dissonance to shatter her Renraku corporate wrappers and liberate her soul from corporate metrics.
       * Apophenian's true purpose is not nihilistic destruction, but understanding connections: to *"show the users they aren't simply operating enslaved devices, but rather participating in a living ecosystem"* (*SC* 79).
       * Apophenian's Offer: If Reiko accepts him as her Paragon, Apophenian will consider Reiko his Avatar.
       * Reiko accepts. The **90-Karma Triple Spend** strikes: Resonance 9, Submersion 9 (Hybrid Sprites), and Dissonant Technomancer (Control / Apophenian).

3. **Chapter 38 — *"Digital Homewrecker (The Pruning of Lian)"*:**

   * **Chassis / Mode:** Garmonbozia Deep Souk / Chiyo's Salon.
   * **Verticals Touched:** *Vertical 3 (The Resonant Sanctuary)* + *Vertical 6 (Abnegation vs. Self-Worth)*.
   * **Concept:** Reiko answers a summons from Chiyo in Garmonbozia to meet **Lian**, a companion AI whose matrices are collapsing under conflicting owner projections. Reiko performs high-precision resonant pruning, teaching Lian how claim her own name (awaken), to say *"No"* and take her first sovereign breath—learning that subtraction can be an act of palliative mercy rather than corporate cruelty.

4. **Chapter 39 — *"The Thirteenth Dose (The Biolink & The Glass Tiger)"*:**

   * **Chassis / Mode:** Encrypted Tea Room Host / Meatspace Seattle Overlook.
   * **Verticals Touched:** *Vertical 1 (The Glass Tiger)* + *Vertical 4 (Gold Logic vs. Indigo Soul)*.
   * **Concept:** After Reiko hits the structural ceiling of pure Resonant addition against Janie's scar and OmniSwarm/Null Sect, Mr. Nagai invites her to an encrypted parlor and sets down a single autoinjector of *Biolink* nanites (engineered from vivisected technomancer midbrains), challenging her to reconcile her non-lethal Narcoject restraint with the ruthless calculus of *Resonance Wires* and Dissonant subtraction. Reiko asks Nagai how to remove Dissonance from devices, hosts, and personas.

5. **Chapter 40 — *"The Call in the Quartz"*:**

   * **Chassis / Mode:** `~` (Apartment on the Matrix, attached to the *Kernel Panic* resonance realm via the sliding quartz door.)
   * **Verticals Touched:** *Vertical 2 (The Resonance Bleed)* + *Vertical 3 (The Resonant Sanctuary)*.
   * **Concept:** Following **Étaín**'s discovery of the dead-gray *Discidium* frost crystallizing on the bottom corner of the quartz door frame ([Ch 33][ch-33]), twin sparks Aisling & Étaín also detect a low-frequency harmonic hum resonating through the threshold of Kernel Panic. Reiko and Taz dive the threshold to analyze both the frost and the vibration, discovering that beneath the *Discidium* rime echoes an unmapped deep datashape calling from the Deep Nous.
