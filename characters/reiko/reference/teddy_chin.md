# Teddy Chin: Character Profile & Reference Dossier

## Overview

Teddy Chin is an Octagon Triad negotiator, fixer, and Cat-tradition shaman based out of Tacoma, Seattle. In stark contrast to his modest physical stature, Teddy’s persona is twelve feet tall—a bombastic, loud-talking showman clad in sharp 1950s couture who treats shadow operations as grand theatrical performances. Despite his swagger and theatrical flourishes, he is an effective operator under the direct authority of Octagon Triad lieutenant Ni Ni Xiaolu.

---

## Physical Appearance & Demeanor

* **Metatype & Heritage:** Human male of Chinese descent.
* **Attire & Distinctive Style:** Crisp white suit jacket over black sharkskin slacks, two-tone wingtips, thick blocky black sunglasses, and an immaculate, pomade-lacquered pompadour.
* **Habits & Swagger:** Rarely seen without a freshly shaken cocktail in his left hand. His right arm hangs fixed at his side, giving his gait a measured, deliberate gunfighter swagger.
* **Demeanor:** Euphoric, manic enthusiasm paired with explosive volume. He lives for drama, theatrics, and celebrating victories—though he swiftly snaps to nervous obedience in the presence of his superior, Ni Ni Xiaolu.

## Base of Operations: The Palace of China (Tacoma)

A historic multi-tiered red-brick Chinese palace structure in Tacoma, weathered by decades of acid rain and snow. Formerly a landmark restaurant, strip venue, and sports club, it now operates as a high-volume subterranean dance club hosting international DJs. 

Behind the sensory assault of peach-scented bass beats and flashing dance floors, the venue's true utility lies in its sunken **conversation pits** and VIP booths. Layered with high-circle acoustic silencing and privacy spells, these pits conceal negotiations from prying eyes, astral snoopers, and optical surveillance, serving as a primary Tacoma nexus for Triad handoffs, Johnson meets, and black-market contracts.

## Campaign Role & Relationship with Reiko (Yuriko Star)

* **SRM 2081-13 (A Cookie from Uncle Lung):** Teddy served as the operational handler on behalf of Ni Ni Xiaolu, orchestrating the ambush of an Eighty-Eights Triad transport carrying the sacred jade statue *Shin Wei Lau* along Marine Drive.
* **Theatrical Logistics:** Teddy insisted the team operate under comic masks and name tags while driving an unassuming minivan to maximize public humiliation for both the Eighty-Eights and Lone Star.
* **Handoff & Demeanor:** When Yuriko and her team delivered the statue intact to The Palace of China, Teddy erupted in manic delight, posing for victory holos before Ni Ni Xiaolu intervened, physically disciplined him with a swift slap to maintain triad decorum, and finalized the team's payment.
* **Standing Favor:** Reiko earned **+2 Favor Points** (+1 for full mask/nametag compliance, +1 for securing the *Shin Wei Lau* intact). Under standard contact advancement rules, Teddy automatically upgraded from Loyalty 0 to **Loyalty 1** (1 FP cost), leaving **1 Favor Point** banked.
