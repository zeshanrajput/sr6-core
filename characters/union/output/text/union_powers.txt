 MONAD ABILITIES & NANITE SWARM TRAITS (NV 4):
  * Monad Matrix Attributes
    Internal nanite swarm synthesizes Living Persona ASDF (A:2 S:6 D:7 F:7)
    with 12d6 Full Matrix Defense (14d6 when Adrenaline Pump active).
  * Resculpt
    Extended INT + NV (16, 1 combat round) test to alter physical biological
    appearance and voice.
  * Monad Toughness
    Adds +2 Condition boxes (NV/2) to Condition Monitors (+1 Physical, +1
    Stun -> 12 Physical, 12 Stun boxes), shifts all wound penalties down by
    1.
  * Physical Attribute Boost
    Minor Action; roll NV test (4 dice). Each hit adds +1 to any Physical
    Attribute (BOD/AGI/REA/STR) or +1 Minor Action (stackable/splittable,
    max boost = min(NV, +4)). Lasts for hits in combat rounds. When ended,
    decreases NV by total boost for 1 minute; boost beyond racial max
    inflicts 1 Physical damage box.
  * Mental Attribute Boost
    Minor Action; roll NV test (4 dice). Each hit adds +1 to any Mental
    Attribute (WIL/LOG/INT/CHA) (stackable/splittable, max boost = min(NV,
    +4)). Lasts for hits in combat rounds. When ended, decreases NV by total
    boost for 1 minute; boost beyond racial max inflicts 1 Stun damage box.
  * Rapid Healing
    Minor or Major Action cellular nanite repair to reduce Condition Monitor
    damage.
  * Adrenal Control
    Minor Action; WIL + NV (2) test to remain conscious when mortally
    wounded or stunned.
