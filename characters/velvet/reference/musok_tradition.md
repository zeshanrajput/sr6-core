## 1. Official Setting Canon (*Street Wyrd* & *Seoul Survivor*)

In Sixth World magical theory, **Musok** (Korean indigenous shamanism, also termed *Muism* or *Sindo*) is an ancient, ecstatic spiritual tradition centered on the reconciliation between human communities, ancestral souls, and nature deities (*Shin* / *Kami*).

### Key Published Concepts (*Street Wyrd*, pp. 48–54)
* **The Mudang:** The Korean shaman, traditionally female (though male practitioners, *Baksu*, exist), who serves as an intermediary between the mundane and spirit worlds.
* **Mansin (Ten Thousand Spirits):** An accomplished mudang who has integrated an expansive pantheon of divine entities and ancestral souls within their spiritual court.
* **Sinbyeong (Spirit Sickness):** The unavoidable physical, emotional, and spiritual affliction marking a prospective mudang before initiation—manifesting as hallucinations, unexplainable bodily fevers, and profound dissociation until the spirits are accepted.
* **Naerim-gut:** The definitive initiation ritual wherein the mudang ceases resisting the spirits, undergoes ritual death and rebirth, and officially welcomes the spirits into their body as sacred internal guests.
* **The Daleketnon Elven Metavariant:** An indigenous Philippine elven metatype documented in Southeast Asian lore. Characterized by preternatural social magnetism, extreme aesthetic elegance, and an innate supernatural gravity (Charisma 10/14) that bends the perceptions and emotional baselines of onlookers.

## 2. Narrative Expansions: The Metaphysics of the Hollow Vessel (*Bin Geureut*)

Within the narrative architecture of `sr6-core`, Velvet’s Musok heritage is not a decorative aesthetic; it is the **philosophical and somatic engine of his survival**:

### A. The Hollow Vessel (*Bin Geureut*)
* **The Emptied Self (*Śūnyatā*):** When Mitsuhama Applied Technologies bio-sculpted away Jin-Young’s Daleketnon face and forced him through 15 years of corporate idol trauma, they believed they were creating an obedient, soulless tool.
* **The Transhuman Inversion:** Drawing upon classical Buddhist and Musok doctrine, Velvet reframed the loss of his static ego: because he has no permanent, fixed face, he is the **unconditioned hollow vessel (*Bin Geureut*)** capable of hosting any human soul with boundless compassion.

### B. Indra’s Jewel Net & Artificial Reincarnation
* **Every Persona an Archetype:** The identities Velvet inhabits (*Lee Ji-yoo, Tanaka Ryo, Mei Jing, Noekie, Zhang Wei*) are not deceptive corporate lies; they are authentic archetypes anchored in **Indra's Net**—the holographic web of lived human experience across creation.
* **The Bodhisattva with Teeth (*Upāya*):** Velvet practices *Skillful Means*—wearing the faces and motor memory of mortals to uncover corporate crimes, bear witness to the suffering of wage-slaves, and enact justice on behalf of the silenced.

### C. The Gaiasphere vs. The Noosphere
* **Velvet’s Primordial Root:** Velvet is the sole protagonist of the three who accesses the **original Akashic record in the Gaiasphere**—the living astral plane and primordial mana matrix of Earth and living souls.
* **Somatic Motor Bleed:** When he channels a past persona or ancestral guide, it does not manifest as a "voice in the head." It manifests as physical motor bleed: fingers tying ancient maritime knots, tracheal shifts in vocal geometry, and an unbearable clarity of presence.
