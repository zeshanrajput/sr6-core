## 1. The Inter-Session Narrative Mandate (Operational vs. Ontological)

* **The Operational Layer (The Gaming Table):** At the table, Velvet is the frontline infiltrator, Face, and tactical edge. He rolls high-dice-pool Influence, Con, and Disguise tests, maneuvers through corporate checkpoints, and provides magical team buffs.
* **The Ontological Layer (The Prose Domain):** In prose, shifting flesh via *Cosmetic Control* carries a severe somatic and spiritual tax. The narrative explores the horror of engineered Charisma 10 (14), the trauma of Mitsuhama bio-sculpting, and the existential question of whether an empty vessel can ever be genuinely loved without the other party falling into his manufactured gravity.
* **Consecrated Capital & Existential Debt:**
  * *Nuyen as Sacred Vestments:* Spending ¥36,100+ (over 30% of lifetime earnings) on counterfeit SINs, high-fashion outerwear, and identity masks is not shopping—it is procuring sacred altars and ritual vestments for each adopted life in Indra's Net.
  * *Karma as Spiritual Debt:* Over 94% of lifetime karma is dedicated to spiritual evolution: Initiation grades, Qi foci bonding (-18 karma), Adept metamagics (*Channeling, Adept Centering*), and reclaiming forced bodily plasticities as sovereign *nirmāṇakāya*.
* **The Two-Tier Dramatic Sphere:**
  * *Tier A Campaign Canon (`canonical: true`):* SRM Fixers and Johnsons (Brynne Taggart, Donovan Pyke, Julian) are bounded by the Canon Ceiling.
  * *Tier B Bespoke Foils (`canonical: false`):* Real narrative consequence, moral failure, and relational fracture are fought against original foils: Hana/Luna (the trans-Pacific captive anchor), Min-seo (the truth-teller who shatters moral retreat), Master Kang (the Musok mentor), and the ghosts of adopted personas.

1. **The Empty Vessel (*Bin Geureut* / Śūnyatā):** Corporate conditioning treated identity erasure as spiritual murder. Drawing upon Korean Musok and Mahayana Buddhist insight, Velvet inverts this: having no static core is not a void of deficiency, but ultimate liberation. Because he has no fixed ego, he has boundless capacity to host form.
2. **Artificial Reincarnation (*Mansin* & Indra's Net):** Every persona Velvet embodies (*Tanaka Ryo, Lee Ji-yoo, Mei Jing, Zhang Wei*) is not a cynical corporate deception; it is an authentic lived chapter in Indra's Net. He retains the motor memory, vocal cadence, and moral grief of every life worn.
3. **Charisma 10 (14) Existential Horror:** High Charisma is an invasive biological gravity. When others look at him, their compliance and affection are warped. Velvet can never know with absolute certainty if another being's love is authentic or merely their nervous system falling into his bio-engineered pull.
4. **Camouflage as Operational Armor, Not Shame:** Disguise and shifting bone structure are not an attempt to erase the baseline self in self-loathing; they are the skillful means (*Upāya*) required to walk the streets and protect the vulnerable under corporate apartheid.
5. **The Bodhisattva with Teeth:** Rejecting passive moral retreat or ascetic martyrdom. Sanctuaries must be defended with formidable force. Wielding the reforged *MCI-EXP-884-LUNA* Power Focus R3 is a sacred blood debt.

## 3. Acoustic Cadence, Sensory Lens, & Somatic Reality

* **Narrative POV:** Third Person Deep Limited. Strict internality; no omniscient leaks.
* **Acoustic Speech vs. Mindspeech:**
  * Acoustic speech: Double quotation marks (`"..."`).
  * Mindspeech / DNI transmissions / astral thoughts: Italics without quotes (`*...*`).
* **Somatic Tax of Cosmetic Control:** Shifting facial features, gender markers, or bone height requires physical somatic friction: wet cartilage clicks, localized burning behind the jaw, ribcage compression, and deep physical exhaustion.
* **Sensory Lens:**
  * *Primary:* Visual and Astral Aura flux (perceiving emotional hues, spirit ribbons, talismanic resonance).
  * *Secondary:* Thermal and Barometric texture (incense smoke, cold tea, humidity, rain on silk).
  * *Blind Spots:* Cold corporate financial abstractions; tends to personalize institutional cruelty into intimate betrayals.
