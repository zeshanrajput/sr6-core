## II. Campaign Continuity & The Two-Tier Dramatic Sphere

To protect shared campaign continuity while allowing high-stakes personal storytelling, all characters and plot threads operate under a strict two-tier separation ([Doctrine][doctrine]):

* **Tier A: Campaign Canon (`canonical: true`):** High-profile Shadowrun Missions NPCs (Donovan Pyke, Eddie Wei, Ms. Snow, Ni Ni Xiaolu, Brynne Taggart, Lady Frederika van de Poll, Princess Juliana-Marie) belong to the shared campaign. Velvet interacts with them in a read-only orbit without altering their macro-timelines.
  * *Chronological Quarantine Rule (The Shadow Chapters):* In *State of the Art: 2084*, Donovan Pyke and Eddie Wei are killed in a late-2081 Seattle ambush, prompting Ms. Snow to transform the Shadow Chapters into a ruthless paramilitary syndicate backed by MCT and OmniStar ([Lore: MCT][lore-mct]). **This plotline is strictly quarantined until Velvet plays the final 2081 Seattle capstone mission at the table.** Until then, Pyke remains alive in Velvet's narrative present, and the Guild of Freelance Assets / Shadow Chapters function as a localized, non-lethal union ([Ch 12][ch-12]).
* **Tier B: Bespoke Foils & Controlled Adversaries (`canonical: false`):** Original characters and localized cells created specifically for Velvet’s narrative (e.g., Hana, Min-seo, TXM bandmates, Aiden Vance, Inquisitor Gray). We hold full narrative agency over these figures—they can be fought, redeemed, compromised, or killed to drive permanent character transformation.

## III. Bespoke Adversaries & Foils (Controlled Antagonists)

### 1. Aiden Vance — *The Algorithmic Mirror*

* **Affiliation:** Senior Field Evaluator, Horizon / The Dawkins Group (Cover: Independent Corporate Mediator & High-Society Culture Consultant) ([Lore: Horizon][lore-horizon]).
* **Profile:** A mundane human face augmented with tailored pheromones, micro-expression optics, and a direct data-link to Horizon's predictive **Consensus** engine. Vance is one of the "disposable, but extremely amiable assets" contracted by the Dawkins Group to slip into corporate and underworld circles, build friendships, and plant psychological seeds.
* **Thematic Function:** The dark mirror to Velvet’s *Anatomy of Un-Manufactured Solace* ([Ch 08][ch-08], [Ch 13][ch-13]). Where Velvet steps down his Charisma 10(14) gravity to create genuine, un-monetized sanctuaries, Vance uses simulated stillness, warm lighting, and artisanal hospitality as a predatory phishing lure to harvest emotional telemetry for the Consensus. He mocks Velvet’s spiritual journey, asserting that Jin-Young’s empathy and tea ceremonies are merely un-depreciated Mitsuhama factory firmware.
* **The Game-Theoretic Collision:**
  * *Aiden's Play (Zero-Sum Phishing):* Relies entirely on information asymmetry and manufactured vulnerability. If the mark accepts the warmth, Aidan harvests micro-tells and steers them into corporate alignment; if the mark turns paranoid or violent, Aidan logs the trauma-points to exploit later.
  * *Velvet's Counter (Radical Sincerity & Daesul):* Velvet recognizes the technique immediately—not with anger, but with master-craft professional appreciation (*"Ninety-two degrees, Aiden? Chapter four of the Dawkins handbook. You brew it exquisitely"*). Velvet refuses both compliance and hostility, starving the Consensus engine of reactive telemetry.
  * *The Honorable Off-Ramp:* Velvet reverses Aidan's planned reversal by proposing an interest-based, non-zero-sum compromise that advances long-term communal stability while letting Aidan deliver clean operational metrics to Los Angeles without puppeting either party.

### 2. Inquisitor Gray — *The Avenger of the Thirty Pieces*

* **Affiliation:** Cell Coordinator, *The Thirty Pieces of Silver* (Cover: Independent Forensic Telemetry Investigator) ([Lore: 30 Pieces][lore-30-pieces]).
* **Profile:** A relentless, cybernetically scarred former military investigator whose family was consumed during the Disian Shadow War. Operating from the decentralized *30 Pieces* hit-lists, Gray hunts anyone linked to pro-Disian megacorporations (specifically **MCT** and **Wuxing**).
* **Thematic Function:** The binary zealot who forces Velvet to wield **Teeth**. In Gray’s ledger, nuance does not exist: Velvet carries MCT patents in his marrow, and **Hana (Director Yueying)** is a public executive for Wuxing ([Ch 06][ch-06]). Gray cannot be charmed, bribed, or soothed. By targeting Hana’s media operations in Neo-Seoul and tracking Velvet’s contacts, Gray forces Velvet to engage in lethal counter-espionage to protect the people they love ([Ch 11][ch-11]).

### 3. The Surviving Death Messengers (TXM) — *The Broken Chord*

* **Affiliation:** Mitsuhama Media Logistics / Special Ritual Assets (Delinquent Division) ([Lore: MCT][lore-mct]).
* **The Roster:**
  * **Ji-Hoon (The Hermetic Conductor):** Cold, precision-obsessed ritual leader who views Jin-Young’s defection as a structural betrayal that ruined fifteen years of discipline.
  * **Min-Ki (The Betrayed Adept):** Kinetic stage-fighter and physical adept who took the physical punishment from MCT handlers when Jin-Young vanished.
  * **Tae-Hyun (The Feral Shaman):** Aggressive corporate conjurer who treats spirits as bound slaves and hungers to drag Jin-Young back in chains.
* **Thematic Function:** With MCT plummeting toward the #10 spot in the 2084 Corporate Court emergency audit and shedding subsidiaries in fire-sales ([Lore: MCT][lore-mct]), the obsolete TXM unit faces liquidation unless they recover their missing astral gateway (Jin-Young) or salvage their genetic patents ([Ch 04][ch-04]).

## V. Internal Conflict Verticals (The Ontological Layer)

### Vertical 4: Empathy vs. Manipulation (The Mirror & The Sanctuary)

* **Core Focus:** The relational horror of engineered perfection. Mitsuhama built Velvet with Charisma 10 (14 with spells) to warp emotions and harvest devotion ([Build][build]). Velvet is slowly discovering genuine empathy by creating *un-manufactured sanctuaries*, yet remains haunted by the paranoia that no one’s affection or trust toward him is real—that every smile is merely a nervous system drowning in his biometric field ([Voice Spec][voice-spec]).
* **Major Previous Events:**
  * **Pavel’s Broth ([07_Bliss][ch-07]):** Sat with the blistered, terrified street mage Pavel at a Barrens noodle cart. Refused to interrogate or manipulate him; bought him hot broth and gave him a clean alibi, restoring his dignity as a practitioner.
  * **Whiskey’s Instinctive Recoil ([02_Faceless_Mirror][ch-02]):** In Touristville, street doc Whiskey exhibited wariness and tension around Velvet’s ambient presence even when his active spells were dropped, highlighting how his engineered gravity unsettles veterans.
  * **Fifteen Minutes in Amsterdam ([11_Silence][ch-11]):** Inverted his Charisma across the oak table to create a pool of pure, sedative stillness for Min-seo, allowing her to drop her Wuxing executive terror and drink hot chicory coffee in peace.
* **Current Status:**
  * Capable of consciously stepping down his ego (*Bin Geureut*) to act as a Shinto Sacred Mirror (*Yata no Kagami*).
  * Still deeply isolated in personal relationships; terrified of accidentally puppeting friends or teammates.
* **Potential Future Threads:**
  * **The Chess Match with Aiden Vance:** Sitting across from Horizon’s **Aiden Vance** during a negotiation or high-society gala. Watching Vance deploy weaponized, Consensus-driven "active listening" and tailored pheromones to break a target, forcing Velvet to confront the nauseating mirror of what corporate empathy looks like from the outside.
  * **The Recoil of a Friend:** An ally or contact snaps out of a high-stress negotiation where Velvet pushed his Charisma to the limit, looking at Velvet with sudden revulsion and asking: *"Did I actually agree to that, or did you just do that to my head?"*
  * **The Unmoved Anchor:** Developing a bond with someone completely immune to his biometric aura (such as Aura in her drone frame, a blind street elder, or a heavily warded practitioner), where trust must be built solely through sacrificial action (*Daesul*) rather than vocal resonance.

Vance is left sitting in his own quiet room—not broken by violence, but deeply unsettled by an encounter with grace that Horizon cannot model.
3. **Sub-Basement Vignette — *"The Ash of Synthetic Silver"*:**
   * **Persona:** Un-sculpted Jin-Young.
   * **Setting:** Deep Seattle Underground safehouse or Whiskey's sub-basement lodge ([Ch 12][ch-12]).
   * **Verticals Touched:** *Vertical 5 (Asceticism vs. Sovereign Teeth)* + *Vertical 6 (Terror of the Engineered Self)*.
   * **Concept:** Performing an authentic *Musok* offering using Barrens tap water, synthetic starch, and a burning certified credstick (*jijeon*), reconciling his synthetic origins with his ancestral calling.
