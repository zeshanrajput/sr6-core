 MONAD ABILITIES & NANITE SWARM TRAITS (NV 6):
  * Monad Matrix Attributes
    Synthesizes Living Persona ASDF (Attack: CHA, Sleaze: INT, Data
    Processing: LOG, Firewall: WIL) via internal nanite matrix
    co-processing.
  * Resculpt
    Extended Intuition + NV (16, 1 combat round) test to alter facial
    appearance, skin tone, and voice; grants +2 dice pool bonus on Disguise
    tests.
  * Monad Toughness
    Adds one-half Nanite Volume (+3 boxes at NV 6) to Condition Monitors (+2
    Physical, +1 Stun), and shifts all Wound Modifiers down by one level.
  * Physical Attribute Boost
    Minor Action: roll NV test (6 dice). Each hit can add +1 Minor Action
    per combat round or boost a physical attribute for duration.
  * Rapid Cellular Healing
    Minor or Major Action: roll NV test (6 dice) to reduce Condition Monitor
    damage boxes by hits (Major Action heals double hits and grants +1
    situational Edge).
  * Adrenal Control
    Minor Action: WIL + NV test to remain conscious and active when physical
    or stun condition monitors are full.
