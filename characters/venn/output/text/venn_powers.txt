 MONAD ABILITIES & NANITE SWARM TRAITS (NV 6):
  * Monad Matrix Attributes
    Internal nanite swarm synthesizes Living Persona ASDF (A:2 S:6 D:8 F:9)
    with 15d6 Full Matrix Defense (17d6 active under Willpower 7).
  * Resculpt
    Extended INT + NV (16, 1 combat round) test to alter physical biological
    appearance and voice.
  * Monad Toughness
    Adds +3 Condition boxes (NV/2) to Condition Monitors (+2 Physical, +1
    Stun -> 14 Physical [10 base + 2 Redliner + 2 Monad], 13 Stun boxes [12
    base + 1 Monad]), shifts all wound penalties down by 1.
  * Physical Attribute Boost
    Minor Action; roll NV test (6 dice). With 4-Edge boost (13 exploding
    dice), rolling >= 3 hits (89.4%) or >= 4 hits (76.2%) adds +4 Minor
    Actions or physical attribute boosts for hits in combat rounds. When
    ended, decreases NV by total boost for 1 minute.
  * Rapid Healing
    Minor or Major Action cellular nanite repair to reduce Condition Monitor
    damage.
  * Adrenal Control
    Minor Action; WIL + NV (2) test (13d6 dice pool) to remain conscious
    when mortally wounded or stunned.
