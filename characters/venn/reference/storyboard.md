## II. Campaign Continuity & The Two-Tier Dramatic Sphere

To protect shared campaign continuity while enabling high-stakes personal storytelling, Venn operates under a strict two-tier separation ([Doctrine][doctrine]):

* **Tier A: Campaign Canon (`canonical: true`):** Shadowrun Missions fixers, CAS corporate suits, and campaign-level VIPs. Venn interacts with them in a professional, read-only orbit without mutating macro-timeline metaplot events.
* **Tier B: Bespoke Foils & Controlled Adversaries (`canonical: false`):** Original characters created specifically for Venn’s narrative. We hold full mortal and dramatic agency over these figures—they can be bargained with, compromised, or eliminated.

## IV. External Narrative Verticals

* **Current Status:**
  * Officially listed as "presumed deceased or purged" in Evo deep-space registries.

## V. Internal Conflict Verticals (The Ontological Layer)

### Vertical 4: The Inversion Chiasmus (Essence vs. Heart)

* **Core Focus:** The psychological role reversal: Nathan’s biological Essence collapses to 0.1, leaving him emotionally flattened and cold, while Veronica evolves from an analytical AI seed into an empathetic emotional pacemaker ([Voice Spec][voice-spec]).
* **Major Previous Events:**
  * **The Collapse to 0.1 ([03_Threading_the_Wire][ch-03]):** Nathan’s sensory world turned monochrome and numb following the skillwire surgery; Veronica felt the cold void in his neurochemistry and stepped forward to regulate his emotional panic.
  * **A Night of Her Own ([04_Night_of_Her_Own][ch-04]):** Veronica’s first experience of physical embodiment, discovering the visceral wonder of rain, cold metal, and mortal vulnerability.
* **Current Status:**
  * Veronica actively monitors Nathan’s amygdala and heart rate, dampening cyberpsychotic rage spikes before they break the surface.
  * Nathan willingly bears the heavy physical toll of chrome so Veronica has a safe vessel to experience reality.
* **Potential Future Threads:**
  * **The Sensor Blackout:** An EMP or high-rating jammer temporarily severs Veronica’s connection to Nathan’s nervous system during a combat run; Nathan is left completely alone with his 0.1 Essence coldness, fighting to maintain human discipline without his emotional anchor.

---

### Vertical 5: Co-Occupancy & Sibling Friction

* **Core Focus:** The lived friction of sharing one nervous system; rejecting frictionless unanimity in favor of earned consensus.
* **Major Previous Events:**
  * **The Vocal Intercept ([03_Threading_the_Wire][ch-03]):** Veronica seized motor control of Nathan’s vocal cords to speak aloud directly to Marcus during surgery: *"The bus is stable, Marcus... seat the chip."*
  * **The Unilateral Choice ([06_Sabotaged_Berth][ch-06]):** Nathan’s raw human shock when discovering Veronica deleted their Mars ticket without asking—followed by mutual recognition that they both wanted to stay.
* **Current Status:**
  * High-speed consensual telemetry; zero-question protocol for observable data.
  * Mutual respect for boundaries: internal debate is reserved for ethics, tactical priorities, and emotional health.
* **Potential Future Threads:**
  * **The Divided Vote:** Nathan wants to take a violent revenge strike against an Atlanta fixer who betrayed them; Veronica refuses to coordinate the skillwires, demanding tactical withdrawal. They must argue it out while pinned behind cover.

---

### Vertical 6: Somatic Dysmorphia & Resculpt

* **Core Focus:** The physical reality of Monad physiology; reconciling the mechanical sensation of used chrome with the plasticity of Monad flesh.
* **Major Previous Events:**
  * **Dual Icons Foundation ([05_Dual_Icons_Foundation][ch-05]):** Exploring the deep architecture of the Foundation as two distinct resonant icons operating in tandem.
  * **The Resculpt Discovery ([04_Night_of_Her_Own][ch-04]):** Unlocking the biological plasticity of Monad physiology to alter vocal timbre and physical posture.
* **Current Status:**
  * Nathan manages chronic metallic stiffness with specialized neuro-lubricants.
  * Veronica handles sensory calibration and micro-motor tuning.
* **Potential Future Threads:**
  * **The Total Resculpt:** Facing a checkpoint where Nathan’s face is flagged by CAS police; Veronica executes a full Monad physical resculpt to step into the driver's seat as the primary physical persona.
