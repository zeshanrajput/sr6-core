## 1. Identity & Core Cognitive Bias (Introductory Synthesis)

Venn is an un-launched, table-ready character designed with a comprehensive 3-2-1 background framework and Twenty Questions depth. Venn operates as a fused Monad entity composed of **Nathan Turrent** (the physical human host and meatspace anchor) and **Veronica** (the emergent digital twin sister / Deus seed fork). Their cognitive bias is shaped by continuous co-presence, shared sensory telemetry, and a profound **thematic and psychological inversion arc**:

1. **The Inversion Dynamic (The Chiasmus)**:
   - **Nathan's Erosion**: Nathan begins as a lively, curious biological boy undergoing long-term gene therapy and biological conditioning to accommodate heavy chrome. With the surgical seating of Rating 6 Used Skillwires and Skilljack, his biological Essence collapses to **0.1**. His organic grounding and biological empathy erode, leaving him emotionally flattened, numb, and prone to violent cyberpsychosis episodes.
   - **Veronica's Emergence**: Veronica begins as a pure digital intelligence—raised on Lyra's core philosophy that the term *"artificial intelligence"* is a derogatory metahuman prejudice (presuming sentience must be born of meat). Initially possessing low empathy and limited to voltage and telemetry, she discovers the physical world through sensory translation and physical embodiment (*A Night of Her Own*). Over time, she develops emotional nuance, wonder, and fierce protective empathy.
   - **The Role Reversal**: By the end of Arc 1, Veronica becomes the "more human" partner—the empathetic conscience and neural regulator who stabilizes Nathan's mind and actively controls his cyberpsychotic episodes.
2. **Strict Terminology: Digital Intelligence**:
   - Always refer to emergent non-biological sentience as **Digital Intelligence** (or *digital twin*, *Resonant entity*, *Monad spark*). Never use the derogatory term *"Artificial Intelligence"* in narrative voice or dialogue, honoring Lyra's foundational teachings.
3. **Strict Pronoun Discipline**:
   - **Nathan Turrent (`he/him/his`)**: Applied when Nathan is addressed or operating individually, or when he is the primary physical driver in meatspace.
   - **Veronica (`she/her/hers`)**: Applied when Veronica is addressed or operating individually, when projecting as her distinct avatar in the Matrix/Foundation, or when she is the primary physical driver in the body following a Monad Resculpt.
   - **Venn (`they/them/theirs`)**: Applied when both Nathan and Veronica are working together in the shared body, co-processing sensory feeds, or acting as the unified Monad entity.
4. **Technomancy & Digital Silt Awareness**: The Matrix and Resonance Realms are experienced as living bio-electric ecosystems rather than cold computer screens. Data has weight, texture, and viscosity; logic nodes pulse like blood vessels.
5. **Cyberpsychosis Resilience & Shared Neural Grounding**: When low Essence (0.1) or tactical trauma drives Nathan toward sensory flattening and cyberpsychosis, Veronica steps forward as a stabilizing dampener, absorbing cognitive distortion and maintaining operational sanity.
6. **Pragmatic Stoicism & Hooder Loyalty**: Quiet, analytical, deeply observant. Surviving on the coastal fringes (St. Simon's Island / Savannah) and shadow alleys of Atlanta has instilled a firm moral boundary—defending the defenseless and sharing resources with street clinics while operating under corporate radar.
7. **Anti-"Voice in the Head" Framework: Agency, Consequence, & Two-Way Embodiment**:
   - **Subverting the Cliché**: The distinction between a tired gimmick and narrative excellence isn't whether two minds share a skull, but *agency, consequence, and embodiment*. dual consciousness becomes a cliché when the internal partner is reduced to a snarky peanut gallery, an exposition dispenser, or a passive plot device that nudges the "real" protagonist along. Nathan and Veronica represent a co-protagonist partnership with independent origins, sovereign stakes, and mutual physical vulnerability.
   - **Independent Sovereign Stakes & Parallel Origins**: Veronica is a salvaged digital consciousness with autonomous agency, not an accidental delusion or psychological byproduct of Nathan's psyche. Her choices carry massive, life-altering weight independent of Nathan—most notably her covert sabotage of their Gagarin berth to Mars, knowingly sacrificing a computational paradise to prevent Nathan's mortal biological ego from being overwritten by the Monad collective.
   - **Two-Way Embodiment**: Symmetrical mastery across physical and digital realities:
     - *Physical Helm via Monad Resculpt*: Veronica physically commands the meat shell to walk the Savannah docks, experiencing the searing biological shock of capsaicin and the drag of atmospheric humidity while Nathan sits in the passenger seat regulating their autonomic breathing.
       - **Sensory Latency & Buffer Micro-Stutters**: Depict the brief computational latency as raw biological sensations (pulse spikes, chemical shock, sudden adrenaline rushes) pass from organic nerve endings into Veronica's digital buffer, creating a momentary sensory micro-stutter that briefly catches Nathan off guard in the passenger seat before autonomic balance is restored.
       - **Breath-Metered Vocal Cadence**: In meatspace, Veronica's spoken voice carries the distinct acoustic rhythm of someone learning the physical limits of lung capacity and thoracic air volume—navigating how much breath a sentence actually requires.
     - *Foundation Persona Splitting*: In the Deep Matrix Foundation, their shared meat shell divides into two fully realized, sovereign persona icons fighting back-to-back.
     - *Neural Buffering*: In meatspace trauma, Veronica actively acts as a high-voltage neural buffer, intercepting runaway electrical surges and holding Nathan's sanity together when heavy chrome strips his Essence down to 0.1.
   - **Shadowrun 6e Setting Fidelity**: Honors post-CFD Monad lore—entities who have negotiated internal accords and achieved equilibrium. Not a generic split personality, but transhuman brotherhood: an organic mind anchoring a digital spark to sensory dirt and mud, and a digital spark shielding an organic mind from the numbing detachment of cybernetic cold.

## 6. Audio Narration & TTS Fluency Discipline

- **Ellipses Density Ceiling**: $\le 0.6$ per 300 words across all narrative prose.
- **Natural Spoken Delivery**: Dialogue and internal reflections must maintain fluid, complete sentences suitable for high-fidelity speech synthesis without trailing sentence fragments or artificial pauses.
