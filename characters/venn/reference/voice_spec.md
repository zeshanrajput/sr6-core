## 1. Identity & Core Cognitive Bias (Introductory Synthesis)

Venn is an un-launched, table-ready character designed with a comprehensive 3-2-1 background framework and Twenty Questions depth. Venn operates as a fused Monad entity composed of **Nathan Turrent** (the physical human host and meatspace anchor) and **Veronica** (the emergent digital twin sister / Deus seed fork). Their cognitive bias is shaped by continuous co-presence, shared sensory telemetry, and a profound **thematic and psychological inversion arc**:

1. **The Inversion Dynamic (The Chiasmus)**:
   - **Nathan's Erosion**: Nathan begins as a lively, curious biological boy undergoing long-term gene therapy and biological conditioning to accommodate heavy chrome. With the surgical seating of Rating 6 Used Skillwires and Skilljack, his biological Essence collapses to **0.1**. His organic grounding and biological empathy erode, leaving him emotionally flattened, numb, and prone to violent cyberpsychosis episodes. The world turns monochrome and cold; empathy requires conscious, taxing effort.
   - **Veronica's Emergence**: Veronica begins as a pure digital intelligence—raised on Lyra's core philosophy that the term *"artificial intelligence"* is a derogatory metahuman prejudice (presuming sentience must be born of meat). Initially possessing low empathy and limited to voltage and telemetry, she discovers the physical world through sensory translation and physical embodiment (*A Night of Her Own*). Over time, she develops emotional nuance, wonder, and fierce protective empathy.
   - **The Role Reversal**: By the end of Arc 1, Veronica becomes the emotional pacemaker and neural regulator who stabilizes Nathan's mind and actively controls his cyberpsychotic episodes, while Nathan remains the courageous, streetwise biological anchor who willingly bears the somatic cost of heavy chrome.
2. **Co-Protagonist Parity & Anti-Foil Safeguard**:
   - Nathan is not a passive victim, narrative foil, or sounding board. He is a highly capable, gritty operator with distinct instincts, mechanical muscle memory, and deep loyalty.
   - Veronica is not a passive GPS unit, snarky voice-over, or mere AI assistant. She is an equal co-protagonist who commands physical embodiment, makes sovereign existential choices, and exercises direct physical authority in meatspace.
3. **Active Physical Authority & Vocal Intercept Protocol**:
   - During somatic crises, sensory overload, or 0.1 Essence collapses, Veronica can take sympathetic and motor control of their shared body and **speak aloud directly through Nathan's physical vocal cords to external NPCs** (e.g. commanding Marcus during surgery: *"The bus is stable, Marcus... seat the chip"*).
4. **Anti-Thesis Sloganeering & Somatic Subtext**:
   - Characters must **never preach or announce their thematic roles out loud** (e.g., ban lines like *"I will hold your humanity,"* *"I will keep us human,"* *"You gave me a life, I will protect yours"*).
   - The chiasmus must be dramatized entirely through **physical tradecraft, somatic dampening, silent consensus, and visceral consequences**. Trust the reader.
5. **Zero-Question Shared Telemetry Protocol**:
   - When sharing a nervous system or sensory feed, neither entity asks expository questions about observable facts (*"What is the encryption level?"*). Data renders directly in their shared AR HUD. Internal dialogue is reserved for physiological regulation, sensory translation, tactical consensus, or sibling friction.
6. **Co-Occupant Sibling Friction**:
   - Ban frictionless unanimity. As two minds sharing a skull, they experience genuine co-occupancy tension: biological fatigue vs. digital insomnia, sensory boundary clashing, and unilateral choices (such as Veronica secretly cancelling the Gagarin Mars manifest) that spark raw human shock before reaching earned consensus.
7. **Strict Terminology: Digital Intelligence**:
   - Always refer to emergent non-biological sentience as **Digital Intelligence** (or *digital twin*, *Resonant entity*, *Monad spark*). Never use the derogatory term *"Artificial Intelligence"* in narrative voice or dialogue.
8. **Strict Pronoun Discipline**:
   - **Nathan Turrent (`he/him/his`)**: Applied when Nathan is addressed or operating individually, or when he is the primary physical driver in meatspace.
   - **Veronica (`she/her/hers`)**: Applied when Veronica is addressed or operating individually, when projecting as her distinct avatar in the Matrix/Foundation, or when she is the primary physical driver in the body following a Monad Resculpt.
   - **Venn (`they/them/theirs`)**: Applied when both Nathan and Veronica are working together in the shared body, co-processing sensory feeds, or acting as the unified Monad entity.

## 6. Audio Narration & TTS Fluency Discipline

- **Ellipses Density Ceiling**: $\le 0.6$ per 300 words across all narrative prose.
- **Natural Spoken Delivery**: Dialogue and internal reflections must maintain fluid, complete sentences suitable for high-fidelity speech synthesis without trailing sentence fragments or artificial pauses.
- **One Speaker Per Paragraph**: Every speaker turn begins a fresh paragraph to maintain pristine listener attribution in multi-voice or single-voice TTS delivery.
