## 1. Overview & The Canon Ceiling Protocol

Across organized play (Shadowrun Missions Seattle, New Orleans, and convention campaigns), our characters (**Reiko / `r31-K0`**, **Velvet / Tanaka Ryo**, and **Venn**) operate alongside a recurring cast of fellow Player Characters (PCs).

### The Tier A Canon Ceiling Mandate
* **Read-Only Relational Orbit:** Fellow PCs are sovereign to their real-world players. They must never be killed, mutilated, fundamentally rewritten, or forced into out-of-character actions in inter-session fiction without explicit player consent.
* **Tabletop Echoes:** Inter-session fiction and downtime interludes celebrate their tabletop camaraderie, tactical quirks, banter, and memorable table moments (e.g., weapon choices, missed rolls, running jokes, and downtime favors) without violating their core agency.
* **Preserving Context:** This registry acts as the single source of truth for fellow PC backgrounds, player handles, and campaign lore so recurring narrative threads (like Rei's U-Dub sociology classes or Mithral's Strider van) remain consistent across all character anthologies.

## 3. Featured Runner Dossier: Rei (Suzuki Ken)

* **Full Profile:** See [`characters/reiko/reference/rei_suzuki.md`](file:///c:/GitHub/sr6-core/characters/reiko/reference/rei_suzuki.md)
* **Core Dynamic:** 
  * Reiko's chosen older brother (*Oniisama*), sharing Renraku corporate heritage, family grief, and birthday celebrations.
  * Son of **Takahashi-sama**, the Renraku researcher who committed *seppuku* after his project failed (the very project that culminated in Reiko's emergence as `r31-K0`).
  * Tabletop Blade Adept who constantly relies on firearms (Ares Predator, Ares Slivergun) due to engagement range, generating the endless table ribbing that he is a "Gun Adept in denial."
  * U-Dub Sociology student auditing Dr. Dana Kessler's lectures on metahuman stratification in exchange for Ork Underground field research.
  * In Aisling & Étaín’s interlude fanfic (*My Glade Adept Onii-sama is too Kawaii!*), he wields the katana ***Stolen Honor*** equipped with modular handles (academic stylus/laser pointer, 3,000 RPM matcha whisk, thespian microphone) and unleashes storms of silver flechette confetti.

## 4. Guidelines for Inter-Session Prose Involving Fellow PCs

When incorporating fellow PCs into narrative chapters, downtime journals, or interludes:
1. **Highlight Their Established Expertise:** Show Mithral's steady wheel-work and situational cynicism, Fox's showmanship and fixer networks, or Bast's surgical tactical movement.
2. **Weave Tabletop Artifacts:** Ground scenes in real gear, vehicles, and running jokes logged in the mission transcripts and character logs (e.g. Mithral's Strider, Rei's Ares Slivergun, the "Ray" action figures, the *Lederhosen of Evasion*).
3. **Respect Mechanical Fidelity:** Align their spells, drones, and combat styles with their known SRM character sheets rather than inventing contradictory powers.
4. **Keep Personal Choices In-Player:** Let relationship drama, permanent cybernetic alterations, or major moral shifts remain in the hands of the PC's actual player.
