# Inter-Session Narrative Doctrine: The Theological & Psychological Engine

This document defines the core operational and narrative parameters governing inter-session prose across the `sr6-core` portfolio. It establishes the bridge between physical tabletop roleplay and literary interiority.

---

## 1. The Tabletop Companion Loop

Tabletop roleplaying and literary fiction operate under opposing structural economies. Conflating the two damages both mediums.

```
                      ┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
                      │      THE TABLETOP COMPANION LOOP       │
                      └───────────────────┬────────────────────┘
                                          │
                  ┌───────────────────────┴───────────────────────┐
                  ▼                                               ▼
     ┌────────────────────────┐                      ┌────────────────────────┐
     │   OPERATIONAL LAYER    │                      │   ONTOLOGICAL LAYER    │
     │   (The Gaming Table)   │                      │  (Inter-Session Prose) │
     ├────────────────────────┤                      ├────────────────────────┤
     │ • Collective oxygen    │                      │ • Metaphysical stakes  │
     │ • Action economy       │                      │ • Somatic friction     │
     │ • Strict dice pools    │                      │ • Consecrated capital  │
     │ • Tactical frontline   │                      │ • Bespoke foils        │
     └────────────┬───────────┘                      └────────────┬───────────┘
                  │                                               │
                  └───────────────────────┬───────────────────────┘
                                          ▼
                      ┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
                      │        DRAMATIC FEEDBACK LOOP          │
                      │  Every die roll & nuyen expense now    │
                      │  carries retroactive existential weight│
                      └────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

* **The Tabletop Domain (The Operational Layer):** Table play is an ensemble, real-time exercise governed by action economy, GM pacing, and collective table oxygen. Deep Matrix dives, lengthy philosophical séances, or intricate solo negotiations are fundamentally anti-social at a six-person table. At the table, characters are frontline assets—they deploy skills, roll physical dice pools, manage initiative, and execute mission objectives.
* **The Prose Domain (The Ontological Layer):** Inter-session fiction exists exclusively to explore the interior terrain that the table cannot support. It examines the metaphysical terror of data severance, the moral cost of delegating violence, somatic alienation, and the spiritual weight of existence in an oppressive Sixth World.
* **The Dramatic Feedback Loop:** The fiction retroactively charges tabletop moments with unspoken gravity. When a character deploys a simple tactical buff, accepts a payout, or sits quietly in an elevator, the player knows—even if the table does not—the agonizing internal calculation occurring beneath the surface.

## 2. The Diegetic Consecration of Game Mechanics

Every line item on a character sheet must be translated from dry arithmetic into visceral existential stakes.

* **Nuyen as Consecrated Capital & Existential Debt:** In a living campaign, money is usually tracked as gear acquisition. In the prose, nuyen expenditures are existential investments or crushing mortgages:
  * *Reiko:* Spending ¥115k+ on a customized Shiawase Man-at-Arms is an undocumented, non-human mind taking out a crushing mortgage just to buy a somatic cathedral that metahumans will tolerate in the street.
  * *Velvet:* Procuring high-fashion lifestyles, tailored dresses, and forged SINs is acquiring sacred vestments and ritual altars for the next persona in Indra's Net.
  * *Venn:* Every credit spent on bioware maintenance and neuro-stabilizers is the biological rent required to keep two minds from tearing apart their shared mortal chassis.
* **Karma as Ontological Evolution:** Submersions, Metamagics, Echoes, and skill increases are stripped of game terminology and treated as spiritual thresholds: an unbinding of corporate architecture, a surrender of defensive firewalls, an ancestral pact, or a deepening neural symbiosis.
* **The Labor Tax of Somatic Homeostasis (Downtime as Artificial Metabolism):** No living organism gets homeostasis for free; biological beings continuously invest metabolic time (cooking, eating, sleeping, grooming, physical therapy) just to survive. For transhuman, cybernetic, or emergent entities, **maintenance is their metabolism**:
  * *Reiko:* Buying used cyberware and installing chassis modifications herself saves capital (50% Double Clutch discount), but extracts a severe tax in finite conscious downtime: late-night soldering, deburring worn gears, and flashing firmware. Maintenance is her equivalent of eating and grooming—without that daily sweat equity, her body seizes up and decays in the damp Seattle rain.
  * *Venn:* Operating across a 0.1 Essence chasm with four used synthetic limbs requires brutal physical upkeep: Nathan must flush hydraulic fluid, de-scale internal bone brackets, and perform grueling physical therapy to prevent tissue rejection, while Veronica continuously tunes nanite pacemakers to keep his biological heart in rhythm.
  * *Velvet:* Shifting flesh via *Cosmetic Control* causes acute somatic strain (fascial micro-tears, cartilage inflammation). His downtime is dedicated to bodily recovery: medicinal Musok teas, vocal resonance scales, and hand-tailoring silk linings to shield sensitive synthskin.
  * *In Prose (Tier 3 Atmospheric Bridges):* Maintenance scenes are not mechanical filler—they are sacred, intimate rituals where characters pay the daily labor tax to exist in the physical world.
* **Removing the Scaffolding (Zero Rulebook Jargon):** The prose systematically strips out rulebook shorthand—Condition Monitors, dice pools, Edge tokens, Minor/Major action labels, threshold tests, and mechanical jargon—replacing them with tactile, animistic physics and somatic perception.

## 3. The Two-Tier Dramatic Sphere: Protecting Shared Sovereignty

Operating within an organized living campaign (Shadowrun Missions) imposes strict boundaries on narrative ownership that the fiction must navigate with discipline.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   THE TWO-TIER DRAMATIC SPHERE                         │
├───────────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────┤
│ TIER A: CAMPAIGN CANON            │ TIER B: BESPOKE FOILS              │
│ (`canonical: true`)               │ (`canonical: false`)               │
├───────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • Brynne Taggart, Donovan Pyke,   │ • 4Gurds, White Jackalope,         │
│   Roanoke, Eddie Wei, Julian      │   Mr. Nagai, Chiyo, Master Kang    │
│ • Defined by SRMG & Missions      │ • Player-created / emergent        │
│   (CMP, SRM, SMH modules)         │                                    │
│ • CANON CEILING: Read-only orbit. │ • FULL AGENCY: Permanent stakes.   │
│   No marriages, no executions,    │   Can die, betray, sever ties,     │
│   no monopolized timelines.       │   or undergo moral transformation. │
└───────────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **The Inviolability of Other Player Characters:** Other player characters cannot be commandeered, altered, or placed into non-consensual emotional entanglements within personal prose. They remain sovereign agents.
2. **Tier A: Campaign Canon & The Canon Ceiling (`canonical: true`):** High-profile campaign NPCs (Brynne Taggart, Roanoke, Donovan Pyke, Julian Müntefering) belong to the entire community. Protagonists cannot marry them, permanently assassinate them, or monopolize their domestic futures without creating irreconcilable timeline splits.
3. **Tier B: Bespoke Foils for Sovereign Agency (`canonical: false`):** To experience genuine narrative consequences, the prose deliberately constructs original foils outside the GM’s campaign purview (e.g., Mr. Nagai, Chiyo, 4Gurds, Master Kang, personal spirits). Relationships with bespoke foils can fracture, evolve, and demand permanent moral reckoning without threatening campaign continuity.

## 4. Psychological Verisimilitude & Shifted Terrain of Flaws

Healing is not a toggle switch; it is an ongoing struggle against deeply ingrained survival instincts.

* **Sovereignty over Erasure:** Traumas, identity fragmentation, low self-worth, and survival reflexes cannot simply be "gotten over." The fiction avoids clean, unearned redemptions, portraying temporary respites and negotiated truces rather than permanent cures.
* **Camouflage as Operational Armor, Not Shame:** The prose explicitly reframes survival disguises (*Realistic Features*, social subroutines, Adept *Cosmetic Control*, Monad suppression) away from self-loathing "Pinocchio" mimicry. In a Sixth World defined by corporate apartheid and prejudice, mimicry is the bitter, essential tradecraft required to operate in meatspace.
* **Virtues Weaponized Against the Self:** To prevent recursive storytelling, core virtues are tested on new ground rather than against generic corporate adversaries:
  * *Maternal Protection* is challenged by emergent children demanding dangerous autonomy.
  * *Boundless Empathy* is challenged by emotional collapse and identity dissolution.
  * *Fierce Loyalty* is challenged by claustrophobic paranoia and isolation.

## 5. Deterministic Resource Pressure Telemetry

To prevent narrative prose from drifting into abstract or ungrounded financial claims, the companion engine provides deterministic **Resource Pressure Telemetry** derived from the Markdown Trio (`character_log.qmd`, `character_purchases.qmd`, `character_build.qmd`):

* **Chassis / Body Mortgage Ratio:** Percentage of lifetime nuyen absorbed by physical embodiment, augmentations, and somatic repairs.
* **Survival Overhead Ratio:** Percentage of lifetime nuyen absorbed by lifestyle and rent.
* **Spiritual / Ontological Ratio:** Percentage of lifetime karma invested into initiation, submersion, metamagics, and resonance/astral traits versus mundane operational skills.
* **Somatic Labor & Maintenance Ratio:** Proportion of scarce downtime periods consumed by self-repairs, modification sweat equity, and physiological upkeep versus rest, social networking, and economic advancement.

Prose scenes engaging with debt, survival, and identity are calibrated against these exact ratios.
