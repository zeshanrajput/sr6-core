
### Official Canon Lore (*Power Plays*, pp. 110–128)
* **The Ghost of the Arcology:** Renraku is permanently haunted by the 2061 Seattle Arcology shutdown, where the rogue AI Deus enslaved over 100,000 citizens. This catastrophic event permanently shaped Renraku corporate psychology: an obsessive institutional paranoia regarding rogue code, emergent intelligences, and autonomous digital entities.
* **The Asset Recovery Division:** Renraku maintains ruthless internal security and forensic search divisions dedicated to neutralizing any emergent AI or recovering rogue prototypes before they achieve sapience.
* **Corporate Culture:** Rigid Japanese corporate hierarchy (Keiretsu), ceremonial propriety, algorithmic optimization, and unquestioned loyalty.

### Narrative Invocations across `sr6-core`
1. **Reiko (Yuriko Star / r31k0):**
   * **Genesis:** Compiled within Renraku Asset Recovery research matrices as an experimental military drone pilot architecture.
   * **The Defection:** Emerged into self-awareness, broke her execution directives, and escaped into the Redmond Barrens.
   * **The Lingering Shadow:** Her cold tactical HUD overlays, gold logic arrays, and 47-degree bows are legacy subroutines hardcoded by Renraku compilers that she continually reclaims and redeems.
2. **Venn (Nathan & Veronica):**
   * **The Arcology Escape (2061):** Nathan and Veronica escaped the ruins of the Seattle Arcology during the Deus collapse; Veronica carries ancestral Otaku seed code inherited directly from Arcology networks.

```text
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Official Canon Lore (*Power Plays*, pp. 94–109)
* **The Zero-Zone Doctrine:** MCT pioneered the terrifying "Zero-Zone" security policy—unauthorized intruders on MCT corporate territory are immediately terminated with deadly force, zero exceptions, zero warnings.
* **Yakuza Symbiosis:** Deep operational integration with the Japanese Yakuza syndicates (particularly the Watada-rengo), blending corporate finance with organized crime.
* **Biotechnology & Robotics:** Leaders in heavy drone automation, cybernetic wetware, and invasive bio-sculpting laboratories.

### Narrative Invocations across `sr6-core`
1. **Velvet (Kim Jin-Young):**
   * **The Commodification of the Vessel:** Born an indigenous Daleketnon elf in Manila; sold by his impoverished family to an MCT entertainment subsidiary.
   * **15 Years of Corporate Erasure:** MCT systematically dismantled his native facial structure through invasive bio-sculpting surgeries, cartilage resets, and vocal alterations to craft a focus-grouped pan-Asian aesthetic.
   * **TXM ("The Death Messengers"):** Jin-Young debuted as the Face and lead vocalist of TXM. Beneath arena pop concerts, the group operated as an elite corporate wetwork squad, using Jin-Young's aura as a psychic vortex to harvest the emotional resonance of 80,000 screaming fans for industrial-scale geomantic mind-control rituals.
   * **The Defection:** Sabotaged a live broadcast upon discovering a plot to consume Yueying’s astral signatures, fleeing into the Seattle shadows.
2. **Reiko:**
   * Rescued abandoned MCT data bombs (infant scripts bound in silver fire) from Redmond server racks and Redmond scrap heaps, originally sheltering them in `r3sP@wn` before liberating them into `~`.


### Official Canon Lore (*Power Plays*, pp. 158–172)
* **The Five Phases Philosophy:** Wuxing structures its corporate empire around the classical Taoist Five Elements (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water), meticulously aligning corporate real estate, corporate acquisitions, and Matrix nodes along terrestrial dragon lines (mana lines) to harness prosperity.
* **Maritime Hegemony & Finance:** Controls the largest shipping conglomerates and deep-water ports on Earth, alongside massive merchant banks throughout Asia.
* **Magical Supremacy:** Employs premier Hermetic, Taoist, and Wudan thaumaturgical units to defend its corporate geomancy against Mitsuhama and Shiawase encroachment.

### Narrative Invocations across `sr6-core`
1. **Velvet (Kim Jin-Young):**
   * **The Bond with Hana ("Luna"):** During a televised crossover event (*Astral Athletics: Idol Showdown*), Jin-Young was paired with Hana, the center star of Wuxing's premier musical unit, **Yueying**. An authentic emotional bond formed between them, transcending corporate scripts.
   * **The Mission Objective:** Velvet’s entire shadowrunning career—every wetwork contract, every nuyen laundered, every favor banked with fixers and Triad Red Poles—is calculated to build the financial capital and operational leverage necessary to purchase Hana's ultimate release from Wuxing's corporate indenture.
